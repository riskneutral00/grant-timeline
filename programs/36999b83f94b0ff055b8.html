Note markdown# Shaanxi San Qin International-Student Scholarship

Non-Chinese registered degree students who have already completed one academic year.

**Verified foreign route:** Applicants should be a non-Chinese citizen — https://is.nwsuaf.edu.cn/home/admissions/masterprogram/330575.htm

**Cycle:** Undated legacy guidance: before September 25 annually; exact current September notice not verified.

**Eligibility:** Valid foreign passport; ≥one academic year at NWAFU; current-year grades above B; HSK proof; no legal/school violations; no existing scholarship such as CSC. Age/degree-level table unavailable.

**Application:** San Qin form, passport/current visa, diploma, current university transcript, two original professor/associate-professor references for graduate applicants.

**Deliverable:** Degree study/research and admission requirements; no startup MVP or pitch-video requirement verified.

**Attendance:** Existing local degree study in Yangling, Shaanxi.

**Award:** Provincial educational scholarship; amount and age table embedded in inaccessible legacy images, so monetary value unknown.

**Costs and conditions:** Study award, not founder capital. Amount, stacking exceptions, application fee, disbursement, tax and annual contract unknown.

**People:** 1 minimum / 1 maximum. Individual student applicant, not startup-team eligibility.
