Note markdown# Creative Tech Shibuya

Free creative technology residency and pilot matching in Shibuya; participants normally pay travel and accommodation.

## Eligibility and participation

Launched product or service, live demonstration capacity, Japanese pilot fit and business English. Overseas startups are the primary population, but headquarters outside Japan is not mandatory.

All five in-person days in Shibuya, February 8–12, 2027, expected; dates tentative. Bring demonstration equipment.

## Support and costs

2027: five-day mentoring, investor and corporate matching, exhibits and possible pilot support. About three pilots are conditional selections; JETRO-designated travel support is conditional, not universal cash.

Participation free. Own travel, accommodation and other expenses unless separately approved for JETRO invitation support. No automatic airfare entitlement.

Public participation form linked; exact live field labels, required uploads and limits were not read.
