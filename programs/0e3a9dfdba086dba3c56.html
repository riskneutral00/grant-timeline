Note markdown# National University AI Digital Trade Innovation Application Competition

Student AI trade proposals; registration closed.

## 1. Problem and deliverable

Conditional two-student software/AI validation opportunity, modest award relative to research effort.

## 2. Eligibility and people

Student limit: 2–5 students; advisers are additional (maximum 2). One or two optional teachers; they are additional to the student team. Record team_max is null because the student ceiling is not a verified whole-team ceiling.

Taiwan-college students, bachelor/upper five-year college, non-executive master or doctorate; nationality unrestricted. Age/gender/company conditions not specified. 2–5 students; optional 1–2 teachers.

## 3. Application fields and files

Track/team/school/member/advisor names; <=10-page Word proposal: problem, AI tools/workflow/results, sources, roles, schedule and benefits.

## 4. Work and originality requirements

Original feasible AI proposal; application track uses company platform data, planning track market/strategy. Cite tools/prompts and verify AI output; no mandatory video/MVP identified.

## 5. Attendance

Taipei November 29 final: six-minute pitch plus two-minute Q&A; slides November 20 17:00. Online training June–October.

## 6. Awards and fees

Team prizes collected by leader under tax rules; registration free. Fee status: none.

## 7. Expenses and IP

Travel and payment date unspecified. Team retains copyright; noncommercial display/publicity licence. No equity requirement stated.

## 8. Dates and chronology

2026-09-30; timezone not verified. Registered teams: proposal October 19 17:00; slides November 20 17:00. Neither is a new-entry deadline. 2026 edition verified; launch date unknown.

## 9. Evidence gaps

Organizer homepage/short registration link unavailable. School-authored notice corroborates eligibility/free entry; competition PDF identified organizer. Next edition unknown.
