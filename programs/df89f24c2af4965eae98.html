Note markdown# Sustainability Kaohsiung Youth Startup Challenge 2026 (company track)

Checked 12 October 2026.

## Edition and scope
2026 company-track competition closed. Extended online deadline supersedes original PDF.

Decision: retain_for_diligence

- **personnel minimum:** None
- **company count:** 1
- **separate track:** Individual-team track minimum two people, one-third age18–35; do not transfer that minimum to company track. Three member rows in company form are not an explicit minimum.

## Eligibility
- **company:** Company founded on/after2017-01-01, capital <=NT$10m, responsible person18–45. Proposal must have Kaohsiung production, research, sales/service or other local connection; explicit Kaohsiung incorporation requirement not found.
- **individual:** Separate individual-team route exists; not the recorded company track.
- **sole proprietor:** Company-track wording is company; business/sole proprietor equivalence not established.
- **restrictions:** Prior2025 finalists ineligible; IP and originality declarations required.

## Application and product requirements
- **deck:** Chinese PDF,16:9, <=20slides excluding cover; business model, innovation, sustainability and team. Suggested market, competition,2024–25 financials,2026–28 projections, risks and benefits.
- **video:** No initial mandatory video found.
- **MVP traction:** Business information and later live demonstration; no universal minimum revenue found.

## Attendance and location
- **application:** Paper screening, online submission.
- **preliminary:** Early May physical pitch7min+5min questions.
- **programme:** Finalists must complete May–June training.
- **final:** Physical demo/pitch in June; subsequent official account records June15. Ceremony attendance conditions apply.
- **post award:** Company winners must participate in investment matching AND overseas international training itinerary; travel coverage not specified. Two-year tracking.

## Award and conditions
- **type:** Cash competition prize
- **currency:** TWD
- **prizes:** [1000000, 500000, 300000]
- **tax:** Gross prizes; domestic10% withholding for qualifying amount overNT$20k, foreign20% stated in rules.
- **claim:** Claim paperwork within14days of results.
- **equity:** No equity condition stated; later investor matching does not itself mean equity awarded.

## Spend, matching, reporting and recovery
- **founder salary:** Not established in the public material reviewed; do not assume permitted or prohibited.
- **office rent:** Not established in the public material reviewed; do not assume permitted or prohibited.
- **travel flights:** Mandatory overseas activity may create travel cost; who pays is unresolved.
- **computers equipment:** Not established in the public material reviewed; do not assume permitted or prohibited.
- **subscriptions API:** Not established in the public material reviewed; do not assume permitted or prohibited.
- **spend:** Public rules do not enumerate founder salary, office rent, flights, computers or subscriptions as permitted/restricted uses. Do not label unrestricted without award confirmation.
- **matching:** No applicant match stated.
- **clawbacks IP:** Misrepresentation, infringement and rule breach can revoke award. Perpetual free worldwide licence to use/reproduce/adapt proposal for publicity.

## Deadlines
- **application close:** 2026-04-12 12:00 extended; original PDF2026-04-08 23:59 is superseded.
- **timezone:** Taiwan local time is planning assumption; no explicit timezone string found.
- **programme:** May–June2026; exact later obligations notified by organiser.
