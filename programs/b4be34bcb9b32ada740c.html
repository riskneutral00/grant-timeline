Note markdown# TiBOOST Innovation Growth Program 2026

Checked 12 October 2026. Public-source diligence; no application or account was submitted.

## Edition and scope

2026 competition verified; applications closed October 5, finalists pending October 23.

Team minimum: No member-count limit; all participating members must be 18 by deadline. No minimum stated.

Scope: include

## Eligibility

- entity: Registered domestic/international startup company; no PRC incorporation, >30% direct/indirect PRC ownership or PRC control. Not listed on Taiwan Innovation Board.

- traction: At least one external fundraising round OR initial market validation such as revenue/users. AI-related technology in specified AI ten-construction themes.

- individual_soleprop: Company法人 registration required; unincorporated individuals not eligible; sole-proprietor acceptance not established.

## Product and application requirements

- submission: Online questionnaire plus registration, sealed non-PRC declaration, ≤25-page business plan PDF (excluding appendices), ≤20-page deck.

- video: No video requirement on public page. Supporting photos/media/certificates/awards optional.

- review: Market 25%, solution 20%, business model 20%, team 15%, capital-market readiness 20%.

## Attendance and location

- submission: Online; closed questionnaire no longer exposed.

- final: Mandatory in-person pitch and Q&A November 10, no online participation per FAQ. Venue not verified.

- travel: Foreign-incorporated finalists may claim airfare/lodging up to USD1,500 with proof, round-trip dates must include November 10. Domestic companies: return high-speed rail plus accommodation up to USD200.

## Award and commercial terms

- cash: First TWD2,500,000; second TWD1,500,000; third TWD1,000,000.

- investment: VC/capital-market introductions are opportunities, no guaranteed investment or equity term published.

- other: Travel reimbursement is separate from prize.

## Permissible spending and obligations

- founder_salary: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- rent: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- computers: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- API_subscriptions: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- travel: Finalist travel claims as above; documentation required.

- matching_reporting_IP: Detailed prize tax, spending restrictions, IP, clawback and matching terms not recoverable from inaccessible Drive folder; no unrestricted-cash claim.

## Dates and deadlines

- deadline: Extended to October 5 2026, 14:00 UTC+8 (06:00 UTC); supersedes September 29/noon wording.

- selection: October 23 finalists; November 10 physical finals/awards.
