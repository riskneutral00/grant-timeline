Note markdown# Civic Action Hub 2026

Checked 12 October 2026. Public-source diligence; no application or account was submitted.

## Edition and scope

2026 guidelines, 16 pages including public forms, inspected. Applications closed; delivery continues into March 2027.

Team minimum: 1 applicant permitted. Final team combines one assigned digital adviser and at least one creative participant.

Scope: include

## Eligibility

- individual: Anyone with lawful Taiwan residence evidence, regardless of nationality/age/status; minors under 18 need written guardian consent. One person may join only one team.

- entity: Individuals/team applications; no incorporation needed. Company status does not replace personal eligibility.

- selection: 4 teams and 4 reserves around local disaster-governance topics in Nantou, Keelung, Miaoli and New Taipei.

## Product and application requirements

- proposal: Chinese editable Word/PPT/ODF with contents and page numbers. Problem analysis, solution/platform/location, roles, resources, delivery and impact. No application video or pre-existing MVP requirement found.

- outputs: Working/test platform, repeated user tests, security fixes, public code and final report/slides. Standard for Public Code compliance and licences required.

## Attendance and location

- pitch: August 25 physical Demo Pitch and government collaboration ceremony.

- programme: September meetings and government collaboration; milestone reviews and presentation/media participation. No blanket remote exemption.

- publication: Code to MODA GitHub by February 15 2027 and final report/slides March 1.

## Award and commercial terms

- type: TWD300,000 equivalent gift vouchers/rewards per selected team, NOT cash.

- instalments: 30%/30%/40% after three accepted checkpoints. Recipient bears taxes; nonresident withholding 20%.

- other_funding: No other funding provided; teams supply their own necessary software/hardware and resources.

## Permissible spending and obligations

- founder_salary: Not a payroll reimbursement programme; vouchers cannot be represented as unrestricted cash salary.

- rent: No additional reimbursement.

- travel: No additional travel funding established.

- computers: Teams provide required hardware themselves; no separate equipment budget.

- API_subscriptions: Teams provide software/services themselves; no API reimbursement promise.

- IP: Open-source/public-code delivery and licence compliance, authors moral rights preserved; confidentiality/personal-data duties.

- reporting_clawback: No reward if agreed deliverables or required promotion not completed. Three checkpoints govern vouchers. Separate cooperation contract remains uninspected; confidentiality annex contains continuing duties and damages liability.

- confidentiality: Annex 3 continues secrecy after departure/end; return all owners data, no unauthorised destruction/retention. Do not exploit project confidential information for own/others business. Personal data only on agreed encrypted systems; no personal computer or other cloud without consent. Breach may cause joint damages liability.

## Dates and deadlines

- application: 2026-08-19 12:00 noon. Local Taiwan time inferred; separate timezone label not verified.

- milestones: Mid-September design; end-September contract/check 1; mid-October first code/test platform; end-November testing with ≥4 users and medium-or-higher security fixes; end-December final/check 2; mid-January security fixes; 2027-02-15 code; 2027-03-01 report/slides/check 3.
