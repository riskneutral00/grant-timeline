Note markdown# Digital Startup Application Reward 2026

Checked 12 October 2026. Public-source diligence; no application or account was submitted.

## Edition and scope

June 2026 ADI 48-page manual and annexes inspected via official university mirror. Supplied plan_detail_1 page is stale 2023. Programme execution reaches June 30 2027, but 2026 intake closed.

Team minimum: No numeric founder/employee minimum.

Scope: include

## Eligibility

- entity: Taiwan sole proprietorship, partnership, limited partnership or company founded on/after 2018-01-01, digital industry J582/J62/J63; positive equity, no branches or PRC investment.

- product: Proprietary digital product/service, not only reseller; market validation/PoB/PMF and credible international expansion.

- financials: Eligibility expressly requires at least 3 years operating record (2023–2025). A generic net-worth footnote permits new-company financial substitutes, but does NOT clearly waive the 3-year operating requirement; younger companies need clarification and must not assume eligibility.

- individual: Unregistered individual cannot meet enterprise/certificate requirements.

## Product and application requirements

- international_goal: At least one measurable overseas goal: physical exhibition, accelerator, fundraising, business development or overseas office; target country/market, actions/resources and acceptance indicators.

- application: Online business certificate, expansion proposal PDF ≤20MB, slides PDF ≤20MB, company/financial/compliance annexes.

- security: Software/AI security and prohibited-origin model rules apply; actual outputs and documented achievement assessed.

## Attendance and location

- selection: Company responsible person or delegated full-time employee must attend and present; absence forfeits eligibility. Up to 5 attendees, limited to company head/full-time staff. Remote option unspecified.

- execution: Participate in requested promotional/award activities, implementation checks and final review. At least chosen physical overseas activity where included in approved target.

- closeout: Results report within 1 week after completion; on-site execution and expense verification within 1 month under manual workflow.

## Award and commercial terms

- type: Up to TWD1m each for approximately 15 enterprises, tied to verified international expansion outcomes and actual eligible costs. Not unrestricted cash prize.

- payment: After outcome and voucher verification; reduced for underachievement/ineligible expense. Taxes including applicable stamp/income tax payable.

- match: No fixed matching ratio found in this reward manual; do not import 50% subsidy rule from separate R&D grant.

## Permissible spending and obligations

- founder_salary: No payroll expense head in this reward; cannot claim ordinary founder salary.

- rent: Eligible event venue/equipment rental and event setup; ordinary home/office rent not established.

- travel: Approved overseas airfare and hotel for full-time project personnel, trip report and ticket/boarding/payment evidence; unrelated itinerary removed. Employee dorms/private house rental or imputed/depreciated owned housing excluded.

- computers: Capital expenditure excluded; full computer purchase not eligible.

- API_subscriptions: General API/SaaS subscriptions not established. Only approved items fitting marketing or commissioned-service categories may qualify.

- categories: Only innovation-service promotion, commissioned professional services and foreign travel. Overseas legal/tax/translation and marketing/event/advertising costs subject to approved purpose, duration allocation and invoices/payment proof.

- reporting_clawback: At least 3 years post-completion outcome cooperation. Goal changes generally requested 30 days before change; results and originals audited. False declarations/expenses, duplicate funding and breaches can revoke award/recover funds; full unrestricted IP/use rights not promised.

## Dates and deadlines

- application: 2026-07-14 by 16:59:59; system closes 17:00. Taiwan local UTC+8 is interpretation, not explicit timezone label.

- execution: From announcement through 2027-06-30. This is not a 2027 application call.
