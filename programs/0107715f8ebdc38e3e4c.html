Note markdown# OIST Sustainable Energy Testbed Validation Program

Current Okinawa energy-technology validation call, including energy software and data; project support is contractual and conditional.

## Eligibility and participation

Japan or overseas incorporated startup/SME; startups generally unlisted and no more than ten years old, with possible exceptions. Technology must be test-ready with a substantive energy connection; pure concepts/basic research excluded. No Japanese subsidiary required.

OIST/Okinawa testbed programme; validation may be on or off OIST campus. Site, partner and practical attendance are project-specific; no universal relocation duration verified. Complete activities, report and final acceptance by March 2027.

## Support and costs

FY2026 approved project support up to JPY 2 million including taxes/charges; possible additional JPY 1 million only for substantive OIST research/core-facility collaboration. Validation coordination and conditional resource access, not guaranteed facilities, payment or investment.

Contract fixes eligible costs/payment, generally after deliverables; payments may be adjusted for incomplete/reduced scope. No double funding same cost; disclose cofunding. Applicant bears approved-budget excess and secures rights/site approvals. Applicant-only IP generally retained, joint IP by agreement; application NDA normally unavailable.

## Application and dates

Guidelines require online Japanese/English responses and budget with major items, amounts, purposes, requested support and cofunding. One application per applicant; optional attachments do not replace required answers. Exact Microsoft Forms fields/upload caps not inspected.

Opens October 9, 2026, 10:00 JST; closes October 28, 2026, 17:00 JST. Project completion by end of March 2027 is not a new intake cutoff.

Unknown participant minimum does not certify solo eligibility. Cohort/company/workforce counts are not participant team minima. This source audit does not establish the owner's eligibility.
