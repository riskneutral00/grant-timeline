Note markdown# NVIDIA Inception

Checked: 2026-10-12T05:53:00Z

Edition: Current public programme checked 12 October 2026; not a promise of future availability.

Scope: Provisionally include; team-size uncertainty is not exclusion.

Team minimum: At least one developer; application requires unique contact information for one developer and one business executive. Whether one person can fulfill both roles is unresolved; no minimum 3.

## Eligibility and legal form
- **applicant:** Officially incorporated, <10 years, active website, ≥1 developer. No revenue requirement.
- **excluded:** Consulting/outsourced developers, cryptocurrency-associated companies, cloud providers, resellers/distributors, public companies.
- **individual:** Unincorporated applicant ineligible; sole proprietor not proven equivalent.

## Application and product requirements
- **deck:** Company pitch deck required. No GPU usage required yet. Video/traction not established.

## Attendance and location
- **format:** Online membership; no fixed cohort/residency established.

## Award and cost
- **type:** Free non-equity membership; training, technical resources, selected hardware/software discounts and introductions.
- **amount:** No universal cash grant or guaranteed credit total. GPU discount is supplier-price rebate on selected enterprise products; availability not guaranteed.

## Spending, reporting and legal conditions
- **salary:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.
- **rent:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.
- **travel:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.
- **computers:** Selected enterprise GPU preferred pricing only; not a blanket computer purchase grant.
- **subscriptions:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.
- **API:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.
- **reimbursements:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.
- **matching:** No application or membership fee/equity requirement.
- **reporting:** Keep company/product portal information updated to remain eligible.
- **IP:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.
- **clawbacks:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.

## Dates
- **application:** Rolling; no cohort cutoff established.

## Public application fields
- Business email: Required; named company-domain email, no generic domains or aliases. [Evidence](https://programs.nvidia.com/phoenix/application)
- Developer contact information: Required, unique contact information. [Evidence](https://programs.nvidia.com/phoenix/application)
- Business executive contact information: Required, unique contact information. [Evidence](https://programs.nvidia.com/phoenix/application)
- Company incorporation date: Required per public FAQ. [Evidence](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/startups/)
- Funding status: Required per public FAQ. [Evidence](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/startups/)
- Company pitch deck: Required per public FAQ. [Evidence](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/startups/)

## Unresolved and access limits
- Business-email login gate blocks later fields and member-only benefit terms. Developer/executive may require two separate contacts; not safe to promise solo eligibility. Benefit-specific expiry and export/country restrictions unresolved.

## Official evidence
- [NVIDIA Inception official programme page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/startups/) — Current as inspected 2026-10-12; publication/update date not supplied
- [Public application landing and minimum requirements](https://programs.nvidia.com/phoenix/application) — Current rendered 2026-10-12; publication/update date not supplied

## Planning caution
No application, account creation or outreach was performed. Undisclosed conditions are not permission to spend. Headline credits, pools and investor access are not unrestricted cash. No future edition is assumed from a past edition.
