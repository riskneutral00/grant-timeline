Note markdown# Miaoli County Local SBIR

2026 intake closed; next cycle unannounced. This is a matched R&D grant with a TWD 1 million individual ceiling, rather than unrestricted prize money.

Innovative software/knowledge/service systems fit published scope. Local industries are priorities, not a verified hardware-only restriction; civilian AI business R&D may be relevant with a qualifying local SME.

**Eligibility and people.** Domestic SME registered in Miaoli with funded execution in Miaoli. Capital <=TWD100m OR fewer than 200 employees; no PRC investment/branches, arrears, serious violations, financial-crime conviction or duplicate public-funded R&D. One company/responsible person/year; at most two Miaoli programme awards within three years as stated by county rules. Single-company route verified; no numeric minimum of people identified. Formal R&D personnel evidence, with small-company alternative. Local SBIR is actual innovation R&D; feasibility-only and >TWD1m requests referred to central programmes. No blanket mandatory MVP/video identified.

**Application.** Company ID/founding date, responsible-person identity/date of birth, capital/revenue/employees and registered/R&D addresses. Project title/dates/budget, PI/contact/dedicated accountant phone/email, innovation/advantages/quantified benefits, research staff CVs and budget. Official cover letter and stamped paper proposal; registration, national/local no-arrears, company AND responsible-person bank non-dishonour proof, latest income tax or VAT for under-one-year company, insurance/under-five statement and consents. Postmark or official hand-delivery receipt determines timeliness.

**Award and expenses.** Individual cash grant <=TWD1,000,000 and <=50% eligible costs; normal self-match <=paid capital. Rules allow term <=one year; 2026 proposal template specifies ten months, 2026-09-01 to 2027-06-30. Half after contract, remainder against approved actual closing costs. No equity purchase identified. Applicant finances remainder and reimbursable cash flow. Dedicated account, unused subsidy/interest return, lawful expense vouchers. R&D salaries, materials, equipment use/maintenance and technology/contract research eligible. Separate annex ZIP retrieval failed; finer category caps, CPA expense requirement and overseas IP restrictions not verified and must not be imported from another county.

**Attendance.** Technical review presentation, execution research logs and interim/final technical/accounting reports, inspection and three-year follow-up; annual follow-up report December 1–15. Exact remote/in-person format unspecified.

**Historical deadline.** 2026-06-10; timezone not verified.

**Remaining questions.** 2026 cutoff clock/timezone not stated in read evidence. Proposal contains generic multi-company wording; not counted as a separately verified county joint grant route. Additional annex ZIP unavailable to web retrieval; fee status and detailed expense ceilings unresolved. No 2027 call verified.

**Evidence coverage.** Found official Miaoli guideline during mixed-language search, then verified county call with Chinese query and current proposal template. No numerical staff minimum copied from adjacent local SBIRs.
