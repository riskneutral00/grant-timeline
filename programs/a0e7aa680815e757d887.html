Note markdown# KMITL Academic Melting Pot—Inbound Foreign Researchers

Edition status: Live guidance with revised February2025 terms. No current deadline or guaranteed rolling approval established.

Target population: Foreign professors/researchers with h-index≥10 for STEM/medicine or≥5 for social sciences, humanities, business and related fields. KMITL host must have no overdue project and at most three active projects. Age cap unknown.

Team minimum: Personnel minimum/maximum unknown. Company/cohort numbers are not team-size requirements.

Application fields: Host nomination, both CVs, research proposal and journal-metric evidence; formal inbound host proposal. Detailed agreement and IP fields unknown.

Eligibility: Foreign professors/researchers with h-index≥10 for STEM/medicine or≥5 for social sciences, humanities, business and related fields. KMITL host must have no overdue project and at most three active projects. Age cap unknown.

Requirements: At least three weeks at KMITL. Joint WoS Q1/Q2 paper/review with JCI≥5 or social-science Scopus Q1/Q2 with CPP≥3, within one year. Periodic performance reports and expense copies within45 days after completion.

Attendance: Foreign researcher physically in Bangkok at least three weeks; higher-budget teaching on site20–45 hours. Outbound route not counted as another foreign landing programme.

Award: Revised2025 inbound project budget≤THB450,000, or≤500,000 with block/module teaching. Round-trip actual travel up to business-class rates, daily1,200, lodging≤2,500/day; teaching≤1,500/hour for20–45 hours. Project budget is not an individual unrestricted cash award.

Expense rules: Local researcher compensation/materials≤45% of budget. Actual reimbursements require retained receipts. Entry fee, advance-payment, repayment and IP terms unverified.

Deadlines: Live guidance with revised February2025 terms. No current deadline or guaranteed rolling approval established.
