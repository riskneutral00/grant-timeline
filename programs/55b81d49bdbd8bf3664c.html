Note markdown# Ningxia Regional Government International-Student Scholarship

Non-Chinese admitted medical students; regional government award, excluding CSC and own university award.

**Verified foreign route:** 持外国有效普通护照的非中国籍公民 — https://www.nxmu.edu.cn/gjjy/info/1034/1325.htm

**Cycle:** Published admission guide without verified 2026/27 funding deadline; amounts historical/undated, not confirmed current offer.

**Eligibility:** Bachelor age≥18, high-school grades≥70/B, English subject≥70 for English MBBS or HSK4 for Chinese. Master bachelor and age≤35; doctorate master and age≤40. Good conduct/health; graduate language follows applicable host/MOE policy.

**Application:** International admission form, notarized diploma/transcript and translations, foreign passport, photos, medical form, graduate study/research plan and two academic references; postal/email routes publicly described, no submission made.

**Deliverable:** Degree study/research and admission requirements; no startup MVP or pitch-video requirement verified.

**Attendance:** Full medical degree study in Yinchuan; campus registration and appropriate admission/visa documents.

**Award:** Published guide: normal-degree tuition/campus housing/insurance waiver; master CNY2,500/month, doctoral CNY3,000/month; outstanding self-funded new bachelor students CNY20,000 scholarship.

**Costs and conditions:** Admission fee CNY400 in guide; scholarship coverage of fee not established. Self-funded tuition CNY12,800 Chinese/CNY29,800 English bachelor; graduate34k/doctoral45k annually; housing5k/year. Award waivers do not imply all costs covered; travel/tax/renewal unknown.

**People:** 1 minimum / 1 maximum. Individual student applicant, not startup-team eligibility.

**Unresolved:** Actual private portal field labels/upload caps, fee (unless explicitly stated), final award contract and current next-call dates not fully verified.

**Distinct family:** Distinct regional authority, fund and application contract; host institutions and scholarship levels count once. Not a national CSC/CAS/NSFC host variant.
