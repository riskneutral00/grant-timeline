Note markdown# Shanghai Magnolia Talent Pujiang Programme

Restricted overseas-returnee/foreign-naturalized talent funding for Shanghai research and enterprise innovation/startups. Distinct from Startup in Shanghai competition.

## 1. Foreign recruitment and value

Official 2024 government guide expressly includes “入外籍留学人员” (overseas students who acquired foreign nationality), not evidence all foreign nationals qualify. Useful conditional foreign-naturalized technical-founder/host grant; substantially narrower than foreign citizenship alone.

## 2. Eligibility and people

Foreign-naturalized overseas-study route confirmed in 2024. Local registered host, under/at published 45-year boundary, recent return/arrival, full-time Shanghai work. Individual route or team of at least three. Startup standing rules bachelor's, one overseas-study year, first-largest shareholder or legal representative with >=30% personal equity. No gender-based restriction found in the inspected public rules. Exact nationality, admission/host and age requirements are edition-specific as described above; no owner eligibility inferred. Individual applicant/recipient route published.

## 3. Application fields and files

Eligibility recognition followed by online project application through host-created applicant account; unit review and startup-park/district recommendation. Public complete form not read; no account created.

## 4. Deliverable

Science/technology research or qualifying technology enterprise; budget/task agreement and completion report. Existing public funding for same research excludes duplicate application. Research results acknowledge grant.

## 5. China attendance

2024 guide at least nine Shanghai work months/year after selection. Standing rules require second-review participation; absence relinquishes application.

## 6. Benefits and fees

Verified 2024: A/B RMB 300,000; C RMB 150,000/person; D RMB 150,000 social science, RMB 300,000 natural science/person or RMB 500,000/team. No assumption 2026 foreign scope/amounts unchanged. Fee status: unknown.

## 7. Expenses and IP

Funds paid to host against written agreement and ring-fenced budget. A/B use Shanghai science-project cost rules; C/D permitted research costs and personal living <=30%. Misuse/suspension consequences governed by standing rules; no organizer equity purchase identified.

## 8. Edition and dates

2024 foreign-naturalized eligibility verified; 2026 call indexed only. 2026 official call found in search index but live page shell and native TLS timeout prevented full read. 2024 quota/arrival cutoffs not carried to 2026. Current foreign-naturalized recognition, fees, contract recovery and academic IP unresolved.
