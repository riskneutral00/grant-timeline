Note markdown# SIIR 2026

Checked 12 October 2026.

## Edition and scope
2026 first-call SIIR rules issuedDecember2025. Second call cancelled after budget allocation; no2027 call verified.

Decision: retain_for_diligence

- **personnel minimum:** None
- **company count:** 1
- **separate routes:** Joint route requires at least three companies; chain route at least ten shops. Those are entity/outlet requirements, not personnel counts.

## Eligibility
- **company:** Domestic company, limited partnership or registered business, positive equity, no foreign branch/mainland investment or disqualifying banking history.
- **individual:** No unregistered individual route.
- **sole proprietor:** Registered business form eligible.
- **limits:** No conflicting ongoing government R&D at cutoff; one company/responsible person peryear; at most two awards in three years.

## Application and product requirements
- **theme:** Smart or low-carbon innovative services with actual market-validation phase and measurable closing benefits.
- **submission:** Online application plus prescribed narrative, financial/legal evidence.
- **video:** No mandatory application video found.
- **international:** International advanced route requires specified target market and export expansion plan.

## Attendance and location
- **application:** Online.
- **review:** PI/project team attend review when notified; joint route each enterprise represented. Exact venue/remote alternative not established.
- **execution:** All project R&D personnel should attend site visits; midterm and final reviews and commercialization verification.

## Award and conditions
- **type:** Matched innovation-service R&D subsidy
- **currency:** TWD
- **individual maximum:** 1500000
- **sports individual maximum:** 2000000
- **international individual maximum:** 5000000
- **sports international individual maximum:** 6000000
- **equity:** No equity purchase. Higher joint/chain ceilings belong to separate tracks.

## Spend, matching, reporting and recovery
- **founder salary:** Formal R&D payroll allocated to project; ordinary staff subsidy cap30%, low-carbon40%. No meal allowances, bonuses or social-benefit provisions. Founder must meet personnel conditions.
- **office rent:** No ordinary office-rent category established; office consumables excluded.
- **travel flights:** Domestic project travel subsidy<=10%; overseas travel must be self-funded.
- **computers equipment:** Approved usage/depreciation; new equipment cost/60, mixed-use and category caps apply. Not full hardware acquisition.
- **subscriptions API:** Prorated project cloud/AI IaaS/PaaS/SaaS expressly allowed; excludes hardware purchases, colocation, maintenance/capital items under cloud category. API must fit approved service and allocation.
- **matching:** At least equal self-funding, ordinarily within paid capital.
- **caps:** Consumables<=15%; technology/outsourcing<=60% subsidy, IP<=30%, consultants<=10%; contracts needed for relevantNT$100k+ items.
- **payment reporting:** 50% following midterm review, remaining50% following final review; invoices, payroll, project records and output verification. No upfront unrestricted prize.
- **clawbacks IP:** Noncompliant, duplicate or unproven costs recoverable; contract/government R&D rights apply. Exact final IP licence and guarantee should be checked before signature.

## Deadlines
- **application close:** 2026-01-30 17:30; timezone not expressly verified.
- **project period:** 2026-02-01 through2026-11-30
- **second call:** Cancelled; do not plan a2026 second-round application.
