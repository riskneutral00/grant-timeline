Note markdown# FinTechSpace Batch 16

Checked 12 October 2026. Public-source diligence; no application or account was submitted.

## Edition and scope

September 2026 Batch 16 international guidance and application/business-plan annexes inspected. Applications open through October 13; residency starts January 2027. Domestic links on webpage still show Batch 15 and must not silently replace current Batch 16 terms.

Team minimum: No numeric team minimum. Resident seats are for qualifying core team; seat caps are not minimum headcount.

Scope: include

## Eligibility

- entity: Registered domestic/international company, capital ≤TWD100m OR employees ≤200; registration proof in Mandarin/English. Domestic company also tax registration, foreign company exempt from that Taiwan tax proof.

- product: Fintech prototype and core innovation such as blockchain, AI, cybersecurity or virtual assets. Regulatory and cybersecurity checks before admission.

- individual_soleprop: Individual applicants do not meet registered-company requirement; sole-proprietor treatment not clear. No founder nationality exclusion stated for international route.

- scope: These are international-route Batch 16 terms; exact updated domestic package should be separately verified.

- integrity: Signed declaration covers company, team and managerial staff: specified convictions and lookback periods, bankruptcy, bill dishonour, unsettled serious credit loss, regulator removal and dishonest conduct. Full form must be checked individually; this is more than an ordinary pending-lawsuit question.

## Product and application requirements

- application: Form 1 and signed/sealed declarations, registration evidence, business proposal ≤15 A4 pages with page numbers. Optional sealed institutional recommendation. No public compulsory video found.

- checks: Financial regulatory and cybersecurity review required. Conditional bank/P2P self-assessment for relevant business.

- resident: Core team members use seats; physical residency and reporting obligations, not a passive grant.

## Attendance and location

- pitch: One day November 3–5; remote option not specified.

- fixed: Taipei, Nanhai Road No.1 floor 13; fixed-desk guideline includes 80% monthly actual attendance. Maximum 6 seats.

- hot: Hot-desk space subject to availability, maximum 10 seats; not evidence of guaranteed full remote participation.

- programme: Required regulatory/cyber review November 23–December 4, signing within 10 working days after acceptance steps; operational/fundraising progress reports.

## Award and commercial terms

- type: Paid subsidised workspace and accelerator services, NOT a cash award or promised investment.

- fixed_desk: 2027-01-01–12-31: NTD2,800/seat/month promotional rate versus 4,000 standard. First rent plus one-month deposit = NTD5,600/seat; monthly prepayment by 30th.

- hot_desk: 2027-01-01–06-30: NTD2,100/seat/month versus 3,000 standard; six-month prepay plus deposit = NTD14,700/seat.

- termination: Early departure generally requires 2 months notice or deposit forfeiture/continuing rent under terms. Fee/deposit commitment must be reviewed before signing.

## Permissible spending and obligations

- founder_salary: No funding for payroll established.

- rent: Participant pays discounted workspace, not a rent cash grant.

- travel: No travel reimbursement promised.

- computers: No cash equipment grant established.

- API_subscriptions: No quantified guaranteed credits in reviewed agreement.

- IP: Applicant owns IP subject to law/agreement and warrants originality; free promotional-use permission.

- reporting_clawback: Operating/fundraising reports, attendance, regulatory compliance and rent duties. False materials, violations or arrears may terminate admission; deposit/fees consequences apply.

## Dates and deadlines

- application: September 3–October 13 2026. Closing hour/timezone not stated in inspected international guide; do not assume end-of-day.

- selection: Eligibility October 20; pitch November 3–5; result November 13; checks November 23–December 4.

- payments_residency: Payment by December 31; January 1 2027 move-in; fixed 12 months, hot 6 months.
