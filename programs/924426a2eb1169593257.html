Edition status: Faculty2026 deadline July20 closed. University quota rules do not prove a2027 call.

Target population: Current undergraduate/graduate students at overseas universities, any field, last-semester GPA≥3.25, English test within two years at TOEIC600/iBT60/CBT150/IELTS5.5, good health, home-university and PSU-host/topic confirmation. Foreign-university targeting; nationality exclusions unstated.

Team minimum: Individual applicant minimum1; referees/cohort slots not contestants.

Application fields: Home-university confirmation, host/topic confirmation, GPA and English evidence. Linked faculty Google form unreadable; exact fields/upload limits unknown.

Eligibility: Current undergraduate/graduate students at overseas universities, any field, last-semester GPA≥3.25, English test within two years at TOEIC600/iBT60/CBT150/IELTS5.5, good health, home-university and PSU-host/topic confirmation. Foreign-university targeting; nationality exclusions unstated.

Requirements: Internship at least six weeks, PSU NEXT-U registration, report within one week after completion. Failure to complete requires full refund unless EILA accepts force majeure.

Attendance: PSU Hat Yai onsite internship, minimum six weeks; advertised project durations six to eight weeks during August–December2026. Three placements grouped once.

Award: THB20,000 partial award; maximum20 scholarships is cohort size. May support airfare, accommodation, living, insurance, passport and visa costs. Not fully funded.

Expense rules: Recipients independently cover actual costs beyond THB20,000 and arrange their budget/insurance. Entry fee and IP agreement unknown. Duration failure can require full repayment.

Deadlines: Faculty2026 deadline July20 closed. University quota rules do not prove a2027 call.
