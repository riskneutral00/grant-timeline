Note markdown# OAC Marine Technology — civilian software

Checked 12 October 2026.

## Edition and scope
2026 OAC marine-technology industry manual, November2025; closed. Only civilian proposals in this record.

Decision: retain_for_diligence

- **personnel minimum:** None
- **company count:** 1

## Eligibility
- **company:** Domestic sole proprietorship, partnership, limited partnership or company; positive equity; Taiwan execution. One company/responsible person application per year.
- **individual:** No unregistered individual route.
- **sole proprietor:** Explicitly eligible registered form; fewer-than-five employee insurance alternatives exist, not an explicit one-person guarantee.
- **scope:** Civilian marine AI, blue economy and net-zero software may fit; defense/unmanned military proposals excluded from this research.

## Application and product requirements
- **submission:** OAC project portal account/login/captcha; public81-page manual contains application, accounting and contract annexes.
- **novelty:** Future project, not already ongoing/completed; marine policy relevance, innovation and feasible measurable results.
- **video:** No application video requirement found.

## Attendance and location
- **application:** Online.
- **selection:** Substantive review explicitly written, followed by award determination; no mandatory application interview in cited manual.
- **execution:** Project checks, reports and site audit cooperation; no remote-only guarantee.

## Award and conditions
- **type:** Matched marine R&D subsidy
- **currency:** TWD
- **maximum:** 5000000
- **rate:** <=50%
- **equity:** No equity purchase.

## Spend, matching, reporting and recovery
- **founder salary:** Eligible actual R&D staff payroll/effort subject to approved budget; founder status alone insufficient.
- **office rent:** Project-specific site rentals can fit other operating cost; ordinary standing office overhead is not thereby approved.
- **travel flights:** Domestic project travel only in stated category; no overseas entitlement.
- **computers equipment:** Capital expenditure excluded. Approved rental of project machinery/equipment allowed; do not budget full computer purchase.
- **subscriptions API:** Software/service costs need approved classification; API subscriptions not expressly confirmed.
- **matching:** Self contribution ordinarily below paid capital, or adequate financial plan.
- **categories:** Personnel, consumables, technology transfer/contract research, domestic travel and other operating expenses. Management<=10%. Project site, transport-vehicle and vessel rentals can qualify.
- **reporting:** Quarterly at least two checkpoints, logs and financial evidence; audits and closeout.
- **clawbacks:** Nonexecution without valid reason can bring three-year exclusion; false declarations/breach can cancel and recover. Confidentiality breach can lead to ten-year ban. Contract includes representative joint-guarantee liability.
- **IP:** Possible national ownership for national security, major marine/ecological impact, stipulated contract/notice or other OAC determination; no relocation of research to mainland China. Do not promise unconditional private ownership.

## Deadlines
- **application close:** 2026-01-20; exact clock not established.
- **implementation:** Approval date through2026-11-30.
