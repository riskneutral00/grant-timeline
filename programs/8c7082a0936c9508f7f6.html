Note markdown# Calling2Scale Gunma Gateway to Japan

European technology startup market-entry programme with a Gunma immersion and conditional travel subgrant.

## Eligibility and participation

Startup from EU or Horizon Europe associated country, PIC registration, TRL 7 or higher, market-ready product and required business/funding readiness. Not globally open to every nationality.

Mandatory Japan immersion during week of November 24, 2026, plus preparatory work and meetings.

## Support and costs

2026 participation free; mentoring and corporate matching, plus EUR 2,200 subgrant for eligible immersion travel/accommodation. No automatic investment.

Participant bears expenses beyond the conditional EUR 2,200 support; precise reimbursement/tax/contract conditions remain unverified.
