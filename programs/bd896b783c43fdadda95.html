## Eligibility and legal form
- **IDEA:** Thai students in Thai higher education or graduates within 5 years; ≥3 qualifying members and TED Fellow route.
- **POC:** Thai juristic entity; Software Park says Thai ownership >51%, qualifying student/recent graduate owns ≥31%. Template additionally requires qualifying shareholder be an authorized signing director. National standing page says Thai ownership ≥51%; do not rely on exact boundary without current rules.
- **unincorporated:** IDEA individual team. POC template says company if any and older national page allows planned incorporation, but current call lists Thai entity; timing unresolved. Sole proprietor equivalence not established.

## Application and product requirements
- **IDEA:** Mockup, initial market validation and complete business plan within 6 months.
- **POC:** Prototype, market validation and first revenue; current TSU call TRL5–9 and clear business plan.
- **video:** No current video requirement found; do not import historical 2021 requirement.
- **template caution:** 2026 linked template cover date and illustrative budgets are not automatically final FY2570 rules.

## Attendance and location
- **application:** Online through Software Park / TED Fellow.
- **programme:** Incubation and pitching; exact Software Park interview, mandatory attendance, venues and remote options not verified.

## Award and cost
