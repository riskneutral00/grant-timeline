Note markdown# NTU Accelerator 2026

Checked 12 October 2026. Public-source diligence; no application or account was submitted.

## Edition and scope

Current official 2026 cohort page verified. 2027 interest registration mentioned, not a confirmed 2027 application call.

Team minimum: 2 committed team members minimum.

Scope: include

## Eligibility

- affiliation: NTU affiliation not required; NTU-related teams prioritised.

- stage: Working MVP/prototype and initial users, PoC or revenue; clear growth milestone and innovative business.

- entity_nationality: Company incorporation/sole-proprietor acceptance, nationality and residence restrictions not expressly specified; do not infer eligibility for all individual applicants.

## Product and application requirements

- commitment: 10-month programme, up to 20 teams. Business development, mentoring and validation/fundraising readiness.

- application: Full current application form unavailable; interest/pre-registration destination is not an application. No verified video/deck file requirements.

## Attendance and location

- programme: March–December 2026, 10 months; committed participation required.

- location: Virtual residency with meeting/event access at Shuiyuan Campus, Taipei subject to facility policy. “Virtual residency” is not proof that every programme activity/interview may be attended remotely.

- interviews_final: Interview, demo/final attendance schedule and exact mandatory frequency unspecified.

## Award and commercial terms

- type: Mentoring, corporate connections, validation and fundraising introductions; no guaranteed cash or investment.

- fees: Programme fee zero; participant bears operating costs, travel, tools and external services.

- equity: No verified compulsory equity percentage in inspected page; agreement not inspected.

## Permissible spending and obligations

- founder_salary: Own operating cost; no grant established.

- rent: No guaranteed office/housing reimbursement.

- travel: Participant expense.

- computers: Participant operating/tools expense.

- API_subscriptions: Participant tools/external-services expense.

- IP_reporting_clawback: Detailed participation/IP agreement not public in reviewed page.

## Dates and deadlines

- 2026: Application December–January, results February; exact dates/year boundary not stated. Programme March–December 2026.

- 2027: Interest/pre-registration only; precise application window and terms pending.
