Note markdown# Taipei SITI R&D

Checked 12 October 2026.

## Edition and scope
Current enduring SITI rules linked in2026; application form dated2026-01-01, plan2024-02-27 and accounting2023-08-07 still officially linked. These dates are disclosed, not represented as newly issued2026 rules.

Decision: retain_for_diligence

- **personnel minimum:** None
- **distinct roles minimum:** 2
- **basis:** PI and accountant cannot be same person. Contact must be formal employee. No three-person minimum established.

## Eligibility
- **company:** Taipei-registered company/business or foreign-company Taiwan branch, registered at least one year.
- **sole proprietor:** Registered business route permitted.
- **individual:** Unregistered individual ineligible.
- **traction:** At least one recognized market proof: prior-year nonzero sales, capital increase or third-party pilot validation.
- **nationality:** Taipei registration is key; foreign branch expressly possible, unlike many central grants.

## Application and product requirements
- **submission:** Prescribed application and R&D plan plus attachments; apply between six months and one day before start.
- **project:** General innovation R&D8–18months; distinguish innovation acceleration and thematic subtracks.
- **video:** No mandatory video found.

## Attendance and location
- **application:** Formal application; no account action performed.
- **review:** PI presents, substitute needs permission; latest2026 meeting notice maximum3attendees, external advisor/vendor at most1. Physical meeting notified.
- **execution:** Corporate-premises checks, milestone and final report verification.

## Award and conditions
- **type:** Matched general innovation R&D subsidy
- **currency:** TWD
- **maximum:** 5000000
- **rate:** <=50%
- **other tracks:** Innovation accelerationNT$3m and thematicNT$2m are separate, with different cost eligibility.
- **payment:** 50% first instalment; balance after final report and accountant audit.
- **security:** First instalment requires bank performance guarantee or pledged term deposit equal to30% of that instalment; validity extends at least three months after project end.
- **equity:** No equity purchase.

## Spend, matching, reporting and recovery
- **founder salary:** Actual eligible full-time R&D salary supported by insurance, payroll and effort; foreign personnel must work lawfully in Taiwan. Founder draw/dividends not qualified by default.
- **office rent:** Shared table venue-rent/decor category is ONLY for Innovation Acceleration commercial events, not general R&D and not permanent premises.
- **travel flights:** Shared table economy overseas flights/accommodation category ONLY for Innovation Acceleration; do not import into general R&D.
- **computers equipment:** Research equipment usage/maintenance allowed; full laptop purchase is not generally reimbursable.
- **subscriptions API:** Software/IP acquisition may qualify within approved outsourcing; cloud market-validation space under market expansion is acceleration-only. General recurring API eligibility unresolved.
- **other:** General R&D personnel, consumables, research equipment use/maintenance, outsourcing and patent costs subject to rules. Office consumables excluded. Accountant audit fee cannot be charged to project.
- **reporting:** Original vouchers, salary tax reporting, budget-purpose compliance, accountant-certified final report. Feedback/contribution obligations included in approved plan.
- **clawbacks:** Unspent grant returned within15days of closeout; non-return can trigger collection/litigation and associated costs. Other false/noncompliant claims subject to recovery.

## Deadlines
- **application:** Rolling; one day to six months before planned start; maximum two applications in any one-year period.
- **project duration:** Eight to eighteen months.
- **2027:** No specific2027 revision verified; recheck enduring rules before submission.
