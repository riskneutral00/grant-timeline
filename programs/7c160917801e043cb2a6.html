## Eligibility
- **individual:** Unincorporated individuals are not applicants. Individual application means one eligible enterprise.
- **company:** Domestic registered SME company, limited partnership or business. Paid capital <=NT$100m OR fewer than 200 regular employees. Branch offices including foreign branches and mainland investment excluded.
- **sole proprietor:** Registered business form eligible; not the same as an unregistered freelancer.
- **nationality residency:** Taiwan registration and Taiwan project execution required; nationality restriction on every founder not found. Foreign R&D personnel need actual Taiwan work and lawful residence/work status.
- **team:** No numeric personnel minimum found; insurance exceptions for fewer than five employees do not establish a one-person entitlement.

## Application and product requirements
- **stage:** R&D after demonstrated feasibility, prototypes and market/pilot verification; previous Phase 1 is not universally mandatory. One or two years.
- **submission:** Online portal; Phase 1 presentation, Phase 2 written plan and evidence. Login-only live portal fields not exhaustively visible.
- **video:** No mandatory application video found.

## Attendance and location
- **application:** Online.
- **review:** PI normally presents; qualified R&D substitute allowed. Maximum three company attendees. Individual review30min; alliance40min. Teams remote instructions exist, but each actual meeting mode is notified separately. Employment proof due three working days before noon; presentation/responses one working day before noon.
- **execution:** Taiwan project and possible site checks; reports and three-year follow-up.

## Award and conditions
- **type:** Matched non-dilutive R&D subsidy, not unrestricted prize
- **currency:** TWD
- **maximum:** 12000000
- **duration amount:** One year NT$6m; two years NT$12m.
- **equity:** No equity purchase in the grant rules.

## Spend, matching, reporting and recovery
- **founder salary:** Company responsible person doing project R&D must be included as R&D personnel; actual payroll, approved person-months and time allocation required. Standard personnel allocation normally <=60%, explain excess. No dividends or arbitrary founder draw.
- **office rent:** No ordinary office rent line established. Office consumables excluded.
- **travel flights:** Necessary domestic R&D/field-validation travel including documented flights; overseas travel not listed as eligible.
- **computers equipment:** Approved research equipment and software usage/depreciation prorated to project. Purchase price is not automatically reimbursable in full.
- **subscriptions API:** Software is recognized in research equipment accounting; ordinary SaaS/API subscriptions are not expressly resolved in the reviewed text. Obtain classification before budgeting.
- **matching:** Subsidy <=50%; self-funding generally should not exceed paid capital unless credible financial plan supplied.
- **reimbursement reporting:** Dedicated accounting, original invoices, payroll/timesheets and progress checks. Semiannual review; Phase 1 PI work record, Phase 2 all R&D staff work records. <=9-month project normally two instalments; longer project final15% holdback. Full advance may require equal bank guarantee. Three-year outcome tracking. Without full guarantee, subsequent instalment requires approved work/accounting reports and cumulative expenditure at least75%.
- **clawbacks:** Unspent funds and interest returned; ineligible costs/missed outputs can reduce award. False claims can trigger recovery and five-year exclusion.
- **IP:** Research outcomes and IP belong to applicant. Government retains free, nontransferable, nonexclusive research use for national/public interests. Mainland implementation is subject to applicable Taiwan investment/technology-cooperation/trade laws.
- **other:** Patent-application budget exists subject to qualified project-generated patent evidence and accounting caps; include it explicitly rather than assume excluded.

## Deadlines
- **application:** Rolling; no fixed 2026 cutoff identified.
- **timezone:** No closing time to convert.
