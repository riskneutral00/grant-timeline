What this event is for

A Taiwan SME R&D subsidy that shares the cost of developing genuinely innovative technology or services.

Theme and focus

Information and communications, electronics, machinery, biotech/pharma, consumer industries and services; civilian software is relevant under ICT or services.

What you would do

Develop an already validated idea into a prototype and small-scale production or trial service, with measurable technical milestones.
