Note markdown# MOFA Taiwan Fellowship

Explicit foreign-researcher funded Taiwan residency. Official application page announces 2028 fellowship intake May 1–June 30, 2027; 2027 award selection intake already closed. Social-science/humanities research, not a general startup relocation grant.

## 1. Foreign recruitment and value

MOFA explicitly recruits foreign experts/scholars from overseas institutions to conduct research in Taiwan. APEC openings are one population allocation within this family, not another programme. Useful funded inbound research for qualified foreign professionals. Low direct relevance to routine SaaS building unless a qualifying Taiwan social-science research agenda exists; do not treat the stipend as unrestricted business income.

## 2. Eligibility and people

Individual application/recipient route; no team formation requirement identified. Foreign overseas professors, associate/assistant professors, postdocs, doctoral candidates/students and equivalent research fellows. Mission-recommended candidates on Taiwan foreign/cross-strait relations also listed. Social sciences/humanities topic tied to Taiwan, cross-strait/mainland China, Asia-Pacific or Sinology required. Currently researching, teaching or studying in Taiwan excluded. No explicit numerical age/gender restriction found; nationality/residence and academic affiliation substantive. No startup company needed.

## 3. Public application fields and files

Online application plus CV/publication list, principally three-page research proposal and two recommendations or recommendation plus chief-administrator consent. Documents also go to Taiwan mission in/near country of residence for preliminary review. Selected candidates then obtain Taiwan academic host consent; July 1, 2026 notice says wait until candidates announced. Detailed live fields unavailable in closed portal; no account/data submitted.

## 4. Actual deliverable

Deliverable is advanced research, not product MVP or pitch. Own Taiwan host/research arrangements. Topic/period changes need programme approval; no required application video identified.

## 5. Taiwan attendance

Research physically in Taiwan for 3–12 months; programme seminars/relevant events required. Research findings >=20 pages within three months of completion; acknowledge fellowship. Leave >14 days in one month suspends that month's stipend. Paid employment requires MOFA consent; limited paid teaching exception appears in agreement.

## 6. Benefits and fees

Monthly TWD 60,000 senior academic/research ranks or TWD 50,000 other eligible ranks. Paid at beginning of month. Direct economy round-trip reimbursed against ticket/payment evidence; regional caps: Northeast/Southeast Asia TWD 35 k, Europe/West Asia 60 k, North America 55 k, Central/South America 75 k, Africa 90 k, Oceania 65 k. Accident death TWD 1 m, accident-injury medical TWD 50 k per detailed rules; general introduction's insurance summary less precise. Application fee status: unknown.

## 7. Expenses and IP

Applicant bears excess costs, dependant support and broader health cover. Programme guidelines state visa fees waived; this is a reported benefit, not personal immigration advice. Concurrent Taiwan government/academic scholarships restricted. No equity or matching capital provision identified; separate entry-fee statement not found. Research report/acknowledgment required; IP transfer terms not established.

## 8. Edition and dates

2028 application's May 1–June 30, 2027 dates appear on current application page. Current introduction still describes 2027 application May–June 2026; distinct editions preserved. Deadline clock/timezone not stated.

## 9. Remaining gaps

Academic/topic/residence restrictions make ordinary software-founder eligibility unproven; owner not assessed. No detailed public live application form or definitive 2028 benefit amendment; award amounts are currently published standing rules, not a promise all 2028 terms unchanged. Insurance covers accidents, not comprehensive family medical support; IP and separate entry fee unresolved.
