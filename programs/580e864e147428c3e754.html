Note markdown# Osaka Foreign-Affiliated Headquarters Location Subsidy

Foreign-affiliated Osaka headquarters property support with minimum operating workforce; not contest team minimum.

## Eligibility and participation

Foreign-affiliated headquarters functions; at least 50 square metres and five regular/equivalent operating workers at the base, with council certification. Workforce is not application team size.

Open within three years; operate purchased base seven years or leased base two years.

## Support and costs

Acquisition: 5% with workforce-dependent caps JPY 15–100 million. Lease: one-third of 24 months' rent with caps JPY 10–60 million.

Restricted capital/rent expenditure; contract and ongoing obligations, fine recovery/tax rules need guidance.

## Application and dates

Exact live form fields and mandatory uploads have not been verified.

Apply before the earliest relevant contract or order day; exact annual deadline not specified.

Unknown participant minimum does not certify solo eligibility. Cohort/company/workforce counts are not participant team minima. This source audit does not establish the owner's eligibility.
