Eligibility
Entity
Company/business/factory registered in Kaohsiung; capital ≤TWD100m OR fewer than 200 employees; project executed in Kaohsiung. Registered sole-proprietor business route exists; private individual alone does not qualify.
Exclusions
PRC investment, branches, related-party joint partners, duplicate/already-developed projects; tax arrears or serious environmental/labour/food/disability violations within 3 years, major public-contract breach within 5 years/suspension and specified financial offences. One annual application per company/responsible person; maximum 3 Kaohsiung awards in preceding 5 years.
Nationality
Founder nationality not independently restricted in reviewed rules; Taiwan local registration and PRC investment restrictions apply.
Requirements
Duration
6 or 9 months; reserve awards 7 months; 6-month project may extend 1 month under rules.
Application
Phase 1 A application and approximately 4-page B concept; invited phase 2 detailed C plan. Word files ≤25MB. Financial, employment and compliance evidence required. No video specified.
Product
Innovative R&D or services with technical/commercial feasibility, measurable milestones and local impact; not subsidy for a finished product.
Attendance
Review
Written concept screening then presentation review; project head/accounting participation as requested. Final presentation explicitly required by contract; remote permission not stated.
Execution
Kaohsiung operations and monitoring/site checks; not an overseas remote accelerator.
Award
Cash
Single ≤TWD1m; two-company joint ≤TWD2m and ≤TWD1m/company. Restricted R&D subsidy, ≤50% total eligible project cost.
Match
Self-funding required; normally within paid-in capital or a documented financing plan.
Payment
Two instalments: first after contract; final after financial/technical closeout and accepted revisions. Amount adjusted to actual eligible expenditure, not flat prize.

Note markdown# Kaohsiung Local SBIR 2026

Checked 12 October 2026. Public-source diligence; no application or account was submitted.

## Edition and scope

2026/ROC115 guide, cost rules, contract and actual A/B/C forms inspected. Closed; no 2027 call verified.

Team minimum: No founder headcount minimum. Single enterprise or exactly 2 independent eligible enterprises jointly.

Scope: include

## Eligibility

- entity: Company/business/factory registered in Kaohsiung; capital ≤TWD100m OR fewer than 200 employees; project executed in Kaohsiung. Registered sole-proprietor business route exists; private individual alone does not qualify.

- exclusions: PRC investment, branches, related-party joint partners, duplicate/already-developed projects; tax arrears or serious environmental/labour/food/disability violations within 3 years, major public-contract breach within 5 years/suspension and specified financial offences. One annual application per company/responsible person; maximum 3 Kaohsiung awards in preceding 5 years.

- nationality: Founder nationality not independently restricted in reviewed rules; Taiwan local registration and PRC investment restrictions apply.

## Product and application requirements

- duration: 6 or 9 months; reserve awards 7 months; 6-month project may extend 1 month under rules.

- application: Phase 1 A application and approximately 4-page B concept; invited phase 2 detailed C plan. Word files ≤25MB. Financial, employment and compliance evidence required. No video specified.

- product: Innovative R&D or services with technical/commercial feasibility, measurable milestones and local impact; not subsidy for a finished product.

## Attendance and location

- review: Written concept screening then presentation review; project head/accounting participation as requested. Final presentation explicitly required by contract; remote permission not stated.

- execution: Kaohsiung operations and monitoring/site checks; not an overseas remote accelerator.

## Award and commercial terms

- cash: Single ≤TWD1m; two-company joint ≤TWD2m and ≤TWD1m/company. Restricted R&D subsidy, ≤50% total eligible project cost.

- match: Self-funding required; normally within paid-in capital or a documented financing plan.

- payment: Two instalments: first after contract; final after financial/technical closeout and accepted revisions. Amount adjusted to actual eligible expenditure, not flat prize.

## Permissible spending and obligations

- founder_salary: Only genuine formal R&D staff project time; founder title alone insufficient. Eligible base/role/technical/manager allowances; overtime, bonuses, pensions, insurance and meals excluded. Personnel normally ≤60% budget, specified sectors 70%; excess justified. Management ≤4 person-months/year; new hires ≤30% R&D person-months.

- rent: No general office/housing rent expense head established.

- travel: No standalone travel head established.

- computers: R&D equipment/software use allocation, not unrestricted full purchase. General office equipment excluded.

- API_subscriptions: Not specifically resolved; must fit approved eligible software/equipment/technical category.

- other: Consumables normally ≤25%, no ordinary stationery; technical transfer/commissioned research ≤50%; advisors ≤TWD10,000/month; domestic patents ≤30,000 and foreign ≤100,000 under limits.

- accounting: Dedicated bank account and separate ledger, originals and monthly actual-cost withdrawals; interest and unused/ineligible funds returned. Midterm and final reports; final draft accounting/report within 10 days of end, revised within 15 days of approval, CPA certification.

- IP: Applicant owns or co-owns research. Government research licence and public-interest/non-use intervention rights; no overseas implementation before obligations complete without written approval.

- clawback: Unused/non-project costs recovered; breach may terminate and demand refund/damages. Subsequent monitoring and records inspection apply.

## Dates and deadlines

- application: 2026-06-17 17:00. Taiwan UTC+8 is local-time interpretation; notice does not separately label timezone.
