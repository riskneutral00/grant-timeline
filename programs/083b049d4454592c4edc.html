Note markdown# TaiwanICDF Professional Workshops — International Human Resource Training Fellowship

Explicit inbound professional training for allied/friendly developing partner countries; funded travel/room/meals and TWD 700/day. Invitation through Taiwan overseas missions required; one workshop family, not thirteen programme additions.

## 1. Foreign recruitment and value

Organizer surveys overseas missions for partner-country training needs, focusing on foreign government officials and NGO professionals. Selected course also admits relevant industry/executive representatives, not all foreign founders. Substantial funded inbound professional exchange for suitable nominated foreign specialists, but short training with return-home/no-business duties limits founder relocation value. Keeping one family avoids padding courses.

## 2. Eligibility and people

Individual application/recipient route; no team formation requirement identified. Developing allied/friendly country professionals through embassy/representative-office/technical-mission invitation. General focus mid/senior government and NGO personnel; 2026 SME course permits trade/investment/smart-park representatives, AI-health course permits relevant executives with >=two years work. English proficiency required. Exact country list and individual age/gender/student restrictions not established; no Taiwan company setup required.

## 3. Public application fields and files

Completed application through Taiwan overseas mission; signed invitation/acceptance returned via mission. Current blank application form/individual field list not retrieved. Public terms require valid passport/travel document and course participant report/evaluation. No account, completed form or communications. No mandatory product, MVP or application pitch/video identified.

## 4. Actual deliverable

Professional short-course learning, site visits and reports in Taiwan. Civilian software relevance demonstrated by AI-health/data/telemedicine and digital-governance themes. Course-specific professional background matters; a tech company alone is insufficient.

## 5. Taiwan attendance

Punctual full course attendance; >=three unapproved absence days and poor participation can end fellowship. 2026 programme contains thirteen courses; smart-health II November 4–13 (entry closedSep 7), SME/smartparks June 2–11 (entry closedMay 4). Return home expected; no accompanying family/friends/pets or employment/business activity during course.

## 6. Benefits and fees

Selected participants' registration, direct economy airfare, arranged room, breakfast/lunch, insurance and TWD 700/day allowance covered. In-kind support, not startup capital or equity. Insurance excludes chronic illness. Unavoidable stopover accommodation against receipts <=USD 150/night, <=USD 450/round trip; arranged stay up to three unavoidable delay days. Application fee status: none.

## 7. Expenses and IP

Passport/vaccination/visa fees, personal room extras, additional itinerary/stay changes and excess costs applicant/employer responsibility. Poor attendance/misconduct may require full repayment including flight, room and allowances. No unrestricted business-spending award; exact allowance remittance dates not verified.

## 8. Edition and dates

Programme schedule active 2026, but actual read example intake deadlines May 4 andSep 7 closed. No family-wide next application date verified; workshop dates not used as application deadlines.

## 9. Remaining gaps

Invitation and partner-country/course-specific professional restrictions; owner qualification unknown. Blank application and exact target-country list unavailable in read sources. Remaining course-specific rule packages not individually audited; no claim every course fits software. No explicit IP allocation for participant output or allowance payment schedule; confirm if later applying.
