Note markdown# Zhejiang Provincial Government Scholarship for International Students

Provincial international-student scholarship established in 2009. Grouped as one family across Zhejiang universities.

## 1. Foreign recruitment and value

Official 2026 host page requires “有效外国护照” and states foreign-student recruitment purpose. Foreign study support with possible technical study value; not unrestricted founder funding.

## 2. Eligibility and people

HZNU master's bachelor's degree, two professor recommendations, under 35; undergraduate high-school equivalent, under 30. Valid foreign passport, Chinese proficiency, excellent performance/conduct; no simultaneous scholarship. No gender-based restriction found in the inspected public rules. Exact nationality, admission/host and age requirements are edition-specific as described above; no owner eligibility inferred. Individual applicant/recipient route published.

## 3. Application fields and files

Host page specifies passport, HSK/language proof and graduate references but directs applicants to international-student office for full checklist; public complete scholarship form not read.

## 4. Deliverable

Admitted/enrolled academic study and annual assessment; software field and host admission must be verified separately.

## 5. China attendance

Study at Zhejiang host; no absence/discipline violations.

## 6. Benefits and fees

HZNU 2026 standing standards: master's RMB 30,000/year, undergraduate RMB 20,000/year. Fee status: unknown.

## 7. Expenses and IP

Partial study support; no promise all tuition/living costs covered. No business equity requirement identified; institutional research IP and refund contract unverified.

## 8. Edition and dates

2026 standing host rules; host-specific calls differ. HZNU says October annually subject to provincial notice; no exact 2026 deadline inferred. Graduate Zhejiang University host separately ended February 28, 2026.
