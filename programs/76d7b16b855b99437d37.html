What this event is for

A three-day research sprint investigating when AI agents collude and how to detect or prevent that behavior. This is a research-paper event rather than a general product pitch.

Theme and focus

Markets and collusion; detection and audit; collusion carriers and countermeasures.

What you would do

Choose an open research question, run a controlled experiment over the weekend, and submit a short research report with limitations and ethical considerations.
