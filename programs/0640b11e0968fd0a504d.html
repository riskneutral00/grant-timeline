## Eligibility

- OFFICIAL FORM requires >=3 core members for team AND enterprise forms. Validation prohibits proceeding/removing below 3. Team first member is top-ranked core member; enterprise first member founder/actual controller.

- Team route includes unincorporated teams, non-Shanghai enterprises with concrete Shanghai setup plan, or Shanghai companies younger than one year at launch.

- Startup companies: nonlisted Shanghai registration Jan 1 2023–Dec 31 2025, previous-year revenue <=CNY30m. Growth: Jan 1 2018–Dec 31 2025, revenue <=CNY200m. High-tech innovative R&D/products/services and uncontested/independent IP.

- Overseas participation allowed; no nationality/residency exclusion stated in announcement. Sole proprietor is not expressly supported. Special tracks have their own conditions.
