Note markdown# MIT $50K Vietnam Entrepreneurship Competition 2026–2027

Checked: 2026-10-12T05:52:00Z

Edition: Live 2026 application round for March 2027 final.

Scope: include; minimum team: 1.

## Eligibility

- Solo or unlimited-size team; lead must be currently enrolled master’s, PhD or postdoctoral student in good standing at accredited Vietnamese university (full/part time). International students eligible.

- All other members affiliated with same institution. Faculty, staff and researchers can be members, not leads; faculty/staff cannot receive prize money. Undergraduates excluded from every role.

- One idea per team, one application per person. Original pre-commercial idea; prototypes/pilots allowed, commercial launch disqualifies. No external funding of any kind for idea (including grants/prizes/donations/loans/equity); own-resource bootstrapping allowed. Disclose funding received after first deadline.

- No company formation requirement; payout can go to registered business only with all members’ written forfeiture of individual shares. Sole-proprietor payout not expressly addressed.

## Application field inventory

- Structured application: Required; individual labels and required/optional branches behind Reviewr account, not publicly exposed. [Evidence](https://www.mit50kvn.org.vn/rules.php?lang=en)

- Written pitch document: Required, maximum 2 pages; excess truncated. [Evidence](https://www.mit50kvn.org.vn/rules.php?lang=en)

- Video link: Required, 90-second pitch; excess truncated. [Evidence](https://www.mit50kvn.org.vn/rules.php?lang=en)

- Eligibility and rights representations: Required by submission; institutional affiliation and original idea rights. [Evidence](https://www.mit50kvn.org.vn/rules.php?lang=en)

- Later external funding disclosure: Conditional required when received after round-one deadline. [Evidence](https://www.mit50kvn.org.vn/rules.php?lang=en)

- Entity prize allocation agreement: Conditional: all members must agree in writing to forfeit individual shares to registered entity. [Evidence](https://www.mit50kvn.org.vn/rules.php?lang=en)

## Deliverables and requirements

- English or Vietnamese application, without organizer translation. Five finalists present in English. Technology-driven innovation, for-profit viability, social impact and execution readiness assessed.

- Final submission cannot be amended or resubmitted. Applicants retain IP; evaluation material restricted to organizers/judges. Avoid highly sensitive proprietary disclosure. No mandatory working MVP or existing traction; commercial operation is actually disqualifying.

## Attendance

- Application: Digital submission.

- Interview: No interview requirement published.

- Final: Mandatory in-person final; finalists must present in English. Location, pitch timing and which/all team members must attend not specified in accessible rules.

- Travel: No explicit airfare/hotel support.

## Award and payment

- Type: Non-dilutive cash prizes.

- Amount: First USD-equivalent 30,000; second 15,000; third 5,000. Paid in VND; conversion rate confirmed nearer final.

- Payment: Team allocates among eligible recipients; recipients handle taxes. Payment timing not given.

- Equity: None; homepage expressly says non-dilutive.
