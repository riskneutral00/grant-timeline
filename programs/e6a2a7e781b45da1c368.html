Note markdownGreater Bay Area Overseas Talent Income Tax Subsidy

Checked October 12, 2026. Current 2026 Shenzhen implementation verified; grouped as one provincial GBA family.

Foreign target: Qualified overseas high-end or in-demand foreign-national workers and professionals paying mainland GBA income tax.

application fields: Published package: passport and visa or permanent-resident ID, talent/role evidence, employment/dispatch/service contract, activated mainland Class I bank account, signed truth/workdays declaration, employer role/business statement and other talent-subsidy evidence. Actual online questionnaire unknown.

eligibility: Foreign nationals expressly eligible in Shenzhen. Recognized high-end/in-demand status, local work or service contract, more than 90 actual Shenzhen days in 2025 and local IIT required. Applicant must still work locally in the qualifying role. Identity changes affect eligible months; simultaneous mainland identity excluded except specified overseas-Chinese categories. No general age/gender/student gate identified.

requirements: Employer review for salary income; pure individual service income can apply directly. Tax-data confirmation required. No startup pitch, MVP or video.

attendance: More than 90 actual Shenzhen workdays in the qualifying year; a stay under 24 hours counts as half a day. Does not fund work wholly abroad.

award: Subsidy of IIT already paid above 15% of taxable income, capped at CNY 5 million per taxpayer/year, itself exempt from IIT. 2026 window covers 2025 tax plus 2024 catch-up claims.

expense rules: Tax reimbursement rather than startup advance. Specified local talent awards deducted. GBA, Qianhai and Hetao IIT preferences cannot be combined. False claims trigger recovery and penalties; handling fees unknown.

deadlines: January 1–March 31, 2026 closed. Next window unknown; guide effective for 2026.

Team minimum: No universal contestant personnel minimum verified. Role, employer and alternative-branch conditions retained below.

Unresolved: Specific talent-directory qualifications, formquestions and othercity implementations not fullyaudited. Future year policy/window unannounced.

Identity: One shared GBA policy under central/provincial notices. City branches and mutually exclusive tax routes are grouped, not counted separately.
