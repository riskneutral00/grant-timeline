Note markdown# NDF Business Angel Investment

Checked 12 October 2026. Public-source diligence; no application or account was submitted.

## Edition and scope

Current programme page and September 1 2026 guidelines/download listing checked; public English first-application form inspected. Continuous programme through 2030, not an annual prize.

Team minimum: No founder minimum published; normally at least one qualifying angel co-investor and one mentor, with committee exceptions.

Scope: include

## Eligibility

- entity: Taiwan registered company or overseas company with substantive Taiwan operating entity; establishment or cash capital-increase investment. Unlisted.

- age_size: Normally within 8 years and paid-in capital/cumulative funding ≤TWD100m; exceptions through investment committee.

- individual_soleprop: Investment in company equity; individuals/sole proprietors do not independently meet company investment structure. Founder nationality unspecified; PRC investment/related-party checks apply.

- co_investment: Founder/team/related-person money does not count as qualifying angel investment. Government ownership must remain below 50%.

## Product and application requirements

- proposal: Business/investment plan, shareholding and financial diligence, commercial/technology evidence and mentor/co-investor arrangements. No universal video/MVP threshold published; equity valuation and feasibility scrutinised.

- valuation: High valuation may require business/R&D growth support or two-year anti-dilution protections.

- instrument: Published English form includes SAFE checkbox, but current operating rules emphasise same share class/price/rights/timing. SAFE eligibility cannot be assumed from checkbox alone.

## Attendance and location

- review: Investment review presentations and due diligence; interview location/remote option not stated.

- after_award: Quarterly financial/operating information, material-event notices, governance and site visits; Taiwan substantive operation required.

## Award and commercial terms

- type: Equity investment, NOT non-dilutive grant. Negotiated dilution and shareholder rights.

- amount: Normally first investment ≤TWD20m; up to TWD30m where qualifying ≥USD1bn institutional investor participates, with NDF amount no greater than that institution. Subsequent cumulative limit TWD100m.

- green_channel: ≤TWD3m, with angel amount at least NDF amount.

- ratio: If NDF exceeds 50% of financing round, new investor(s), not prior shareholders, must contribute at least 50% of NDF amount. Same-round economic terms normally aligned.

- guarantee: Caps are not entitlements; diligence, committee and investment agreements control.

## Permissible spending and obligations

- founder_salary: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- rent: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- travel: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- computers: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- API_subscriptions: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- budget: Investment working capital is not a reimbursement menu or unrestricted personal cash. Permitted uses, reserved matters and any repayment/default rights depend on signed agreements.

- reporting_IP: Quarterly reporting, governance and material-change notices. No blanket assignment of all IP or founder salary permission established.

## Dates and deadlines

- programme: Applications operate through 2030-12-31 according to current page; no fixed 2027 annual window verified.
