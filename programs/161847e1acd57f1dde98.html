Eligibility requirements

  • Keep capital or previous fundraising within NT$100 millionRequired · To apply

    Requirement source ↗
  • Arrange an angel co-investorRequired · To apply

    Co-investment and mentorship normally apply. Cases without co-investment require committee acceptance.

    Requirement source ↗
  • Verify company age at formal acceptanceRequired · To apply

    The Business Angel Investment Program eligibility requires that the entity be established for no more than 8 years before the executive agency issues an acceptance certificate after the application documents have completed the preliminary review and all information is ready. (Example: If a startup's approved establishment date is September 11, 106, the date the acceptance certificate is issued must not exceed September 10, 114.) Committee exceptions can be considered.
