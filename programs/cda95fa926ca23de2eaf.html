## Eligibility
- individual: Principal applicant: HKID holder, aged 18+.
- company: Hong Kong incorporated limited company; founders/cofounders/director 18+. Landing page says incorporated upon admission; March 2026 guidelines describe HK registration/incorporation at application. Resolve timing before applying. Sole proprietorship is not the limited-company route.
- project: Digital-tech, idea/early development. Prior/similar CCMF/CIP projects excluded. Same/similar HKSTP/HK Design Centre seed/pre-incubation/incubation support before or during application/project can disqualify.
- nationality: HKID requirement is not a citizenship restriction; no blanket Hong Kong nationality condition established.

## Application and deliverables
- MVP: Idea-stage allowed; existing MVP not mandatory. Launch within 3 months is preferred, not mandatory.
- selection: Shortlist presentation plus Q&A; team/model/innovation/social responsibility evaluated.
- form coverage: EMS application is login-gated. Only public guidance and EMS workflow inspected; inventory is NOT exhaustive.
- implementation: Six months; interim and final reports. Sign agreement within 30 calendar days of result.

## Remote and onsite attendance
- application: online EMS
- presentation: Cyberport physical or Zoom per EMS; applicant cannot assume free choice
- programme: Six-month project; mandatory onsite attendance schedule not specified

- type: project cash grant
- amount: 100000
- currency: HKD
- equity: No equity term stated in inspected guidance; recipient agreement not public in inspected flow.
- disbursement: 10,000 after agreement;45,000 approved interim;45,000 completed project and approved final. Release within 60 days of complete approved documents. HK personal bank account for individual; designated HK company bank account for company.
