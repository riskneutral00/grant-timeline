Note markdown# 智策AI：2026資策會AI創新實戰大賽

Checked 12 October 2026. Public-source diligence; no application or account was submitted.

## Edition and scope

2026 competition verified. Individual registration closed October 11; proposal submission remains October 18 for already registered teams.

Team minimum: 1–5 participants, plus at least one institutional supervising professor; professor not described as competing team member.

Scope: include

## Eligibility

- individual: Taiwan college/university enrolled students (undergraduate/master) or 2026 graduates, any major/year; all members provide eligibility evidence. Cross-campus teams allowed; one team/person.

- company_nationality: Student competition, no company required; nationality rule not expressly stated, institutional student status is essential.

## Product and application requirements

- proposal: One A4 page team/motivation plus about two A4 pages proposal/execution plan, Word AND PDF. Filename topic_team. Topic from AI tech/industry/cybersecurity areas.

- mentor: At least one campus professor with signed consent. Cybersecurity tests/data must be authorised.

- final: 15-minute in-person presentation plus 5-minute questions; report/slides required December 13. No initial video requirement found.

## Attendance and location

- submission: Every member registers personally online; signed declarations, undertakings, consents and professor consent emailed to chenchihung@iii.org.tw. Leader sends proposal.

- programme: October 30 exchange event; November 2–December 11 mentoring/practice.

- final: December 17 on-site presentation; Taipei Da’an event location stated, exact venue not provided in page; remote option not stated.

## Award and commercial terms

- cash: TWD50,000 / 30,000 / 20,000 for top three; three elite prizes TWD10,000 each.

- certificate: All finalist members completing presentation receive certificate.

- clawback: False/forged identity or eligibility evidence can cancel entry/award and recover prize/money/benefits.

## Permissible spending and obligations

- founder_salary: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- rent: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- travel: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- computers: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- API_subscriptions: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- match_IP: No unrestricted-use promise; complete IP/tax clauses in linked attachments not accessible.

## Dates and deadlines

- registration: 2026-10-11 23:59; proposal October 18 23:59. Timezone not stated, Taiwan local UTC+8 a planning interpretation only.

- final: Report/deck December 13 23:59; final December 17.
