Note markdown# Japan JDS Human Resource Development Scholarship

Japan postgraduate scholarship for eligible foreign public-sector personnel; current Bangladesh 2027–28 route open, country rules differ.

## Eligibility and participation

Audited Bangladesh route: citizen, below 40 on April 1, 2027, bachelor's/16 years education and specified grades; confirmed eligible civil-service, central-bank, judicial or legislative official with at least two years' experience. English/IELTS and controlling-authority permission required; prior/concurrent scholarship exclusions.

Two-year master's at Japanese accepting university, English instruction; selection tests/interviews and preparatory training, then Japan departure August 2027. Return-home contribution expected.

## Support and costs

Accepted fellows receive tuition, return airfare, arrival/accommodation allowances, monthly scholarship, insurance and specified book/shipping/travel support. Exact current cash rates unverified; not founder capital.

Selection-stage transport coverage limited to specified Dhaka tests/interviews for candidates outside Dhaka. Documents not returned. Detailed stipend, dependent support, recovery, tax and final pledge terms unverified.

## Application and dates

Online application plus controlling-authority permission, research plan and prescribed documents; live fields and full download kit not inspected. IELTS and applicable mathematics tests included.

Bangladesh applications October 1–November 30, 2026, 16:00; timezone not explicitly stated. Other countries differ, so no whole-family universal cutoff encoded.
