    Note markdown# PostHog for Startups

    Checked: 2026-10-12T05:53:00Z

    Edition: Current public programme checked 12 October 2026; not a promise of future availability.

    Scope: Provisionally include; team-size uncertainty is not exclusion.

    Team minimum: Unknown; no minimum of 3+ established.

    ## Eligibility and legal form
    - **applicant:** Company <2 years old, funding <$5m; PostHog signup Jan1 2023 onward, company-domain account email and paid usage plan. Generic email not accepted.
    - **incorporation:** Specific legal form and sole-proprietor acceptance not stated.

    ## Application and product requirements
    - **MVP deck video:** None established from public page; account-gated form not inspected.

    ## Attendance and location
    - **format:** Online account/application; optional community events.

    ## Award and cost
    - **type:** US$50,000 PostHog usage credit over 12 months; approval required; not cash.
    - **other:** Advertised partner perks >$12k are separate conditional benefits. YC deal does not stack.

    ## Spending, reporting and legal conditions
    - **salary:** Not cash; cannot pay salaries.
    - **rent:** Cannot pay physical rent.
    - **travel:** Cannot pay travel.
    - **computers:** Cannot buy computers.
    - **subscriptions:** Most PostHog usage including analytics, replay, AI observability and context warehouse.
    - **API:** Most PostHog usage including analytics, replay, AI observability and context warehouse.
    - **excluded spend:** From Sep14 2026: PostHog Desktop, Slack app, Replay Vision, PostHog AI and Inbox excluded. BAA under Boost and paid onboarding/custom services excluded.
    - **reimbursements:** Service-billing credit, not cash reimbursement.
    - **matching:** No cash match established except stated co-pay/overage rules.
    - **reporting:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.
    - **IP:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.
    - **clawbacks:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.

    ## Dates
    - **application:** Rolling; age/funding limits apply.
    - **expiration:** 12 months; no confirmed extension.

    ## Public application fields
    No reliably inspectable field inventory recovered. This is an access gap, not evidence that the application has no questions.

    ## Unresolved and access limits
    - Account and paid-plan prerequisite prevents complete public application-field inventory. No universal cash-equivalent value; use only forecast eligible consumption.

    ## Official evidence
    - [PostHog for Startups official programme page](https://posthog.com/startups) — Current as inspected 2026-10-12; publication/update date not supplied

    ## Planning caution
    No application, account creation or outreach was performed. Undisclosed conditions are not permission to spend. Headline credits, pools and investor access are not unrestricted cash. No future edition is assumed from a past edition.
