Note markdown# AWS Activate

Checked: 2026-10-12T05:53:00Z

Edition: Current public programme checked 12 October 2026; not a promise of future availability.

Scope: Provisionally include; team-size uncertainty is not exclusion.

Team minimum: Unknown; no minimum of 3+ established.

## Eligibility and legal form
- **applicant:** Startup <10 years, pre-Series B, active company website, AWS Paid Tier account. Founders self-funded/new to credits; Portfolio provider-backed with valid Org ID and latest funding within 12 months where applicable.
- **excluded:** Government entities; previous credits of equal/higher amount for requested tier.
- **legal form:** No guaranteed individual/sole-proprietor acceptance established.

## Application and product requirements
- **deck video:** No mandatory deck/video exposed in public guide. Authenticated form branches inaccessible.

## Attendance and location
- **format:** Online; no physical attendance stated.

## Award and cost
- **Founders:** US$1,000 initially; selected applicants may reach US$5,000.
- **Portfolio:** Up to US$200,000, provider/package dependent.
- **AI:** Above $200k invite-only scaling support, not general entitlement.
- **renewals:** Approved higher offers generally give difference from prior credits, subject lifetime cap.
- **expiration:** Usually 1–2 years by package; exact Billing expiry controls.

## Spending, reporting and legal conditions
- **salary:** Not cash; cannot pay salaries.
- **rent:** Cannot pay physical rent.
- **travel:** Cannot pay travel.
- **computers:** Cannot buy computers.
- **subscriptions:** Eligible AWS services and some support, including third-party foundation models through Amazon Bedrock.
- **API:** Eligible AWS services and some support, including third-party foundation models through Amazon Bedrock.
- **excluded spend:** Marketplace excluded except Bedrock third-party model spend; Mechanical Turk, Managed Services, Professional Services, training/certification, Route53 domain registration/transfer, crypto mining, upfront Savings Plan/Reserved Instance fees and ineligible premium support; taxes excluded.
- **reimbursements:** Service-billing credit, not cash reimbursement.
- **matching:** No cash match established except stated co-pay/overage rules.
- **reporting:** Accurate compliance records and documentary evidence on request.
- **IP:** Publicity licence for submitted company marks/materials; no product-IP assignment established.
- **clawbacks:** Misused credits may be void and reimbursement demanded.
- **overages:** Ineligible usage, taxes and usage beyond credits billed; payment card needed. Support-credit exhaustion may leave billable support until downgraded.

## Dates
- **application:** Rolling; Portfolio within 12 months latest funding where applicable; guide 7–10 business-day review (landing 5–10).
