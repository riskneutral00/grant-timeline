Note markdown# Digital Service Innovation Subsidy 2026

Checked 12 October 2026. Public-source diligence; no application or account was submitted.

## Edition and scope

2026 April official ADI 88-page manual inspected via official public-university mirror. Supplied plan_detail_2 URL is stale 2023 material and must not govern 2026. Closed; no 2027 call verified.

Team minimum: One enterprise applies; no numeric employee/founder minimum published.

Scope: include

## Eligibility

- entity: Taiwan registered sole proprietorship, partnership, limited partnership or company in J582/J62/J63 digital industries; positive equity, not bank-refused. Branches and PRC-invested businesses excluded.

- track: Digital innovation, enterprise/consumer services, sustainability or core AI with proprietary innovative R&D. Completed existing technology not eligible.

- limits: One application; generally no more than 3 concurrent government projects for same enterprise/responsible person, with stated exceptions; no more than 2 ADI R&D subsidies in preceding 3 years.

- nationality: Founder citizenship not separately specified, but PRC staff/ICT and investment restrictions apply.

## Product and application requirements

- application: Business-certificate-authenticated online submission, financial/declaration documents and mixed online/uploaded PDF proposal ≤20MB. Review deck required. No general application video requirement found.

- security: Cybersecurity budget ≥7% total; security rating before contracting and at end; third-party app testing if app delivered. Prohibits PRC-origin AI models through API/MCP and specified PRC-brand ICT/software/cloud and data storage/transfers, including relevant Hong Kong/Macao restrictions.

## Attendance and location

- selection: Presentation and requests for additional evidence; physical versus remote review not established.

- execution: Technical and financial monitoring, semiannual reports and final review; no residential accelerator requirement. Outcome follow-up for at least 3 years.

## Award and commercial terms

- type: Restricted, matched R&D grant ≤TWD15m/project, ≤50% eligible total cost; project ≤2 years.

- match: At least 50% own financing.

- payment: Initial payment requires bank performance guarantee; dedicated account/separate books. Further disbursement linked to prior work and ≥75% prior cost execution. Unspent balance/interest returned. Budget approval governs.

## Permissible spending and obligations

- founder_salary: Eligible only for actual approved R&D employment/project time with payroll/time evidence; no founder living stipend.

- rent: General office/housing rent not an established expense head.

- travel: Approved domestic R&D/project travel; not blanket international travel.

- computers: Allocated approved R&D equipment/software use; cannot assume full laptop purchase reimbursed.

- API_subscriptions: Approved professional R&D software and cloud IaaS/PaaS/SaaS rental may qualify using project allocation or incremental cost, excluding setup/consultancy/one-off fees. API service is not automatically accepted, and prohibited-origin AI restrictions still apply.

- other: Personnel, consumables, equipment use/maintenance, intangible technology/commissioned R&D/verification, domestic travel and innovation promotion categories. Technology/research group ≤40%; promotion ≤10%. Specified consumables/technology/research/verification/promotion subsidy share under 50%.

- accounting: Invoices, payment proof, assets/cloud allocation and payroll/time records; final financial audit. Changes require approval.

- IP_clawback: Company generally retains results subject to governmental public-interest/research intervention rights. Misuse, false claims and breach can trigger recovery and 1–5-year disqualification; not unrestricted cash.

## Dates and deadlines

- application: 2026-05-18, submit by 16:59:59 before system closes 17:00. Taiwan UTC+8 is local-time interpretation; no separate timezone label verified.

- future: No verified 2027 application dates.
