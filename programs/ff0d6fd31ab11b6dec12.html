Note markdown# FITI — From IP to IPO Innovation and Entrepreneurship Incentive Program

Academic technology commercialisation training and awards; 2026 second intake closed, next intake unannounced.

## 1. Problem and deliverable

Potentially strong academic spinout funding and mentoring. Two founders could meet people count, but the affiliation/recommendation and TWD500k capital gate matter. Distinct from U-start despite formal referral linkage.

## 2. Eligibility and people

At least 50% of team must be students, graduates within one year, or full-time researchers affiliated with institutions eligible for NSTC research funding. An affiliated full-time assistant professor or above must recommend. No independent age/gender/citizenship/residence restriction found; foreign-recipient forms exist, but do not prove every foreign applicant qualifies. Company not required initially; established companies >one year must disclose and distinguish project from existing products. Rules recommend 2–5 at registration and expressly require 2–5 participating members during selection; do not label the latter merely optional.

## 3. Application fields and files

Public templates: field/team/project names; member name, DOB, institution/title, phone/email; recommending professor and institution-office contact; affiliation, prior FITI/company participation and related-member disclosures. Upload signed application/undertaking/consents, business plan Word/PDF, slides PPT/PDF; recommended plan covers technology/TRL progress, market/buyer evidence, pain points, finances and team. Optional public 1–3-minute video. Current application account not entered.

## 4. Work and originality requirements

Original innovative technology or health application tied to academic research; no same core work previously in FITI top 20. Prototype/business-model completeness evaluated later, not a universal mature-MVP entry rule.

## 5. Attendance

Approximately six months; technology owner attends major opening, camps, demonstrations/matching, reviews and awards with 2–5 participating members. Absence can cancel participation or incentives. Operational and career follow-up and free publicity licence required.

## 6. Awards and fees

Public-entry initial selection TWD10,000, then additional TWD50,000 and TWD200,000 by stages; referral entrants do not get initial TWD10,000. Outstanding award adds TWD1,000,000 company fund, not equity investment. Training free; award places separately determined per batch. Fee status: unknown. Training is explicitly free; a separate entry-fee statement was not established.

## 7. Expenses and IP

Implementation grants distributed to members per signed ratio/receipts, with tax withholding; apply within five working days of stage results. Million-dollar fund paid only to Taiwan company with qualifying registration dates and core project technology; apply within one year. Requires team governance/beneficiary relationship, business/finance plan, bank/tax/register documents and statutory capital audit proving team investment >=TWD500,000 into company capital increase. No fixed payment date; financial-year schedule applies. Funds support startup costs; no blanket receipt-free guarantee beyond published procedures.

## 8. Dates and chronology

2026-06-24; timezone not verified. Second batch intake closed. June 24 noon appears in university circulation, but clock not established in directly read organizer announcement; left null. Organizer states launch March 2013.

## 9. Evidence gaps

No next edition verified. Current 2026-2 download package uses rules revised May 15, 2025; illustrative forms retain older FITI2024 codes. Team investment is a company-fund condition, not an entry fee or state equity purchase. No complete public authenticated application view. Detailed travel reimbursement not established.
