What you would do

  • Expense rules: Implementation grants distributed to members per signed ratio/receipts, with tax withholding; apply within five working days of stage results. Million-dollar fund paid only to Taiwan company with qualifying registration dates and core project technology; apply within one year. Requires team governance/beneficiary relationship, business/finance plan, bank/tax/register documents and statutory capital audit proving team investment >=TWD500,000 into company capital increase. No fixed payment date; financial-year schedule applies. Funds support startup costs; no blanket receipt-free guarantee beyond published procedures.
  • Requirements: Original innovative technology or health application tied to academic research; no same core work previously in FITI top 20. Prototype/business-model completeness evaluated later, not a universal mature-MVP entry rule.
