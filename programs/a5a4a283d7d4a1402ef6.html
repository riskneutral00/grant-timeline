Note markdown# Hsinchu City Local SBIR

2026 intake closed; next cycle unannounced. This is a matched R&D grant with a TWD 1 million individual ceiling, rather than unrestricted prize money.

Knowledge platforms, innovative services and ICT/digital R&D expressly fit civilian software. Local city entity/execution requirements and matching costs are the practical gates.

**Eligibility and people.** Domestic SME registered in Hsinchu City and project execution in the city; Hsinchu County is a different jurisdiction. SME capital <=TWD100m OR fewer than 200 staff. No PRC investment or branches, tax arrears, serious violations or duplicate funding. One company or responsible person/year; at most two grants within three years; unfinished city subsidy projects excluded. One-company route; joint route needs two companies, not two people. Under-five-employee evidence alternative exists; no numeric minimum of personnel identified. Formal project R&D staffing and new innovation necessary. Mandatory pre-existing MVP/video not identified.

**Application.** Company ID/registration/capital/revenue/staff; PI, contact and accountant phone/email; project title/duration, subsidy and self-match, industry, innovation/IP/market. Staff CVs, budget and checkpoints; registration/factory if applicable, latest income/VAT, insurance, national no-arrears, self-check and individual consent; conditional incubator/outsourcing/ethics documents. Online system and proposal Word file <=25MB. No post-submission project-document supplements; qualification supplements have seven-day post-cutoff window. Public forms embedded in PDF were read; login portal not entered.

**Award and expenses.** Individual <=TWD1,000,000 and <=50% project cost; optional joint TWD2,000,000 / TWD1,000,000 per company. Ten months: 2026-08-01 to 2027-05-31. Normally self-match must be below paid-up capital. Half after contract, remainder against accepted closure. No equity purchase identified. Dedicated account/ledger and expense evidence; return interest and unused subsidy. Personnel generally <=60% of total subsidy under published notes, not a copied total-budget cap. Consumables have 25% total-budget reference unless justified; annual maintenance <=20% original purchase cost. Technology transfer table has a 60% limit; account-level subsidy <=50%. Software use/IP licences can qualify as R&D costs; office equipment and VAT excluded.

**Attendance.** PI must personally present at review; relevant technical partner/adviser attends where applicable. Non-attendance can waive application. Execution reports/inspections and three-year follow-up.

**Historical deadline.** 2026-06-22 at 17:00:00; timezone not verified.

**Remaining questions.** Personnel-cap wording is inconsistent: explanatory/accounting notes say 60% of total subsidy, while self-check item eleven says 60% of total project cost. Confirm the applicable basis before budgeting. National map displayed May 19 as deadline; actual current city documents establish it as opening date and June 22 as cutoff. Fee not explicit; no numeric people maximum verified. Clock verified, timezone unstated. No 2027 call verified.

**Evidence coverage.** Native HTTPS download of the official current 49-page PDF succeeded; earlier web retrieval failed. Handbook downloaded but not used for unsupported extra claims.
