Note markdown# Microsoft for Startups (current programme, historically Founders Hub)

Checked: 2026-10-12T05:53:00Z

Edition: Current public programme checked 12 October 2026; not a promise of future availability.

Scope: Provisionally include; team-size uncertainty is not exclusion.

Team minimum: Unknown; no minimum of 3+ established.

## Eligibility and legal form
- **applicant:** Early-stage B2B technology/software/AI startup, pre-seed–Series B, independent/private, eligible headquarters region. MVP in progress or existing.
- **incorporation:** Registered entity required for business verification/$5k milestone. Initial $200 route permits identity verification first; not proof of full sole-proprietor eligibility.

## Application and product requirements
- **verification:** Personal LinkedIn identity route; registered name/address must match legal documents. No public deck/video requirement.
- **advanced credits:** Workload milestones require sustained actual usage, not just activating services.

## Attendance and location
- **format:** Online; no mandatory event attendance established.

## Award and cost
- **type:** Azure service credits, not cash.
- **entry:** $200 non-referral route; $1,000 initial investor-code route per Oct9 application guide.
- **progression:** $5k verified legal business; $25k 5+ workloads, ~$60-day sustained period; $50k 7 workloads; $150k 10+ workloads plus ~$3k/month sustained Azure usage.
- **conditions:** Each qualifying workload ≥$1 spend throughout 60 consecutive days. Higher milestones may be direct; not sums to add together.
- **expiration:** At $25k milestone, one-time two-year extension. Initial expiry not established.

## Spending, reporting and legal conditions
- **salary:** Not cash; cannot pay salaries.
- **rent:** Cannot pay physical rent.
- **travel:** Cannot pay travel.
- **computers:** Cannot buy computers.
- **subscriptions:** Eligible Azure/AI/cloud services.
- **API:** Eligible Azure/AI/cloud services.
- **excluded spend:** Exact Marketplace/third-party-product exclusions not established in inspected pages; external subscriptions/APIs cannot be assumed covered.
- **reimbursements:** Service-billing credit, not cash reimbursement.
- **matching:** No cash match established except stated co-pay/overage rules.
- **reporting:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.
- **IP:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.
- **clawbacks:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.
- **overages:** Sponsorship automatically becomes pay-as-you-go when credits expire/exhaust; saved card can be charged.

## Dates
- **application:** Rolling; typical review 3 business days.
