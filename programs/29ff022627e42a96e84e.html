Non-Chinese bachelor/master/doctoral degree students in Hebei; all fields.

**Verified foreign route:** Applicants must be non-Chinese — https://cice.hebtu.edu.cn/a/en/SPrograms/HBGScholarship/index.html

**Cycle:** Current undated official guidance; new students usuallyJune30 andexisting studentsOctober1–30, but2026/2027 exact cutoff unverified.

**Eligibility:** Diploma appropriate todegree; HSK4≥180bachelor,HSK5≥180master;doctoral HSK5≥180 or IELTS6.0; goodhealth/grades/conduct.

**Application:** Scholarshipform,passport,language,diploma/transcripts,plan,2graduate references,art/musicworks as relevant,China guardian letter under18.

**People:** 1 minimum / 1 maximum. Individual student applicant, not startup-team eligibility.
