Note markdown# Wuxi Taihu Cup International Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition

Foreign international-team entry to Wuxi's local competition; civilian AI and next-generation software routes. One family including city/university/overseas rounds.

**1. Foreign recruitment and value** Official September 2026 international notices allow “路演人为外籍人士” (foreign-national presenter) among the registered three core members; no Chinese-citizenship-only condition in main rules. Strong foreign software-founder route, but three-person minimum; international final can be remote. Landing support is conditional.

**2. Eligibility and people** International founder not returned/arrived for China work before January 1, 2026. Team at least three core members; project not registered in Wuxi before that date and founder not existing Wuxi executive. Intend Wuxi registration or local technology-development/transfer contract. Core members own dispute-free project IP; certain previous competition winners excluded. No gender-based restriction found in the inspected public rules. Exact nationality, admission/host and age requirements are edition-specific as described above; no owner eligibility inferred. International team route minimum is three core members including founder. Local enterprise branch has no numerical people minimum established; do not generalize three to every branch.

**3. Application fields and files** Complete online registration materials and integrity commitment; detailed login fields unavailable. International round PPT named project-ID/presenter/project; template/manual supplied as images/QR, contents not decoded. No application submitted.

**4. Deliverable** Innovative market-facing AI/IT project; no separately stated MVP/video requirement located. Due diligence precedes final. Foreign presenter must arrange own interpreter if unable to use Chinese.

**5. China attendance** 2026 international semifinal online, seven-minute pitch plus five-minute questions. Wuxi November final allows international teams online; other groups onsite.

**6. Benefits and fees** 2026 final cash RMB 100,000/50,000/10,000 first/second/third. Excellence awards no cash. Conditional landing/Taihu Talent B support in 2027 and eligibility to seek A; loan credit up to RMB 10 million is debt, not prize. Fee status: unknown.

**7. Expenses and IP** False claims/IP violations revoke prizes and talent support, enter credit record and may cause liability. Equity investment matchmaking separate. Main rules do not promise universal travel reimbursement or matching-budget exemption.

**8. Edition and dates** 2026 main entry closed; final planned November 2026. June 30 24:00 main entry cutoff. International PPT due September 24 at 16:00 Beijing time; September 28 round already held/planned. November final exact day unannounced. No 2027 entry call; full fee/payout/tax/IP-transfer contract and QR attachments unresolved. Official international notices explicitly state Beijing time; main deadline itself does not explicitly repeat timezone.

**9. Remaining gaps** June 30 24:00 main entry cutoff. International PPT due September 24 at 16:00 Beijing time; September 28 round already held/planned. November final exact day unannounced. No 2027 entry call; full fee/payout/tax/IP-transfer contract and QR attachments unresolved.
