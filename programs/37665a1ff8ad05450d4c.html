Target population: Eligible foreign country/territory and central-government focal-point nomination; age≤45, bachelor and/or related professional experience, English, good health and not pregnant under official rules. Relevant government work preferred. No owner eligibility inferred.

Team minimum: Individual applicant minimum1; referees/cohort slots not contestants.

Application fields: Online and endorsed hard copies through home focal point/Thai mission: application, medical form, transcript, degree, at least three recommendation letters, English result≤2 years and course-specific documents. Country nomination quota is not team size.

Eligibility: Eligible foreign country/territory and central-government focal-point nomination; age≤45, bachelor and/or related professional experience, English, good health and not pregnant under official rules. Relevant government work preferred. No owner eligibility inferred.

Requirements: Full-time study, no programme change or outside part-time employment, no accompanying family. Selection weighs practical use of training after return to government work. Revocation for rule violations.

Attendance: Listed Thai university onsite courses one to two years. Relocation and full-time graduate study; no remote exemption verified.

Award: Revised2026 full support: tuition, monthly living THB7,000 and lodging7,000 or9,000; settlement6,000; books10,000/year; approved thesis fieldwork≤20,000 and printing12,000 or independent-study8,000. Airfare/visa/insurance depend on country support category; non-ODA other countries receive tuition only.

Expense rules: Airfare and Thai visa fees only for full-support eligibility. Front visa/logistics expenses and rental deposit; suggested USD300 pocket money. Own personal baggage, excluded insurance, equipment insurance/transcript/certificate charges. Exact repayment rule unstated.
