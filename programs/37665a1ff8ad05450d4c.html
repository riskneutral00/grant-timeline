What you would do

  • Expense rules: Airfare and Thai visa fees only for full-support eligibility. Front visa/logistics expenses and rental deposit; suggested USD300 pocket money. Own personal baggage, excluded insurance, equipment insurance/transcript/certificate charges. Exact repayment rule unstated.
  • Requirements: Full-time study, no programme change or outside part-time employment, no accompanying family. Selection weighs practical use of training after return to government work. Revocation for rule violations.
