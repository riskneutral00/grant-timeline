Note markdown# Tainan City Local SBIR

2026 intake closed; next cycle unannounced. This is a matched R&D grant with a TWD 1 million individual ceiling, rather than unrestricted prize money.

Explicit AI, big-data, cloud and digital transformation support can fund a new commercial software research project. Administrative costs and restrictions make this a conditional company grant, rather than a quick solo prize.

**Eligibility and people.** Domestic SME registered in Tainan with funded execution in Tainan. Capital up to TWD100m OR fewer than 200 employees. Branches/PRC investment excluded; unfinished city grants and three or more previous city awards excluded. Qualification pack expressly permits under-five-employee alternatives. No founder citizenship or residence condition independently established beyond entity and staffing rules. Individual route is one company; optional joint route is two companies, not two people. AI proposals must describe algorithm/model. Innovative Design route requires 1–3 months of trial operations and economic-benefit verification; not a blanket requirement for every route. New R&D required. Rules prohibit Chinese software and Chinese IT outsourcing in funded work.

**Application.** Company ID/founding date, responsible-person identity/date of birth, paid capital/revenue/staff, registered/contact addresses and industry. PI, contact and dedicated accountant phone/email/mobile, project dates, budget, innovation/IP/market, checkpoints, staff CVs, incubator and prior-funding disclosure. Upload Chinese A4 proposal and qualification scans, including registration, listed tax-year report, national/local no-arrears documents issued after March 20, insurance evidence after February 2026, undertaking/recusal/consent forms. Online formal submission and receipt required; paper not accepted.

**Award and expenses.** TWD1,000,000 individual cap; <=50% total cost. Joint cap TWD2,000,000 with TWD1,000,000 per company. Nine-month project. Proposal dates 2026-09-01 to 2027-05-31. Initial 50% after contract, closing balance based on approved costs. No equity purchase provision found. Rules additionally require self-funded share greater than subsidy in every accounting category and normally below paid-up capital. Payroll <=60% total budget; advisers <=30% payroll; materials <=25%; equipment use/maintenance <=50%; technology and outsourced research combined <=50%. Excludes office equipment and VAT. Closing accounting report must be CPA-certified, paid by applicant outside project budget. Dedicated account and vouchers required; unused subsidy and interest returned.

**Attendance.** Review presenter must be applicant employee listed in project R&D personnel; PI preferred, not strictly required. Advisors/outsourcers cannot substitute. Interim and final technical/accounting reports, closing review and inspection obligations apply.

**Historical deadline.** 2026-05-05 at 11:59:59; timezone not verified.

**Remaining questions.** 2026 guidelines still request ROC113 (2024) tax report; do not silently replace with 2025. Confirm applicable portal guidance when next cycle opens. Public templates read; live login portal was not entered. No mandatory video rule identified. Application fee not explicit; CPA certification is a separate mandatory applicant expense. Timezone unstated; cutoff 11:59:59, system closes noon. No 2027 call verified.

**Evidence coverage.** Current city package read locally after the public ZIP succeeded. The themed-alliance branch was not separately audited or counted.
