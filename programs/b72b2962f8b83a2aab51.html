What this event is for

2026 intake closed; next cycle unannounced. Individual R&D grant up to TWD 1,000,000 with at least matching applicant finance. Domestic SME registered in Tainan with funded execution in Tainan. Explicit AI, big-data, cloud and digital transformation support can fund a new commercial software research project. Administrative costs and restrictions make this a conditional company grant, rather than a quick solo prize.

What you would do

  • Expense rules: Rules additionally require self-funded share greater than subsidy in every accounting category and normally below paid-up capital. Payroll <=60% total budget; advisers <=30% payroll; materials <=25%; equipment use/maintenance <=50%; technology and outsourced research combined <=50%. Excludes office equipment and VAT. Closing accounting report must be CPA-certified, paid by applicant outside project budget. Dedicated account and vouchers required; unused subsidy and interest returned.
  • Requirements: Individual route is one company; optional joint route is two companies, not two people. AI proposals must describe algorithm/model. Innovative Design route requires 1–3 months of trial operations and economic-benefit verification; not a blanket requirement for every route. New R&D required. Rules prohibit Chinese software and Chinese IT outsourcing in funded work.
