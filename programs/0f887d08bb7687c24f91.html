- Final: 2026 finalists exhibit/pitch through InnoEx; exact mandatory 2027 attendance and funded travel unknown.

## Award and payment

- 2027: Unannounced/unverified.

- 2026 international cash: Winner USD 2,000 cash; total package advertised up to USD 100,000 is largely non-cash. Top 30 get two-day InnoEx booth and VIP-stage passes; Top 5 additionally PR; winner four articles.

- 2026 vietnam: First prize cash VND 150,000,000 plus in-kind. Current page advertises VND 5 billion package while indexed excerpt differs; cash is stable, package valuation not reliable.

- Standing payment: Cash to startup representative within 15 working days after award ceremony; organizer collects/remits Vietnamese PIT, other applicable taxes/fees borne by winner.

- Warning: Homepage up-to-USD-1-million figure is not a single cash grant. No equity investment amount promised.
