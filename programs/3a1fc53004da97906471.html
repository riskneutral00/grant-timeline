Content route participants must attend marketing advisory sessions and provide operating documents unless formally exempted. Commercial validation must start by December 2026 and finish by June 30, 2027.

Taiwan cultural-content context; exact mandatory meeting venues unverified.
Award
Cash
Historical caps: TWD5m for content or TWD6m for venues, including tax, and no more than 50% of total project cost.
Sharing
TAICCA receives 30% of validation revenue below the support amount, 20% between support and total project budget, and 10% at or above total budget. Aggregate receipts are capped at its support amount.
Incentives
Up to TWD300k per qualifying outcome: revenue above 150% of support; content ROAS of at least 3.5, or confirmed subsequent venue content. These are conditional bonuses.
Equity
Angel-like risk sharing is described, but no fixed equity stake verified.
Fee
No application fee established; applicant must finance the unsupported budget.
Expense rules
Eligible limits
Content modifications up to 30% of total budget; venue content production up to 50%. Marketing at least 15%, with digital advertising at least 30% of marketing unless justified alternatives accepted.
Excluded
Building rent, space management, administrative management and unnecessary project costs. Capital equipment, software, IP and renovations may only be allocated proportionally.
Accountability
Third-party audit covers the full budget, including applicant funds. Signed agreement required; project period cannot be extended.
