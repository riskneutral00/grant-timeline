Note markdown# MongoDB for Startups

Checked: 2026-10-12T05:53:00Z

Edition: Current public programme checked 12 October 2026; not a promise of future availability.

Scope: Provisionally include; team-size uncertainty is not exclusion.

Team minimum: Unknown; no minimum of 3+ established.

## Eligibility and legal form
- **applicant:** <7 years, Series A or earlier, scalable single software product/service, first programme participation, website and LinkedIn. New/existing Atlas users eligible. Direct bootstrapped route exists; partner referrals encouraged.
- **individual:** Legal form not expressly settled; terms entrants 18+, resident where lawful, valid email; no employees.
- **tiers:** Inspire/Grow/Innovate/Scale exist; current public page does not disclose tier amounts.

## Application and product requirements
- **deck video MVP:** No deck/video upload shown in public unfilled form. Hidden funding-dependent fields not tested by entering data.

## Attendance and location
- **format:** Online.

## Award and cost
- **type:** Atlas credits plus Voyage AI tokens, amount determined by MongoDB; not a verified universal $5k/$100k award.
- **expiry:** 12 months to activate; 12 months after activation to use; extensions discretionary.

## Spending, reporting and legal conditions
- **salary:** Not cash; cannot pay salaries.
- **rent:** Cannot pay physical rent.
- **travel:** Cannot pay travel.
- **computers:** Cannot buy computers.
- **subscriptions:** Atlas eligible future usage and Developer Support; Voyage AI terms/amount unverified.
- **API:** Atlas eligible future usage and Developer Support; Voyage AI terms/amount unverified.
- **excluded spend:** No cash substitute, nontransferable, no past charges; incidental costs/taxes recipient pays.
- **reimbursements:** Service-billing credit, not cash reimbursement.
- **matching:** No cash match established except stated co-pay/overage rules.
- **reporting:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.
- **IP:** Linked terms permit company/name/logo/likeness and product-usage-data publicity.
- **clawbacks:** Disqualification for breach; unreachable recipient after five days may forfeit package; conditional tax-document request.

## Dates
- **application:** Rolling; FAQ follow up after 5 business days; linked legacy terms acceptance notification within 10 days.
