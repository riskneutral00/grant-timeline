Note markdown# Nanjing Municipal Government Scholarship for International Students

Nanjing city foreign-student merit award established in 2013; local budget family distinct from Jiangsu provincial and CSC awards.

## 1. Foreign recruitment and value

2026 host call targets “外国籍留学生”; standing NAU page explicitly requires foreign citizenship. Real foreign academic support; no direct startup funding.

## 2. Eligibility and people

NJU full-time foreign undergraduate/master's/doctoral students. No concurrent Chinese government or university scholarship. Current undergraduate host GPA >=4.3 humanities or >=3.8 science, no failed subjects; graduate academic/interim-assessment merits. No gender-based restriction found in the inspected public rules. Exact nationality, admission/host and age requirements are edition-specific as described above; no owner eligibility inferred. Individual applicant/recipient route published.

## 3. Application fields and files

Scholarship application, admission letter or transcript, HSK, conduct promise; continuing students also faculty recommendation. Optional publications/awards. One ordered scanned PDF submitted; public Word attachments not fully read.

## 4. Deliverable

Academic study; one-year award, not startup product delivery. NAU's standing graduate admission route requires study plan and two references.

## 5. China attendance

Nanjing university enrolment; withdrawal or disciplinary sanction cancels eligibility.

## 6. Benefits and fees

NJU 2026 undergraduate RMB 10,000; postgraduate RMB 20,000 per academic year; initial quota 20 each, adjustment possible within fixed total. Fee status: unknown.

## 7. Expenses and IP

NAU standing rules require advance tuition payment and annual review. Fees, accommodation and full financial coverage not promised by city award; academic IP unresolved.

## 8. Edition and dates

2026 Nanjing University host selection closed. August 24–28 is NJU host selection, not all-city cutoff. NAU standing page has a different March–July window; do not universalize. 2027 call not announced/read.

## 9. Remaining gaps

August 24–28 is NJU host selection, not all-city cutoff. NAU standing page has a different March–July window; do not universalize. 2027 call not announced/read.
