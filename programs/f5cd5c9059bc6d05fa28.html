Note markdown# PSU Faculty of Science Foreign Graduate Scholarships

Edition status: April1–30 or July1–31,2026 applications closed; second round conditional on unused first-round slots. November studies do not reopen applications. Next unknown.

Target population: Foreign graduate students whose first degree was obtained abroad, applying to PSU Faculty of Science in2026. Bachelor/master prerequisites, health and English IELTS5/TOEFLiBT61 or listed alternatives within two years. Full master award requires first-author Q1/Q2 paper, partner-university link or completed PSU research internship.

Team minimum: Individual applicant minimum1; referees/cohort slots not contestants.

Application fields: Signed blank form: funding type; SDG/partner/research-strategy alignment and work plan; name, age, gender, nationality, passport and contacts; education, GPAX and English. CV, transcript,800-word English research plan, passport, medical and English certificates.

Eligibility: Foreign graduate students whose first degree was obtained abroad, applying to PSU Faculty of Science in2026. Bachelor/master prerequisites, health and English IELTS5/TOEFLiBT61 or listed alternatives within two years. Full master award requires first-author Q1/Q2 paper, partner-university link or completed PSU research internship.

Requirements: Thesis aligns with at least two of SDGs, partners and priority research areas. Six-month progress reports, GPA≥3.25, one master/two PhD first-author Q1/Q2 or eligible WoS papers with faculty acknowledgement. No programme change.

Attendance: Onsite studies in Hat Yai from June or November2026; selection interview online. Appropriate study permission required; tourist entry not accepted for enrollment.

Award: 2026 four full master awards: tuition THB56,000/semester up to four, stipend8,000/month up to24. Partial three master/two PhD awards: tuition56,000/semester up to four/six and double-room rent9,000/semester. Award levels are one family.

Expense rules: Partial funding excludes living, utilities and off-campus accommodation. Application fees, IP and repayment agreement not established; other faculty rules may apply.

Deadlines: April1–30 or July1–31,2026 applications closed; second round conditional on unused first-round slots. November studies do not reopen applications. Next unknown.

Foreign targeting evidence: Eligibility is restricted to applicants with their first academic degree obtained from an abroad higher education institute — https://graduate.sci.psu.ac.th/index.php/en/scholarships-for-foreign

Family identity: Faculty of Science foreign graduate fund with its own rules. Full/partial awards and rounds grouped; distinct from EILA short internship funding.
