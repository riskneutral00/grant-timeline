Note markdown# Chula Media Fellowships

Edition status: Apply by November15,2026, or earlier announced closure. No clock/timezone published. Announced October1,2026; currently announced2027 intake.

Target population: Foreign or Thai journalists employed by an international or English-language news organization in Thailand or abroad, with at least one year of journalism experience, good English and employer permission. Non-Thai citizens receive the larger stipend. No numerical age limit verified.

Team minimum: Individual applicant minimum1; referees/cohort slots not contestants.

Application fields: All documents in English: CV; recent photograph within six months, JPG without hat/dark glasses; statement of purpose at most one A4 page with study interests/reporting topics; links to media work from the past two to three years; supervisor acknowledgment letter; official media profile. Email submission, no account or message sent.

Eligibility: Foreign or Thai journalists employed by an international or English-language news organization in Thailand or abroad, with at least one year of journalism experience, good English and employer permission. Non-Thai citizens receive the larger stipend. No numerical age limit verified.

Requirements: At least one GenEd and one faculty course, full programme participation and at least80% class/activity attendance. Work-related exceptions require programme approval. Noncompliance or withdrawal can trigger cancellation, suspension and full or partial recovery of funds; force-majeure leniency is discretionary.

Attendance: January–June2027 at Chulalongkorn University in Bangkok, starting from the actual participation month. Employer must authorize the entire programme. Four fellowship places are not a team-size requirement.

Award: 2027 non-Thai stipend THB30,000 monthly after withholding tax; Thai stipend THB15,000. Tuition/course/training fees up to THB60,000 and university activities/excursions up to THB30,000 per person, paid directly to university units. Workspace, temporary staff card, library, WiFi and sports facilities.

Expense rules: Recipients cover additional international/local travel, travel insurance, visa and bank-opening fees beyond the stated support. Award is non-repayable only while obligations are met; withdrawal may require returning earlier payments. Application fee and IP ownership clauses not located.

Deadlines: Apply by November15,2026, or earlier announced closure. No clock/timezone published. Announced October1,2026; currently announced2027 intake.
