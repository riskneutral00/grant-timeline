Note markdown# A+早期新創補助計畫

Checked 12 October 2026. Public-source diligence; no application or account was submitted.

## Edition and scope

2026 new programme; applications through December 31 2027. Full 70-page official manual reviewed in relevant application, contract and accounting sections; file cover June 2026, download page updated October 1. Search PDF title is erroneous; cover/content are A+ early-stage.

Team minimum: Single enterprise; no minimum founder/employee count stated. Three TARGET CUSTOMERS required, not three founders.

Scope: include

## Eligibility

- company: Taiwan-registered company under 3 years at receipt, positive net worth, not bank-refused or PRC-invested. Individuals/sole-proprietorships are not included in company-only eligibility.

- product: Working functional prototype and independently developed difficult-to-replace technology required. No mature-tech integration, reselling, routine expansion or marketing-only project.

- evidence: Market/customer interaction proof (LOI/MOU/test/site intent) and at least 3 target customers with needs/use case/contact stage; financial capacity evidence.

## Product and application requirements

- MVP: Functional prototype at application; aim for productisation and commercial results.

- deliverables: Year 1 product/prototype validation and at least one formal order; year 2 at least three formal orders in same target market; year-2 commercial outcomes normally ≥50% subsidy. Technical checkpoints at least every 6 months; outcome checkpoint every 12.

- submission: Paper package to Taipei office plus electronic files to rye@tdp.org.tw.

- video: No standalone video mandate found.

## Attendance and location

- review: Required notified pitch/Q&A meeting; remote permission not stated.

- programme: Periodic technical/financial checks and final report; no residential accelerator requirement.

## Award and commercial terms

- type: Restricted R&D/productisation grant.

- cap: Normally up to TWD20,000,000 over 2 years, ≤50% project total; remainder self-funded.

- payment: Contract and staged disbursement; security and reconciliation apply, not freely spendable prize.

- IP: IP clearance required; contract rights and public-benefit obligations apply.

## Permissible spending and obligations

- founder_salary: Only genuine project R&D performed by company-hired personnel, apportioned with time/R&D records and payroll/payment proof; founder title alone is insufficient. Administrative/accounting/project-management work excluded.

- salary_items: Base pay, role/professional allowances, overtime, tax-exempt meals and actually paid bonuses can qualify; employer pension/severance/labour-health insurance excluded.

- rent: No ordinary office/home rent category. Professional R&D software, cloud equipment and bandwidth rentals may qualify as approved apportioned equipment use.

- computers: R&D computer/IT equipment monthly use cost C/36 × project use/months; other equipment C/60. Not full purchase cash reimbursement; administrative equipment excluded.

- travel: Domestic travel for listed R&D personnel; consultant/expert travel and fuel subsidies excluded. No foreign-travel approval established.

- API_subscriptions: Cloud/R&D software rental categories supported; API charges not specifically named, so require classification/approval and project-use evidence.

- other: Consumables, intangible assets, commissioned R&D, verification and patent application categories. Receipts, contracts, payment proof and approved changes required.

- clawback: Termination returns unspent/nonconforming subsidies plus interest; rescission can recover all paid subsidy plus interest, normally within 30 days. Serious breaches can bar grants 1–5 years.

- reporting: Periodic work/financial reports, R&D logs, technical checks, final report and post-award outcome/promotional cooperation.

## Dates and deadlines

- opening: One month after June 16 2026 notice (July 16 by calendar calculation); verify operational receipt start if applying.

- closing: 2027-12-31; explicit cutoff hour not located. Rolling review.
