A+ Early-Stage Startup Subsidy Program

What to know

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis; applicant companies must be domestic companies established for less than three years, with a functional prototype, positive net worth, and evidence of market demand.

What this event is for

Support early Taiwan startups turning difficult-to-replace proprietary technology from a functional prototype into an MVP, customer validation and early commercial results.

Theme and focus

Original core technology, MVP development, customer deployment and market validation.

What you would do

Demonstrate an existing functional prototype and execute a funded development and commercialization plan.
