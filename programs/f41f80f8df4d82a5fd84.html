## Eligibility

- entity: Taiwan SME startup founded after 2018-04-25; PRC-invested companies and Taiwan branches of foreign companies excluded.

- individual_soleprop: Requires eligible business/startup evidence; unregistered individuals not established eligible; sole-proprietor acceptance not resolved from notice.

- focus: AI/SaaS solutions for automotive electronics, plastics/rubber/chemicals, food and logistics; industrial PoC with southern Taiwan industry. Approximately 20 teams.
