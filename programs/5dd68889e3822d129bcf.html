Checked October 12, 2026. 2026 host call plus provincial blank template verified; one province family, not one per university.

Foreign target: Overseas PhD students, graduates and researchers, including foreign nationals, visiting Fujian.

application fields: Blank DOCX read: name, gender, nationality, origin, birthdate, country, discipline, education/institution, overseas employer/title, phone/email, visit dates, study/work history, academic achievements and IP/truth declaration. Host fills cooperation, timetable/results, opinions and seal. Passport/entry evidence, degree verification or student proof and job evidence required.

eligibility: PhD (including current students) or researcher/postdoc at a well-known overseas university/research body; generally age at most 40 and no more than three months since mainland entry. Nationality unrestricted. The audited 2026 FAFU host seeks biology/bioinformatics, food/materials and forestry research; not a generic founder incubator.

requirements: Host agreement specifying tasks and rights. At least three lectures or two months of research/project cooperation; template also offers at least 16 teaching hours. Software relevance conditional on the host's research demand.

attendance: Actual Fujian exchange; monthly support prorated using 30 days. Usually at most six months across years; urgent cases may reach one year.

award: One-off origin-based travel support: Americas CNY 23,000, Europe 20,000, Australia 10,000, Asia 8,000. Monthly work allowance: associate professor+ 18,000; PhD/postdoc 12,000. Source institution/employer location determines origin. HK/Macao/Taiwan routes excluded from this foreign-targeted scope.

expense rules: Travel/work allowances, not venture capital. Host agreement governs tasks and rights/IP. Fees, receipts, tax and clawback rules unknown; no equity promised.

deadlines: 2026 host notice May 22; no verified cutoff or current vacancy status. Next intake unannounced.

Team minimum: Individual applicant expressly permitted; other project/host role conditions retained below.
