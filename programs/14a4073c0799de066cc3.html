Note markdown# METI Japan Internship Program

Foreign professional placement with Japanese SMEs; 2026 intake closed. In-person and online routes grouped once.

## Eligibility and participation

OECD/DAC-listed nationality excluding China; currently residing outside Japan. Generally age 18–39 on August 24, 2026, final-year university student or graduate, Japanese N3/B1 or adequate English. Prior FY2016–2025 participants excluded; career-change applicants may qualify.

In-person October 7–November 26 or November 4–December 20, 2026, up to 30 business days. Online August 24–September 18. All training/final presentation and daily reporting compulsory.

## Support and costs

2026 in-person allowance JPY 3,000 per stay day, economy return airfare, insurance, training, housing/utilities and commuting support. Online route JPY 1,000-equivalent per activity day after completion, plus equipment; not employment salary or startup capital.

After matching, participation cannot be declined under published instructions; interruptions prohibited. Public programme outcomes may be published. Final pledge, IP/tax/refund terms not fully read; visa support is limited to programme.

## Application and dates

Public guidance requires degree/enrolment certificate, recommendations and qualification proof after document screening; actual intake form no longer inspected.

Application closed June 30, 2026, JST; no exact cutoff clock verified. Current internship stay dates are not new applications.
