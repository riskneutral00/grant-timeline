Thailand Foreign Film Production Incentive Measures

Thailand Film Office / Department of Tourism

What to know

Cash reimbursement:15% for THB50–100million qualifying spend, capped25% including extras;20% for THB100–150million, capped30%;25% above150million, capped30%. Extras: Thai personnel5%, tourism5%, designated provinces3%, Thai postproduction3%. Annual budget availability applies. Live2025 guidelines and2026 documentation. Start shooting within120 days of approval, with one discretionary90-day extension. ICM2 within120 days after completion below THB1billion spend,150 days above. Guideline requires minimum one-year application/payout gap; simplified website timing differs. No calendar intake cutoff.

Team minimum: Personnel minimum/maximum unknown. Company/cohort numbers are not team-size requirements.

Application fields: ICM1: foreign company/country/address, authorized representative/passport/nationality/residence, local supervisor, copyright holder, licensed Thai service company; format/runtime/episodes/filming dates/days/spend/release, postproduction contractors/budget, synopsis≤oneA4, claimed extras and crew/location evidence.

Eligibility: Foreign-based production with a Thailand Film Office permit, overseas-origin funds and an incorporated, licensed Thai coordination company. Qualifying Thai spend around THB50million is required: narrative says exceeding50million, while rate table starts at50million. TV commercials excluded; threshold edge needs confirmation.

Requirements: Film permit before ICM1; submit within90 days of permit and before principal photography. Audited ICM2 expenses require receipts and physical/scanned documents. Approved script, environmental/legal compliance and tourism logo/Thailand credits required.

Attendance: Film production in Thailand. Postproduction cannot exceed50% of qualifying local spend. Non-Thai crew pay generally excluded, except foreigners domiciled in Thailand and paying Thai tax for at least three years.

Award: Cash reimbursement:15% for THB50–100million qualifying spend, capped25% including extras;20% for THB100–150million, capped30%;25% above150million, capped30%. Extras: Thai personnel5%, tourism5%, designated provinces3%, Thai postproduction3%. Annual budget availability applies.
