Thailand Foreign Film Production Incentive Measures

Thailand Film Office / Department of Tourism

What to know

Cash reimbursement: 15% for THB50–100 million qualifying spend, capped 25% including extras; 20% for THB100–150 million, capped 30%; 25% above 150 million, capped 30%. Extras: Thai personnel 5%, tourism 5%, designated provinces 3%, Thai postproduction 3%. Annual budget availability applies. Live 2025 guidelines and 2026 documentation. Start shooting within 120 days of approval, with one discretionary 90-day extension. ICM2 within 120 days after completion below THB1 billion spend,150 days above. Guideline requires minimum one-year application/payout gap; simplified website timing differs. No calendar intake cutoff.

What you would do

  • Expense rules: Production fronts large local spending. Rebate is subject to1% withholding and bank-transfer fees, tax-compliant invoices, budget availability and committee review. Disputes or missed requirements can delay or nullify payment. No public entry-fee waiver verified.
  • Requirements: Film permit before ICM1; submit within90 days of permit and before principal photography. Audited ICM2 expenses require receipts and physical/scanned documents. Approved script, environmental/legal compliance and tourism logo/Thailand credits required.
