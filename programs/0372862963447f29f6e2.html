Note markdown# Cross-Domain R&D

Checked 12 October 2026.

## Edition and scope
2026 public guide and forms; application closed2025-12-09. No2027 call verified.

Decision: retain_for_diligence

- **personnel minimum:** None
- **company count minimum:** 2
- **startup company required:** True
- **warning:** Two-company consortium is not evidence that two people suffice; prior three-company minimum should not be carried forward.

## Eligibility
- **company:** Consortium of at least two domestic eligible SMEs with at least one startup, using company/business/limited partnership forms; company-level conditions apply to each.
- **individual:** Unregistered individuals cannot replace consortium enterprises.
- **nationality residency:** Domestic registration and Taiwan R&D requirements; exact individual nationality not universal.
- **startup definition:** FAQ defines startup as founded after2017-10-17, under eight years at this call; not a rolling2026/2027 date.

## Application and product requirements
- **themes:** AI, unmanned systems and net-zero. Restrict this record to civilian activity.
- **submission:** Joint plan, administrative form, slide deck and consortium/IP agreements.
- **MVP:** R&D feasibility and commercialization evidence required in plan; universal existing revenue minimum not established.

## Attendance and location
- **review:** Physical review, at most six consortium attendees, at least one representative from EACH company.
- **execution:** Project reviews and site audits; end-of-project reporting.

## Award and conditions
- **type:** Matched non-dilutive R&D grant
- **currency:** TWD
- **maximum:** 7000000
- **equity:** No equity purchase in grant.
- **payment:** 30% retained as final payment; remaining payments follow contractual progress and cumulative expenditure. Dedicated account and ledger; unused funds/interest returned.

## Spend, matching, reporting and recovery
- **founder salary:** Actual formal R&D payroll and approved project effort. Personnel normally<=60% total; pending hires<=30% total R&D person-months. Annual role reference caps NT$1.5m PI/1.25m researcher/1m associate/750k assistant/500k research assistant, higher amounts need pay evidence. Employer retirement/severance/insurance contributions excluded. International staff must actually work lawfully in Taiwan; mainland personnel excluded from international category.
- **office rent:** No general office rent established.
- **travel flights:** Only eligible project R&D staff domestic travel tied to approved technology/outsourcing cooperation or specified field verification. Flight tickets/boarding and payment evidence required; no generic overseas travel budget.
- **computers equipment:** Approved research equipment usage/maintenance, not blanket computer purchase.
- **subscriptions API:** Exact recurring API/SaaS classification unresolved.
- **matching:** <=50% subsidy; grant<=match, match ordinarily<=paid capital unless finance plan.
- **reporting:** Work/accounting reports, KPI/site visits and three-year performance tracking/exhibitions. Consortium must allocate self-funding and bears joint payment liability. Grant paid through lead; departing member matching must be covered, and consortium falling below original membership can terminate and trigger recovery.
- **other:** Consumables normally<=25% total, explain excess; office consumables excluded. Technology/outsourcing normally<=60%, technology/IP purchase<=30%. Exhibition/competition/conference fees including approved venue/design/equipment rental and registration<=5% total. This venue category does not authorize standing office rent.

## Deadlines
- **application close:** 2025-12-09 17:30 for2026 projects; timezone not explicit.
- **implementation:** Approval-month first day through2026-10-15.
