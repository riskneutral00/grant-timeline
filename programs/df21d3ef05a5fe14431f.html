Note markdown# Zhongguancun International Frontier Technology Competition

Overseas-registered companies and early international research-origin teams; AI among 12 sectors.

**Verified foreign route:** 在京外或境外注册成立的企业，无注册时间限制。 — https://kw.beijing.gov.cn/zwgk/zcwj/202608/t20260821_4831894.html

**Cycle:** Tenth edition: sector applications open until October 31, 2026; rounds November 2026–March 2027; early track rolling.

**Eligibility:** Sector: emerging frontier technology, clear IP, good credit/conduct, no IPO or earlier top-three win; Beijing companies founded on/after August 1, 2019, overseas companies no incorporation-age limit. Early teams or companies founded on/after August 1, 2025; one track/sector only; large-funded early companies move to sector track.

**Application:** Official competition portal registration and project materials; public announcement does not enumerate upload fields, MVP or video limits.

**Deliverable:** Frontier-technology product/business project and roadshow; early concept validation and camp participation. IP must be clear and undisputed; submission licence/ownership transfer unverified.

**Attendance:** Written review then roadshows; final live March 2027. Early track twice-monthly offline pitches and three-month acceleration camps (first December 2026–February 2027). Exact foreign travel/visa obligations unknown.

**Award:** Sector final: CNY1m / 500k / 300k and seven 150k prizes. Conditional sector top-three R&D support up to CNY300k. Early camp: 3–5 winners CNY100k each; separate conditional technology-transfer policy up to CNY3m.

**Costs and conditions:** Investment/debt introductions are not awarded equity or guaranteed money. R&D/transfer caps require separate policy eligibility. Fee, tax, relocation/disbursement/clawback and IP licence contract not stated.

**People:** None minimum / None maximum. Numeric personnel minimum unknown; institution/project counts do not establish a people minimum.

**Unresolved:** Actual private portal field labels/upload caps, fee (unless explicitly stated), final award contract and current next-call dates not fully verified.

**Distinct family:** One competition family across sector, early and thematic tracks; not separate programmes or regional preliminary rounds. Separate from HICOOL and Beijing foreign-R&D centre incentive.
