Note markdown# Google for Startups Cloud Program

Checked: 2026-10-12T05:53:00Z

Edition: Current public programme checked 12 October 2026; not a promise of future availability.

Scope: Provisionally include; team-size uncertainty is not exclusion.

Team minimum: Unknown; no minimum of 3+ established.

## Eligibility and legal form
- **Start:** Digital-native startup, working MVP, clear model and intention to seek venture funding; founded within 24 months; no previous Cloud credits except free trial.
- **Scale:** Recent institutional equity funding pre-seed–Series A; founded <5 years; previous credits ≤$5k; funding verification for stealth.
- **AI:** VC-funded with Gemini integral/planned integral to primary product; pre-seed/seed within 5 years or Series A within 12 months.
- **excluded:** May reject acquired/public companies, education/government/nonprofits, blogs, agencies/dev shops/consultancies and crypto-mining/noncompliant tokens.
- **legal form:** Individual/sole-proprietor and incorporation timing not conclusively specified.

## Application and product requirements
- **MVP:** Start tier explicitly requires working MVP.
- **deck video:** Unknown; application login-gated.

## Attendance and location
- **format:** Online application/resources; no mandatory travel established.

## Award and cost
- **Start:** US$2,000 Cloud credits; not US$200k/350k.
- **Scale:** Year1 100% eligible spend to US$100k; Year2 20% to US$100k additional.
- **AI:** Year1 100% to US$250k; Year2 20% to US$100k additional.
- **cashflow:** Year2 maximum requires $500k eligible spend and leaves $400k payable, assuming full eligible credit; arithmetic planning implication.
- **extras:** Separate conditional Workspace/support/Maps/training benefits; not fungible credits.

## Spending, reporting and legal conditions
- **salary:** Not cash; cannot pay salaries.
- **rent:** Cannot pay physical rent.
- **travel:** Cannot pay travel.
- **computers:** Cannot buy computers.
- **subscriptions:** Eligible Google Cloud including proprietary Gemini/Gemma; separate Workspace/Maps benefits only as awarded.
- **API:** Eligible Google Cloud including proprietary Gemini/Gemma; separate Workspace/Maps benefits only as awarded.
- **excluded spend:** Third-party services, Marketplace offerings and third-party models excluded from programme Cloud credits.
- **reimbursements:** Service-billing credit, not cash reimbursement.
- **matching:** Scale/AI Year2 covers only 20%, applicant pays remaining 80%; do not describe as fully funded $350k.
- **reporting:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.
- **IP:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.
- **clawbacks:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.

## Dates
- **application:** Rolling eligibility; credit windows by tier/award.
- **expiration:** Scale/AI two years; Start credit expiry not fully verified in accessible text.

## Public application fields
- 18-character Google Cloud billing account ID: Required before applying. [Evidence](https://cloud.google.com/startup/pre-funded)
- Funding verification: Conditional: stealth Scale applicants. [Evidence](https://cloud.google.com/startup/early-stage)

## Unresolved and access limits
- Google account gate blocks application fields. General programme legal terms, Start expiry, country/sole-proprietor eligibility, Workspace eligibility and any repayment clauses not fully inspected.

## Official evidence
- [Google for Startups Cloud Program official programme page](https://cloud.google.com/startup/pre-funded) — Current as inspected 2026-10-12; publication/update date not supplied
- [Scale-tier eligibility/benefits](https://cloud.google.com/startup/early-stage) — Current as inspected 2026-10-12; publication/update date not supplied
- [AI-first tier and model exclusions](https://cloud.google.com/startup/ai) — Current as inspected 2026-10-12; publication/update date not supplied
- [Programme FAQ](https://cloud.google.com/startup/faq) — Current as inspected 2026-10-12; publication/update date not supplied
- [Application Google sign-in gate](https://cloud.google.com/startup/apply) — Current as inspected 2026-10-12; publication/update date not supplied

## Planning caution
No application, account creation or outreach was performed. Undisclosed conditions are not permission to spend. Headline credits, pools and investor access are not unrestricted cash. No future edition is assumed from a past edition.
