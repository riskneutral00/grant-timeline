Note markdown# Hualien County Local SBIR

2026 intake closed; next cycle unannounced. This is a matched R&D grant with a TWD 1 million individual ceiling, rather than unrestricted prize money.

Knowledge/service platforms, software systems and design/digital services expressly qualify. Local workplace and restrictions on overseas use of funded results make it conditional for globally sold software.

**Eligibility and people.** Taiwan domestic SME; funded R&D workplace must be in Hualien. Read rules do not expressly require the company's registered address to be Hualien. Domestic SME capital <=TWD100m OR fewer than 200 employees; project execution location in Hualien. Branches/PRC investment, arrears, serious violations, duplicate government R&D and already completed developments excluded. One company/responsible person/year. Single-company application; no numeric personnel minimum identified. Formal employee research staffing with under-five-employee evidence alternative. No fixed mandatory MVP/video rule found. Foreign R&D staffing documentation is distinct from unverified founder nationality requirements.

**Application.** Stage one concept proposal, application and no-arrears evidence; initial basic registration through published Google form, then paper mail/hand delivery. Stage two full technical/budget/staff proposal and review, registration/tax/insurance documents, previous-funding differences, consent/recusal forms and conditional outsourced work/adviser/IP/incubator/ethics evidence. Separate DOCX template was not opened; field list is guideline-derived. No account, completed form or submission.

**Award and expenses.** Individual cash R&D subsidy <=TWD1,000,000 and <=50% approved costs; self-funding >=subsidy. Half after contract, rest against closure. Ten-month term. Landing page expects 2026-09-01 to 2027-06-30; linked rules expect 2026-08-01 to 2027-05-31. Contract must resolve conflict. No equity purchase identified. Dedicated account, original vouchers, cost audit and interest/unspent return. Payroll 60% total-budget reference, future hires <=30% person-months; technology transfer 30% and outsourcing 60% published caps. No general office equipment/consumables or VAT. Software/equipment use and R&D IP can qualify; adviser travel excluded. Funded R&D results cannot be produced/used outside Taiwan for two years from creation; review can extend this restriction where overseas use would affect domestic competitiveness/research layout.

**Attendance.** Concept stage followed by technical review and full proposal; reports, inspections and three-year follow-up. Specific in-person dates not verified.

**Historical deadline.** 2026-05-29 at 17:00:00; timezone not verified. Stage-one concept intake only; no separately verified stage-two public deadline.

**Remaining questions.** Do not interpret overseas-use clause as an approval exemption; international software distribution needs clarification. Direct attachment host returned 403; publisher's own public proxy download succeeded without bypassing authentication. Landing-page/rules execution dates conflict. Fee not explicit. Timezone unstated; cutoff has postmark alternative. No 2027 call verified.

**Evidence coverage.** Rules and accounting PDFs read locally through exact publicly linked publisher proxy; standalone application DOCX not retrieved. May 29 is stage-one concept intake only; no separate stage-two public application cutoff was verified.
