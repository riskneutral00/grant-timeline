Note markdown# JETRO Invest Japan Business Support Center

Approved foreign companies receive Japan establishment and expansion advice and temporary offices.

## Eligibility and participation

Foreign/foreign-affiliated companies establishing or expanding Japan bases; JETRO approval and service conditions. Individual entrepreneurs excluded from described special investment-project visa measure.

Japan offices in six cities plus online support; location variants grouped once.

## Support and costs

Bilingual specialist advice and market/legal/labour information; approved temporary office free for up to 50 business days, while some services are chargeable.

Detailed expense, tax, fee, equity and recovery terms need the applicable current agreement.

## Application and dates

Inquiry/support plan interview; actual form fields not inspected.

Ongoing approval service, no annual deadline established.

Unknown participant minimum does not certify solo eligibility. Cohort/company/workforce counts are not participant team minima. This source audit does not establish the owner's eligibility.
