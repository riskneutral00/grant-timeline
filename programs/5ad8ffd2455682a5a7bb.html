## Edition and status
2026 challenge applications closed June 21; finale explicitly January 16 2027. A 2027 finale does not imply fresh 2027 application intake.

## Team size
- startup track: 2
- growth track: 10
- status: Start-up track: 2 core members; Growth track: 10 employees, both explicit

## Scope decision
include startup track 2 only exclude growth track retain record

## Eligibility
- startup: Company incorporated on/after 28 Feb 2025; maximumone equity investment round;2+core members; HKU student/staff/alumnus or nominated; proprietary uncontested IP/key technology.
- growth: Company before 28 Feb 2025; angel round or higher; funding≤RMB 100 m;10+employees, excluded for<3 people.
- nationality residency: Overseas startups explicitly accommodated; no citizenship requirement published. Company business licence required; sole-proprietorship applicability unresolved.
- additional: Company OR institution may own project. HKU nomination can come from student/staff/alumnus. Projects placed top 3 in an HKU innovation/entrepreneurship competition on/before 10 Feb 2026 excluded.
