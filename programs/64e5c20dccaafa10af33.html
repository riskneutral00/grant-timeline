Note markdown# Shanghai Government Scholarship for International Students

Shanghai-funded international degree-study support. One city scholarship family across university hosts; distinct from CSC.

## 1. Foreign recruitment and value

2026 official host guide: “Applicants must be non-Chinese citizens”. Substantive funded study route for qualified foreign students, not commercial startup financing.

## 2. Eligibility and people

Shanghai Maritime 2026: non-Chinese, healthy; undergraduate under 25/high-school qualification, master's under 35/bachelor's, doctorate under 40/master's. Other simultaneous scholarship support excludes. No gender-based restriction found in the inspected public rules. Exact nationality, admission/host and age requirements are edition-specific as described above; no owner eligibility inferred. Individual applicant/recipient route published.

## 3. Application fields and files

Signed scholarship PDF and admission-system upload. Public PDF fields cover nationality/passport, birth, gender/marital status, residence, education/work, languages, prior China visas, family/emergency contact, programme/major choices and study/research plan.

## 4. Deliverable

University degree admission and successful academic study; no MVP/pitch requirement. Read scholarship PDF without personal submissions.

## 5. China attendance

Physical Shanghai university enrolment; annual attendance, conduct and academic review controls renewal.

## 6. Benefits and fees

2026 host Type A tuition, dormitory, insurance RMB 800/year plus monthly RMB 2,500 undergraduate, 3,000 master's, 3,500 doctorate (12 months). Type B tuition and insurance only. Fee status: unknown.

## 7. Expenses and IP

Separate host admission/application fees and unprovided costs need confirmation; failed annual review suspends/terminates support. No startup equity provision identified; academic IP policy not verified.

## 8. Edition and dates

2026 Shanghai Maritime host intake closed; other hosts differ. 2027 call and host-specific admission costs/programme eligibility unresolved. City family deadline is not universal; inspected deadline belongs to Shanghai Maritime.

## 9. Remaining gaps

2027 call and host-specific admission costs/programme eligibility unresolved. City family deadline is not universal; inspected deadline belongs to Shanghai Maritime.
