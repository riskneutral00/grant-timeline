Note markdown# NSTC International Internship Pilot Program (IIPP)

Explicit foreign-talent recruitment into Taiwan research and future careers. Student-only overseas-enrolment route with TWD 1,000/day, not an employed-founder relocation fellowship. All 2026 entry/matching deadlines closed.

## 1. Foreign recruitment and value

NSTC expressly attracts global foreign student talent to Taiwan research, hoping subsequent study/career connections. Different family from TIIP, TEEP or student startup contests; no speculative benefit imported from them. Real cash-supported inbound research/career pathway for enrolled foreign technical talent; useful conditional on academic status. Significant self-financing and host IP obligations reduce direct solo-founder value.

## 2. Eligibility and people

Individual application/recipient route; no team formation requirement identified. Foreign bachelor/master/PhD students enrolled at overseas university through completion. Mainland China/Hong Kong/Macau nationality OR studying in those regions excluded. No age limit per FAQ; gender restriction not stated. Only once per person; withdrawal/negative record may exclude. Taiwan priority research fields and humanities/social sciences admitted. No business/entity requirement; professional founder without current qualifying enrolment not covered.

## 3. Public application fields and files

Account/profile required, not created. Public how-to: 360×480 headshot, enrolment certificate, recommendation, CV English/Chinese <=twoA4 pages/version, one-minute introduction video for language proof; FAQ also passport/temporary ID. Public privacy notice lists name/nationality/gender/DOB/contact/address/passport/school/department; privacy categories are not proof each live field mandatory.

## 4. Actual deliverable

Individual matched Taiwan academic/research placement 28–90 consecutive days; beginsJune–December 2026 and may run toFebruary 2027. Match completed and PI consent uploaded by applicable deadline, then secondary review/admission/declarations. No business pitch/MVP. PI advisory fee belongs to host PI, not intern benefit.

## 5. Taiwan attendance

Onsite research, confidentiality and institution rules; monthly reports/attendance gate subsequent stipend. Monthly reports 21 st in first/second months; final five days before end perFAQ; oral presentation if required. Host/PI change needs approval. Overseas follow-up encouraged.

## 6. Benefits and fees

TWD 1,000/day for 28–90 days, maximum TWD 90,000 individual allowance. Host institution pays monthly; first payment within 30 working days, no advance. No overlapping Taiwan government funding. Programme cap 600 interns is cohort quota, not team maximum. Application fee status: unknown.

## 7. Expenses and IP

Intern pays airfare, visa, room/living costs, insurance, utilities and host facility/internship fees. Thus not fully-funded travel. Entryfee separately unverified; possible hostcharges cannot be ignored. Violations can reduce/recover allowance. IP follows supervisor's institution, clarify beforehand; no applicant-owned IP assumption.

## 8. Edition and dates

2026 entry/matching deadlinesFebruary 13,April 15,July 15 per current FAQ; clocks/timezones unstated. Research may continueFebruary 2027 but not a new 2027 intake. Old 2025 rules retained above updated 2026 section; used currentdatedsection andFAQ.

## 9. Remaining gaps

Foreign-student only, not ordinary foreign-worker/founder programme; owner eligibility unknown. Legacy iipp.tw failed; verified currentNIARdomain. Publicpage mixes prior-year and currentrules; 2025 rolling/September 15 deadline discarded. Authenticated form not inspected. Exact software placements/host cost/IP terms vary and not asserted; no next call verified.
