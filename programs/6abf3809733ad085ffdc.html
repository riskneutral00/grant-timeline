## Eligibility
- **company:** Taitung-registered SME company, limited partnership or business; capital<=NT$100m OR fewer than200 employees; Taiwan/county execution. Branches/mainland investment excluded.
- **sole proprietor:** Registered business route.

- **limits:** One application per enterprise/responsible person per year; since2021 maximum two awards; no duplicate funding.
