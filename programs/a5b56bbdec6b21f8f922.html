Note markdown# X-HUB TOKYO Inbound Program

Tokyo entry mentoring and matching for overseas mobility/cleantech companies; 2026 intake closed.

## Eligibility and participation

Overseas corporation without Japanese corporation; unlisted; launched/tested product, expansion/team capacity and business English. Representative decision-maker with replacement cover.

Online, mid-October 2026–March 31, 2027; full participation, responsiveness and follow-up surveys.

## Support and costs

Free lectures, mentoring, pitches and Tokyo corporate/VC matching; no guaranteed investment.

Detailed expense, tax, fee, equity and recovery terms need the applicable current agreement.

## Application and dates

English entry sheet plus business/pitch documents; actual logged-in fields unavailable.

Applications June 17–July 31, 2026, 17:00 JST, closed; earlier 2025 lecture calendar is illustrative history.
