Note markdown# 2027 NIA Start-Up Challenge and Accelerator

Checked: 2026-10-12T05:52:00Z

Edition: Confirmed forthcoming 2027 call; actual initial submission form not published until 2026-12-01.

Scope: include; minimum team: 1.

## Eligibility

- Individual, group, or entity; participants at least 18 at submission. Individual prize recipients and team lead must be US citizens or permanent residents. Non-US people may participate on an otherwise eligible team but cannot receive any monetary share.

- Entity must be US-incorporated with US primary place of business. At participation, certify existing or intended US-owned/operated SBIR/STTR-eligible small business by 2027-11-01. Sole-proprietor treatment is not separately stated.

- Cannot be PI on an active NIH SBIR/STTR award at submission close, or previous NIA Start-Up Challenge finalist. Federal entities, HHS employees, official federal-employee participation, judges/organizers and specified immediate family excluded. Other federal employees need ethics review.

## Application field inventory

- Initial registration and submission: Required; complete field inventory unavailable before December 1 opening. No invented fields. [Evidence](https://www.nih.gov/challenges/2027-nia-start-challenge-accelerator)

- Team lead / entity point of contact: Conditional: team lead submits for team; entity contact submits for entity. [Evidence](https://www.nih.gov/challenges/2027-nia-start-challenge-accelerator)

- Innovation concept and benefit from support: Required substantive criteria: aging need, scientific basis, differentiation, commercialization, submitter expertise, need for training/resources, and at least two stated NIA impact pathways; these are judging requirements, not verified portal field labels. [Evidence](https://www.nih.gov/challenges/2027-nia-start-challenge-accelerator)

- SBIR/STTR-eligible company and program-officer meeting: Stage 2 required by November 1, 2027. [Evidence](https://www.nih.gov/challenges/2027-nia-start-challenge-accelerator)

- Prize impact statement: Stage 2 required, one page. [Evidence](https://www.nih.gov/challenges/2027-nia-start-challenge-accelerator)

- Pitch video: Stage 2 required, 7 minutes; product significance/innovation/commercialization, team, delivery. [Evidence](https://www.nih.gov/challenges/2027-nia-start-challenge-accelerator)

- Mentor assessment: Stage 2 evaluation input. [Evidence](https://www.nih.gov/challenges/2027-nia-start-challenge-accelerator)

- Verification and payment documents: Conditional winner requirement within 3 business days of notification; document names not published. [Evidence](https://www.nih.gov/challenges/2027-nia-start-challenge-accelerator)

- UEI/SAM registration: Optional for entities; encouraged to expedite payment. [Evidence](https://www.nih.gov/challenges/2027-nia-start-challenge-accelerator)

## Deliverables and requirements

- Science-driven aging/older-adult innovation including dementia, healthy aging, care delivery and caregiver burden; AI explicitly of interest. No initial mandatory MVP, customers or revenue threshold stated.

- Multiple submissions allowed, but only one finalist selection per individual/team/entity. Avoid all confidential/proprietary content: submission licenses NIH publication of all or parts.

- Training develops a 2-minute elevator pitch and 7-minute presentation. Stage 2 scoring: mentor/impact statement 45 points, video 55 points.

## Attendance

- Application: Online submission; screening call for leading candidates.

- Programme: Approximately five months June–November 2027, 6–8 hours/week, weekly virtual sessions and mentor meetings.

- In person: Three-day bootcamp June 2027 and three-day innovation/investment conference November 2027. Strongly encouraged; comparable training/conference alternative requires advance approval. Registration paid; participant pays travel.

- Final: 7-minute submitted video; no separate final-stage travel obligation stated.

## Award and payment

- Type: Cash prizes plus no-cost accelerator; not described as equity investment.

- Stage 1: Up to 21 finalists × USD 24,000; disbursed within three months of accelerator start.

- Stage 2: Up to 7 selected finalists × USD 70,000; selected January 2028.

- Total pool: Up to USD 994,000; a Stage 2 winner can receive USD 94,000 across stages.

- Payment: Electronic funds transfer; taxes/withholding possible. Team lead receives team prize; entity receives entity prize directly. NIH may cancel/modify or withhold unworthy prizes.

## Expenditure and obligations

- Salary: Not specified in accessible edition-specific rules; do not assume permitted or reimbursable.

- Rent: Not specified in accessible edition-specific rules; do not assume permitted or reimbursable.

- Travel: A portion of Stage 1 cash may explicitly support in-person attendance travel.

- Flights: Travel-related expenses permitted in principle; flights are not separately enumerated.

- Computers: Not specified in accessible edition-specific rules; do not assume permitted or reimbursable.

- Subscriptions: Not specified in accessible edition-specific rules; do not assume permitted or reimbursable.

- Api credits: Not specified in accessible edition-specific rules; do not assume permitted or reimbursable.

- Matching: No matching requirement stated.

- Reimbursement: Not specified in accessible edition-specific rules; do not assume permitted or reimbursable.

- Reporting: Stage 2 deliverables and mentor assessment; separate financial reporting not published.

- Clawback: Not specified in accessible edition-specific rules; do not assume permitted or reimbursable.

- Notes: Stage 1 supports early business/product development. Stage 2 impact examples include team expansion, pilot data, customers and fundraising, but examples are not itemized allowable-cost guarantees.

- Federal funds: Federal grants/cooperative funds only where consistent with original award, with entry on behalf of awardee entity; prize becomes program income. Federal contract funds prohibited for submission/support.

- Ip: Publication permission for submission; ownership transfer not stated. No confidential/proprietary materials.
