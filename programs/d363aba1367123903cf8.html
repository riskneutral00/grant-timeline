Note markdown# Presidential Hackathon Domestic Track 2026

Checked 12 October 2026. Public-source diligence; no application or account was submitted.

## Edition and scope

2026 domestic rules and FAQ verified; submission closed. No 2027 call verified.

Team minimum: Maximum 10; no numeric minimum established. Government staff may apply in personal name; non-government entrants must form a team, minimum unspecified.

Scope: include

## Eligibility

- applicants: Government organisations/staff, private organisations, public and students. No incorporation required by reviewed rules.

- nationality_residency: No general nationality/residency condition established in domestic documents; do not treat silence as guaranteed foreign eligibility.

- proposal: Must not already be underway, tendered, or have applied for government budget, including phased projects. Original work and attribution required.

## Product and application requirements

- theme: Smart agriculture, safety and sustainability; smart governance, safe society and sustainable transition.

- initial: Proposal form only; FAQ explicitly says no initial slides/video.

- final: Physical presentation and observed practical work; prototype/testing/cooperation progress assessed.

- AI: Declare use, tool names, purpose, functions and scope; AI-generated core code is discouraged, not phrased as automatic disqualification.

## Attendance and location

- submission: Online proposal; registration/login.

- initial: Document review.

- programme: Mentoring September 18–November 10; workshop October 14.

- final: Physical final review November 11; venue/remote exception not established. Award ceremony December 13 (tentative schedule).

## Award and commercial terms

- cash: Five excellent teams receive TWD200,000 each.

- other: Trophies/certificates, mentoring and cloud resources based on assessed needs; later government implementation support is not an automatic extra cash award.

- tracking: Winning policy implementation tracked quarterly.

- IP: Broad free, worldwide/time-unlimited nonprofit use/adaptation/sublicensing of supplied promotional materials and likeness; materials rule excludes dashboard/module body itself.

## Permissible spending and obligations

- founder_salary: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- rent: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- travel: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- computers: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- API_subscriptions: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- reimbursement_match: Cash prize exists, but no spending schedule or unrestricted-use promise established; no matching requirement found.

- clawback: Ineligibility, non-original/false submissions can cancel participation; full payment terms not published in reviewed pages.

## Dates and deadlines

- application: July 31–August 31 2026; cutoff hour/timezone not explicit in PDF. Taiwan local time is planning assumption only.

- final: November 11; winner list November 12; ceremony December 13, all marked tentative.
