Note markdown# Osaka Springboard International Startup Market Entry Support

Current overseas-startup legal/business consultation and Japanese corporate matching.

## Eligibility and participation

Overseas startups younger than ten years willing to establish an Osaka base; companies with offices elsewhere in Japan may also apply.

Osaka/Kansai market entry with online meetings; optional themed pitch events grouped once.

## Support and costs

Individual business/legal advice, administrative referrals and corporate matching; no grant/equity promise.

Detailed expense, tax, fee, equity and recovery terms need the applicable current agreement.

## Application and dates

International Startup Application Form linked; actual fields uninspected.

Service June 1, 2026–March 31, 2027; service end is not treated as an application cutoff.
