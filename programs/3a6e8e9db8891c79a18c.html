Note markdown# Chengdu Government Sister-City International-Student Scholarship

Foreign-passport international students from Chengdu sister cities, subject to identity confirmation.

**Verified foreign route:** 持有有效外国护照，来自成都市友好城市的国际学生 — https://ghc.sicau.edu.cn/info/1102/3936.htm

**Cycle:** May 12, 2026 current published guidance; no exact application cutoff stated.

**Eligibility:** Bachelor high-school and age≤30; master bachelor≤35; doctorate master≤40; language≤45; good academic/conduct/health record; language attendance≥90%; HSK4 bachelor/HSK5 graduate or English IELTS5.5/TOEFL60 bachelor, TOEFL70 graduate. No other government scholarship.

**Application:** Online scholarship application and uploads per portal; official sister-city identity-confirmation template. Additional live fields not inspected.

**Deliverable:** Degree study/research and admission requirements; no startup MVP or pitch-video requirement verified.

**Attendance:** Sichuan Agricultural University study and timely in-person registration; language attendance≥90%.

**Award:** 2026 guidance: doctoral CNY30k/year up to three years; master25k up to three; bachelor20k up to four; language1k/month capped10k per academic year.

**Costs and conditions:** Annual review may suspend/cancel. Unapproved late arrival cancels award and bars three years; >10 days unapproved absence/registration failure/contact loss stops payments. No extension for personal delay; registration/fees required. Travel/tax/fee amount unknown.

**People:** 1 minimum / 1 maximum. Individual student applicant, not startup-team eligibility.

**Unresolved:** Actual private portal field labels/upload caps, fee (unless explicitly stated), final award contract and current next-call dates not fully verified.

**Distinct family:** Independent Chengdu municipal sister-city fund, distinct from Sichuan provincial scholarship; no extra count for levels or hosts. Chengdu Belt-and-Road fund retained only as a separate unreviewed lead.
