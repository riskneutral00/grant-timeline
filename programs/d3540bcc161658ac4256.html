Note markdown# OIST Research Internship

Four-to-six-month research internship in Okinawa for eligible undergraduate/master's students or recent graduates.

## Eligibility and participation

Last two years of undergraduate study, master's study or recent graduation from qualifying institutions in Japan or overseas. Research unit fit and institutional approval apply; PhD applicants are not the target population.

Research in Okinawa. Spring 2027 placement window April 1–September 30, 2027; placement duration four to six months.

## Support and costs

JPY 2,400 per working day plus programme travel, accommodation and visa assistance. Family expenses and all personal expenses are not presumed covered.

Detailed expense, tax, fee, equity and recovery terms need the applicable current agreement.
