What this event is for

A paid market-expansion program helping established technology companies prepare commercial entry into Europe and the Middle East. It takes no equity.

Theme and focus

International expansion for B 2 B or B 2 B 2 C scaleups, including fintech, AI, proptech and related technology sectors.

What you would do

Prepare remotely, spend ten full-time days in Hong Kong making expansion decisions and starting outreach, then complete coached follow-through and a scored readiness report.
