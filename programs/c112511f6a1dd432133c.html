Note markdown# Chihlee New Venture Cup — Creativity, Innovation and Entrepreneurship Planning Competition

Taiwan-administered student business/App/cloud planning contest; 2026 registration closed, finalist files still due October 21.

## 1. Problem and deliverable

Software/cloud themes are genuine, but very small prizes and ceremony travel make this mainly student portfolio/feedback value.

## 2. Eligibility and people

Student maximum: six; minimum unstated; advisers are additional (maximum 2). Published ceiling eight total consists of six students plus two advisers; minimum student/adviser count unstated. Record team_max is null because the student ceiling is not a verified whole-team ceiling.

Worldwide college/university students including masters; separate high-school track. Cross-school allowed. English-presentation category requires non-English native speakers; other categories not given this language restriction. No other age/gender/citizenship/entity condition found. At most six students plus two advisors (eight total); no numerical student minimum stated. Do not set people maximum eight without distinguishing advisors.

## 3. Application fields and files

Track/project title; Chinese 200–500-character abstract (English category English), school/department/name/email/mobile and teachers. Initial DOC/PDF; final PPT <=20 pages and 10MB, blind to school/department/advisor identities, plus AI-use table describing tools, stages, purposes, operation and AI-content proportions.

## 4. Work and originality requirements

Business model, App or product proposals; cloud/big data and online marketing explicitly included. AI assistance allowed with human originality/checking, not wholesale generation. No universal built MVP or mandatory video; foreign overseas teams have prerecorded-demonstration alternative.

## 5. Attendance

Both rounds written review. Awarded teams need >=two-thirds registered members present through November 4 award ceremony in Taiwan, unless overseas-rule alternative applicable; clarify exception interaction.

## 6. Awards and fees

Published modest team cash plus certificates; places adjustable. Overseas foreign winning teams added outside existing award quotas. No equity term found. Fee status: unknown.

## 7. Expenses and IP

Fee, reimbursement, remittance and tax terms not stated. Organizer may publicly display/promote work; ownership-transfer clause not found. Participants responsible for third-party IP and honest AI disclosure.

## 8. Dates and chronology

2026-09-30 23:59:00; timezone not verified. Finalist submission October 8–21 at 23:59; award November 4. Finalist stage is not new-entry intake. Rules' AI-table annex says upload before registration cutoff but body says with final PPT; confirm timing. Eleventh edition in 2026; exact first-year launch not verified.

## 9. Evidence gaps

Minimum student number unspecified; account/form not submitted. Fee/transport/payment unknown; award ceremony attendance versus overseas video exception needs clarification. Only Taiwan-run family counted; globally open eligibility is not a global franchise regional round. No next edition verified.
