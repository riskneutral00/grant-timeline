What this event is for

A two-day collaborative hackathon turning social and environmental problems into technology-driven community solutions.

Theme and focus

Community well-being; inclusive and sustainable development; climate action and sustainable communities.

What you would do

Collaborate with peers and mentors to develop practical, scalable ideas for real-world community challenges.
