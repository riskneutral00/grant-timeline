    Note markdown# GitHub for Startups

    Checked: 2026-10-12T05:53:00Z

    Edition: Current public programme checked 12 October 2026; not a promise of future availability.

    Scope: Provisionally include; team-size uncertainty is not exclusion.

    Team minimum: Unknown; no minimum of 3+ established.

    ## Eligibility and legal form
    - **applicant:** Affiliated with programme partner, outside funding up to Series B, no Enterprise plan within last 6 months, no previous GitHub credits/Enterprise licences under programme.
    - **legal form:** Incorporation, nationality/residence, sole-proprietor rules not disclosed.

    ## Application and product requirements
    - **application:** Partner route; no open general application-field inventory. No public video/deck requirement established.

    ## Attendance and location
    - **format:** Online, optional guidance/office hours.

    ## Award and cost
    - **type:** US$10,000 platform credits for up to 12 months, credited to Enterprise account.
    - **products:** Enterprise licences, Copilot licences/premium models/agentic capabilities, Advanced Security and eligible metered services incl Actions.
    - **historical:** Do not carry forward historical 20 free seats/one-year programme as current offer.

    ## Spending, reporting and legal conditions
    - **salary:** Not cash; cannot pay salaries.
    - **rent:** Cannot pay physical rent.
    - **travel:** Cannot pay travel.
    - **computers:** Cannot buy computers.
    - **subscriptions:** Eligible GitHub platform subscriptions/add-ons and metered usage.
    - **API:** Eligible GitHub platform subscriptions/add-ons and metered usage.
    - **excluded spend:** External subscriptions/APIs, salaries, travel, hardware not a covered cash expense; precise billing exclusions unverified.
    - **reimbursements:** Service-billing credit, not cash reimbursement.
    - **matching:** No cash match established except stated co-pay/overage rules.
    - **reporting:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.
    - **IP:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.
    - **clawbacks:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.

    ## Dates
    - **application:** Rolling via partner; advertised 2–3 business-day response.
    - **expiry:** Up to 12 months.

    ## Public application fields
    No reliably inspectable field inventory recovered. This is an access gap, not evidence that the application has no questions.

    ## Unresolved and access limits
    - Partner-specific application inaccessible without selected affiliation; precise consent fields, credit exclusions, legal/termination clauses and account-specific billing after expiry not inspected.

    ## Official evidence
    - [GitHub for Startups official programme page](https://github.com/enterprise/startups) — Current as inspected 2026-10-12; publication/update date not supplied
    - [Current offer and eligibility FAQ](https://github.com/enterprise/startups) — Current 2026; replaces historical seat offer; publication/update date not supplied
