Note markdown# AI Innovation Service R&D Subsidy 2026

Checked 12 October 2026.

## Edition and scope
2026 June application pack; October1 amendment extends innovation-service implementation to2027-03-31. Latest consolidated revised form not accessible.

Decision: exclude_verified_3plus_personnel

- **personnel minimum:** 4
- **basis:** June2026 plan Annex7 requires ability analysis for at least FOUR principal project personnel; not merely four blank rows.
- **company count:** 1

## Eligibility
- **company:** Domestic company, sole proprietorship, partnership or limited partnership in J582/J62/J63 information-software services; positive equity, no mainland investment/branch route.
- **individual:** Registered qualifying enterprise required.
- **sole proprietor:** Explicitly included but four-person project requirement remains.

## Application and product requirements
- **submission:** Industry/commercial certificate authenticated portal; electronic application and PDFs <=20MB.
- **technology:** AI innovation services or AI Agent; cybersecurity at least7% plan resources; Agent requires TrustAI proposal/test evidence for closeout.
- **team:** At least four principal-personnel capability profiles in public plan, therefore outside fewer-than-three scope.

## Attendance and location
- **application:** Online; no physical submission requirement found.
- **review programme:** Review and project audit obligations; exact interview venue/remote policy not established.

## Award and conditions
- **type:** Matched R&D subsidy
- **currency:** TWD
- **innovation service maximum:** 5000000
- **AI Agent maximum:** 20000000
- **equity:** No equity purchase in grant description.

## Spend, matching, reporting and recovery
- **founder salary:** Actual formal project R&D payroll under approved budget; finance/admin staff excluded. Founder only if satisfies eligible staff/payroll conditions.
- **office rent:** No ordinary office-rent category established.
- **travel flights:** Domestic project travel category; overseas travel not established as subsidized.
- **computers equipment:** Usage/maintenance permitted under allocation rules; equipment purchase not automatically reimbursed.
- **subscriptions API:** Project cloud/software rental including approved cloud-service categories may be prorated; generic API is not automatically approved. Hardware acquisition, colocation/capital items excluded from cloud rental.
- **matching:** Subsidy no more than50%; item-level and category caps apply. Outsourcing/intangibles normally <=40%, service promotion<=10%.
- **reporting IP:** Audits and three-year follow-up; specified government intervention rights and prohibition on relevant mainland equipment/services; final signed terms not inspected.

## Deadlines
- **application close:** 2026-07-24 16:59:59; portal closes17:00. Timezone not explicitly established.
- **original project start:** 2026-06-25
- **innovation service end:** 2027-03-31 after October1 amendment; originally2026-11-16.
- **AI Agent end:** 2027-11-16 in original June pack; verify latest award.
