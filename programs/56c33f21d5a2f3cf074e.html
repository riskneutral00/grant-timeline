Note markdown# Changhua County Local SBIR

2026 intake closed; next cycle unannounced. This is a matched R&D grant with a TWD 1 million individual ceiling, rather than unrestricted prize money.

Explicit software/knowledge platforms, systems, innovative services, cloud ICT and commercial open-data applications. Useful for original software R&D by a qualifying local small business.

**Eligibility and people.** Domestic SME registered in Changhua with funded execution in Changhua. Capital <=TWD100m OR fewer than 200 employees. No PRC investment or branches. Arrears, significant violations and duplicate public-funded R&D excluded. One company/year; concurrent work needs sufficient staff and financial capacity. Consecutive funding in previous two years requires self-match >1.5 times subsidy. Single company eligible; optional joint route combines two companies. Under-five-employee declaration allowed. No numeric minimum of people found. New innovation with measurable deliverables; no mandatory video or MVP requirement verified.

**Application.** Application/self-check, project innovation/method/market, milestones, R&D personnel and budget. Four paper proposal copies plus Word/PDF, registration and conditional factory evidence, income/VAT/balance-sheet/P&L, national/local no-arrears and insurance evidence. Individual consents, previous grants and conditional incubator/outsourcing/property-asset documents; optional joint agreement. Paper post/hand delivery then electronic copy via published Google form; form itself not opened.

**Award and expenses.** Individual TWD1,000,000 cap and <=50% of cost; joint TWD2,000,000 total / TWD1,000,000 per company. Ten months with potential one-month extension; published contract dates 2026-08-01 to 2027-05-31. Half after contract and balance after accepted closure. No equity purchase identified. Self-match and working capital required. Dedicated bank account, ledger and original cost evidence; interest/unspent subsidy returned, violations can cause recovery. Payroll <=60% budget; future hires <=30% research person-months; formal employees, not ordinary temporary/part-time staff. Equipment use/maintenance combined cap TWD100,000. Technology/IP and contract research normally <=50% budget unless justified; patent filing may qualify, annual maintenance does not. Office supplies and VAT excluded.

**Attendance.** Technical review presentation, execution inspections and progress/final reporting; three-year follow-up. Exact presentation delegate rule not established in this audit.

**Historical deadline.** 2026-04-30; timezone not verified.

**Remaining questions.** Separate proposal-template link failed; field overview comes from guidelines, not a claim to have opened that form. No verified deadline clock or timezone; historical date only. Application fee not explicit; no 2027 call verified.

**Evidence coverage.** Independent county budget and intake, not a central SBIR phase. Application, accounting and contract sources establish substantive obligations.
