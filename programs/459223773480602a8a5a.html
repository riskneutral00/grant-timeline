**Verified foreign route:** Non-Chinese citizens,friendly to China and in good health — https://ic.sicau.edu.cn/info/1100/1449.htm

**Cycle:** Official2023 policy remains published;2026/2027 call not verified. June15–30 annually described, not promoted as exact current cutoff.

**Eligibility:** Bachelor<30,master<35,doctorate<40,language<45;appropriate diploma; no otherChinese government scholarship; Chinese/Englishlanguage thresholds perlevel.

**Application:** Online scholarship form,transcripts, supervisor certificate, other admission documents; detailed live portal unavailable.

**Deliverable:** Degree study/research and admission requirements; no startup MVP or pitch-video requirement verified.

**Attendance:** Full host study in Sichuan; annual review andregistration.

**Award:** 2023-published guidance: doctorateCNY30k/year,master25k,bachelor20k,language10k; annual evaluation.

**Costs and conditions:** Recipients must first pay tuition, insurance, housing/other fees. Missing unapproved registration cancels award and may bar 3years; annual failure suspends/disqualifies; no extension for self-caused delay.

**People:** 1 minimum / 1 maximum. Individual student applicant, not startup-team eligibility.
