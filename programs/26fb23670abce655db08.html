Note markdown# ikigai Launchpad Fall 2026

Checked 12 October 2026. Public-source diligence; no application or account was submitted.

## Edition and scope

Record name is Fall 2026; form explicitly closes Fall after September 11. Current programme site announces Spring 2027, dates/deadline TBA. Treat present terms as current, not proof Fall contract was identical.

Team minimum: 1; co-founder explicitly not required.

Scope: include

## Eligibility

- individual_company: Can apply without any entity. Accepted founders typically establish a US entity to receive investment. Worldwide founders accepted; no Taiwan entity prerequisite.

- stage: Idea-to-MVP through friends/family, pre-seed and seed; already-funded founders eligible. Technical/domain credibility required.

- commitment: Full time or ready to become full time; able to join Taipei.

## Product and application requirements

- application: English concise answers; seven-page Tally form introduction lists question categories.

- video: Short founder/team intro video requested; duration/upload requirements not visible.

- deck: First interview is 20 minutes and explicitly requires no pitch deck; do not infer no deck ever.

## Attendance and location

- submission: Online.

- interviews: Two interviews; first 20 minutes. Remote format not confirmed.

- programme: All co-founders expected on-site Taipei for core 10-week programme; office provided.

## Award and commercial terms

- type: Equity-linked investment, not grant.

- amount: USD100,000 via SAFE for 8%; no extra programme fee; possible follow-on not guaranteed.

- in_kind: Office, mentoring, introductions, workshops and corporate perks.

## Permissible spending and obligations

- founder_salary: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- rent: Free Taipei office during programme; living/housing costs not promised.

- travel: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- computers: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- API_subscriptions: Perks include examples of software/cloud products, but no universally guaranteed amount or cash substitute established.

- contract: SAFE valuation/conversion, reporting, warranties, termination, IP and permitted-use contract not publicly examined.

## Dates and deadlines

- Fall2026: Applications submitted past September 11 go to Batch 5; cutoff hour/timezone not supplied.

- Spring2027: Announced next batch; no current deadline and application dates TBA.
