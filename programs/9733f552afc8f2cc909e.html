Note markdown# Ningbo Municipal Government Scholarship for International Students

City-funded foreign-student award with a current NingboTech October 15 host intake; existing enrolled students, not general founder relocation.

## 1. Foreign recruitment and value

Current official host call: “Non-Chinese citizens holding a valid foreign passport”. Current explicit foreign population support, low fit without qualifying Ningbo university enrolment.

## 2. Eligibility and people

NingboTech self-funded degree international students in years two–four, normal programme duration, no overdue visa or tuition, good legal/conduct record. Grade/attendance >=85% criteria on page specifically describe university outstanding-student award, not automatically municipal award. No gender-based restriction found in the inspected public rules. Exact nationality, admission/host and age requirements are edition-specific as described above; no owner eligibility inferred. Individual applicant/recipient route published.

## 3. Application fields and files

Scholarship form, transcript, paid tuition/housing evidence, next-year comprehensive insurance, medical certificate, optional HSK and awards. Original/certified copies; paper plus named folder.

## 4. Deliverable

Maintain academic study; municipal award subject to allocated quotas. Linked Word form not read. No startup MVP/pitch.

## 5. China attendance

Physical enrolled study in Ningbo.

## 6. Benefits and fees

Current NingboTech notice does not state amount. Separate NBUFE official 2025 guide: city undergraduate RMB 20,000/year; language RMB 6,000/year. Do not transfer those host amounts to current NingboTech. Fee status: unknown.

## 7. Expenses and IP

No stacking monetary awards with host freshman/outstanding awards; higher amount only. False material/rule violations recover money; school transfer requires full refund. Applicant must already pay tuition/housing/insurance.

## 8. Edition and dates

2025–2026 study-year NingboTech host selection open. Deadline 12:00 October 15, 2026; timezone not explicitly stated. Current host award amount, exact age/gender maxima, provincial stacking and IP unresolved.

## 9. Remaining gaps
