## Eligibility

- Individuals and existing teams/projects accepted; primary age 18–30, older applicants expressly invited to enquire/apply. No technical background/entrepreneurial experience needed.

- Open to all, including international students studying in Singapore; no published citizenship or incorporation requirement. Company or sole-proprietor registration not required by visible form.

- In-person Singapore participation. No visa sponsorship or immigration advice; participants are not Tangent employees.

- Conflict: FAQ caps cohort team size at 3; application form explicitly caps in-person participants at 2. Use 2 for provisional planning pending organizer clarification.
