Note markdown# Tourism Administration Digital Transformation

Checked 12 October 2026.

## Edition and scope
2026 licensed travel-agency first e-commerce-system installation reimbursement; official page updated2026-04-09.

Decision: retain_conditional_three_distinct_claim_signatories

- **personnel minimum:** None
- **administrative signatories:** 3
- **basis:** Receipt/voucher instructions require handler, accountant and responsible person to be different people. This is a three-distinct-person administrative requirement, not proof of a three-founder or three-employee rule.

## Eligibility
- **company:** Licensed domestic travel agency first installing qualifying e-commerce management system in2026. This is a buyer/implementation subsidy, not a generic software-startup R&D grant.
- **individual:** No route for unregistered individual developer.
- **sole proprietor:** Eligibility depends on licensed travel-agency legal form; no independent sole-trader software route.

## Application and product requirements
- **submission:** Install first, then registered-post reimbursement application; first-come within budget.
- **MVP traction:** Actual installed system and expenditure/results evidence required; no investor deck or pitch video.

## Attendance and location
- **application:** Postal claim, no live pitch identified.
- **checks:** System/result verification and audit; remote-only guarantee not stated.

## Award and conditions
- **type:** Reimbursement of system installation and first-year service
- **currency:** TWD
- **rate:** 90%
- **maximum 5 or fewer staff:** 50000
- **maximum 6 or more staff:** 100000
- **equity:** No equity condition.

## Spend, matching, reporting and recovery
- **founder salary:** Not an eligible installation/service invoice category.
- **office rent:** Not an eligible category.
- **travel flights:** Not an eligible category.
- **computers equipment:** Hardware purchase not established as covered by this software implementation measure.
- **subscriptions API:** Qualifying system first-year service fee; generic developer/API subscriptions outside that approved system not established.
- **proof:** Original invoices, payment/receipt, system implementation and before/after screenshots, bank cover, budget/results documents.
- **clawbacks IP:** Fraud, duplicate claims and noncompliance can trigger recovery; application includes photo/publicity authorization.

## Deadlines
- **opened:** 2026-03-16
- **application close:** 2026-11-30 postmark; earlier exhaustion possible.
- **timezone:** Date-based postal deadline.
