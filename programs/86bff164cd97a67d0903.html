Note markdown# TSP Scale x Landing 2026

Checked: 2026-10-12T05:53:00Z

Edition: 2026 application closed August15; selection October15 and onboarding November1 planned.

Scope: Provisionally include; team-size uncertainty is not exclusion.

Team minimum: No minimum 3 established; workspace offers 1/2-person desks, which is NOT an eligibility guarantee.

## Eligibility and legal form
- **applicant:** Deep-tech startups/companies for Thailand landing or Thai international expansion; technology focus AI/life sciences/robotics. Thai deck targets market-ready products and NSTDA commercialization/incubation/public-support or overseas-expansion cases.
- **incorporation:** Foreign business registration assistance/private-office route; precise pre-application incorporation/sole-proprietor eligibility not settled.

## Application and product requirements
- **selection:** Technology focus, market potential and programme alignment; optional online interview.
- **MVP:** Thai deck targets market-ready technology products.
- **deck video:** Programme information decks are not evidence applicants must upload a deck/video.

## Attendance and location
- **duration:** Two years
- **interview:** Online if applicable
- **programme:** Thailand Science Park, Pathum Thani, with market exploration and international network activities; mandatory residency/travel days not established.

## Award and cost
- **type:** Subsidized workspace, technical-service access/discounts and market/network support; no universal cash prize.
- **rent support:** Thai deck: 75% subsidy year1 and 50% year2.
- **equity:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.

## Spending, reporting and legal conditions
- **salary:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.
- **rent:** Discount applies programme workspace, not arbitrary private rent. Deck lists domestic flexi/shared/team-space and foreign private office rates; table extraction ambiguous, so do not quote exact rents without visual verification.
- **travel:** International market exploration is a benefit; no verified flights/hotel/per-diem reimbursement.
- **computers:** Access to research equipment/technical services at special pricing, not cash to buy computers.
- **subscriptions:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.
- **API:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.
- **reimbursements:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.
- **matching:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.
- **reporting:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.
- **IP:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.
- **clawbacks:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.
- **deposit:** Deck states six months at full rent collected as deposit on registration; refund/forfeiture terms unknown.

## Dates
- **application:** July15–August15 2026; exact clock/timezone unstated.
- **screening:** August15–September1
- **interviews:** September1–October1
- **selection:** October15
- **onboarding:** November1
