Note markdown# Japan Foundation Japanese Studies Fellowship

Japanese studies research residency; natural sciences, engineering and industry skill training are excluded.

## Eligibility and participation

Overseas doctoral candidates or established Japanese studies researchers with host affiliation. Undergraduate/master's study and natural science, medicine or engineering projects excluded. Southeast Asia is routed to the related ASEAN category, grouped here.

Full-time research in Japan; start June 20, 2027–March 20, 2028. Long-term four to twelve months or short-term 21–89 days.

## Support and costs

2027 long-term researcher stipend JPY 400,000 per month; doctoral JPY 310,000 per month; short-term researcher JPY 17,000 per day. Category-specific airfare, insurance and allowances are conditional, not an aggregate cash prize.

Overlapping travel/accommodation fellowships cannot be retained concurrently. Fraud can trigger revocation and repayment with charges. Host and accommodation arrangements are the fellow's responsibility.

## Application and dates

Public guideline and completion requirements read; live portal fields were not inspected.

December 1, 2026: 13:00 JST for others; 23:59 EST for U.S. citizenship/permanent residence route, subject to the overseas-residency exception. No single timezone applied to all applicants.
