Note markdown# Chongqing Municipal Mayor International-Student Scholarship

Non-Chinese bachelor/master/doctoral applicants studying in Chongqing.

**Verified foreign route:** 非中国籍公民，身心健康。 — https://international.cqnu.edu.cn/info/1331/5219.htm

**Cycle:** 2026 CQNU call March 1–May 30 closed; 2027 call unannounced.

**Eligibility:** Bachelor high-school qualification and age<25; master bachelor and age<35; doctorate master and age<40; no other scholarship. Bachelor CSCA plus HSK4≥180; masterHSK5≥180; Chinese doctorateHSK5≥180; English math/biology doctorate IELTS6/TOEFL80 or English-medium proof/exemption.

**Application:** Scholarship/admission forms, valid passport, bachelor CSCA, notarized diploma/transcript, ≥1000-word plan, language proof, two graduate references, recent medical/no-criminal proofs, bank balance≥CNY25k equivalent, CV, relevant art/design works.

**Deliverable:** Degree study/research and admission requirements; no startup MVP or pitch-video requirement verified.

**Attendance:** CQNU degree study and competitive admission review, municipal education commission approval.

**Award:** CQNU2026: bachelor CNY25k/year, master30k, doctoral35k; support cannot exceed normal degree length.

**Costs and conditions:** Fixed educational award does not guarantee full actual costs. Bank balance is eligibility evidence, not a fee or award. Fee, travel, tax, disbursement and clawback details not stated.

**People:** 1 minimum / 1 maximum. Individual student applicant, not startup-team eligibility.

**Unresolved:** Actual private portal field labels/upload caps, fee (unless explicitly stated), final award contract and current next-call dates not fully verified.

**Distinct family:** Distinct regional authority, fund and application contract; host institutions and scholarship levels count once. Not a national CSC/CAS/NSFC host variant.
