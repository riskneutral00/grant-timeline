Note markdown# Jilin Provincial Government International-Student Scholarship

Non-Chinese master students; one provincial family, not separate host awards.

**Verified foreign route:** 申请人须为非中国籍公民，身体健康 — https://liuxue.ybu.edu.cn/jxj/jlszfjxjs.htm

**Cycle:** Undated current guide: June 15 annually; exact 2026/27 intake unverified.

**Eligibility:** Bachelor degree, age below 35 at application; HSK5 or corresponding Chinese proficiency; health and admission standards.

**Application:** Provincial form, notarized diploma/transcript, ≥800-word study/research plan, two professor/associate-professor references, relevant art/music portfolio, medical form, passport copies, photos and no-criminal proof; Chinese/English or notarized translations.

**Deliverable:** Degree study/research and admission requirements; no startup MVP or pitch-video requirement verified.

**Attendance:** Three-year Chinese-taught master in Yanbian, with physical recheck/registration.

**Award:** Yanbian: two funded years of tuition/twin-room waiver, CNY1,700/month living support and basic medical insurance; three-year master degree.

**Costs and conditions:** Remaining unfunded year fully self-paid. School explicitly charges no fees and recruits no scholarship agents. Failure to arrive loses scholarship; incomplete documents rejected and submitted files not returned. Tax/annual review details unverified.

**People:** 1 minimum / 1 maximum. Individual student applicant, not startup-team eligibility.

**Unresolved:** Actual private portal field labels/upload caps, fee (unless explicitly stated), final award contract and current next-call dates not fully verified.

**Distinct family:** Distinct regional authority, fund and application contract; host institutions and scholarship levels count once. Not a national CSC/CAS/NSFC host variant.
