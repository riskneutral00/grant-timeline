Foreign target: Overseas entrepreneurial teams or individuals of any nationality seeking Shenzhen market entry.

eligibility: Currently overseas and not already a shareholder or legal representative of a Shenzhen-registered entity. Own the project IP; no disputes. Prior Shenzhen competition prize recipients cannot re-enter. Additional professional qualifications apply to teams with Chinese overseas-study/work members. Individuals expressly allowed; age, gender and student limits not identified.

requirements: Innovation and business plan. Software, AI, digital content, advertising, streaming and games explicitly included. English pitch with Chinese/English slides: eight minutes plus seven minutes of questions. No universal video or MVP upload requirement established.

attendance: Overseas rounds and APEC online route form one competition. Shenzhen industry finals in late November and final in early December 2026, subject to updated notices. Invited advanced members' Shenzhen visit expenses covered; exact limits unknown. Filming and public showcase required.

award: CNY 9.42 million total prize pool. Final first/second/third: CNY 500,000/250,000/150,000 immediate prizes, plus conditional Shenzhen landing subsidies of 1,000,000/500,000/300,000. Preliminary awards: 100,000/50,000/30,000; industry awards: 150,000/100,000/50,000. CNY 5 billion investor pool is potential investment, not a guaranteed grant.

expense rules: Landing subsidies require a qualifying Shenzhen entity and local operation. Prizes paid to CNY/USD accounts. Other equity, loans and policy incentives need separate approval/contracts. Fees, travel caps and detailed clawback terms unknown.
