    Note markdown# Cloudflare for Startups

    Checked: 2026-10-12T05:53:00Z

    Edition: Current public programme checked 12 October 2026; not a promise of future availability.

    Scope: Provisionally include; team-size uncertainty is not exclusion.

    Team minimum: Unknown; no minimum of 3+ established.

    ## Eligibility and legal form
    - **applicant:** For-profit technology startup incorporated ≤10 years; public website/social profile and matching business email; first acceptance; up to Series B; page says funding within 12 months.
    - **tiers:** Bootstrapped/<$1m raised Tier3; affiliated partner plus <$5m Tier2; affiliated partner plus ≥$5m Tier1.
    - **ambiguity:** General recent-funding criterion conflicts with bootstrapped tier; bootstrapped applicants need branch-specific verification.
    - **excluded:** Existing Enterprise customers; education/personal blogs, consultancies/agencies/MSPs/resellers.

    ## Application and product requirements
    - **form:** Apply Here link verified to 404; no reliable complete field inventory. Account/payment method required if accepted.

    ## Attendance and location
    - **format:** Online; optional events/office hours.

    ## Award and cost
    - **Tier3:** US$10,000 one year
    - **Tier2:** US$100,000 one year
    - **Tier1:** US$350,000 one year
    - **expiry:** 12 months or consumed; no extensions. Upgrades discretionary and do not reset expiry.
    - **equity:** No equity exchange advertised; service credits, not cash.

    ## Spending, reporting and legal conditions
    - **salary:** Not cash; cannot pay salaries.
    - **rent:** Cannot pay physical rent.
    - **travel:** Cannot pay travel.
    - **computers:** Cannot buy computers.
    - **subscriptions:** Workers/platforms, Durable Objects, workflows, KV/D1/queues/vectorize, R2, images/stream and selected delivery; free security/network plan benefits separate.
    - **API:** Workers/platforms, Durable Objects, workflows, KV/D1/queues/vectorize, R2, images/stream and selected delivery; free security/network plan benefits separate.
    - **excluded spend:** AI Gateway, registrar purchase/renewal, enterprise add-ons by default, Magic Transit/Enterprise Spectrum/dedicated IP/China Network.
    - **reimbursements:** Service-billing credit, not cash reimbursement.
    - **matching:** No cash match established except stated co-pay/overage rules.
    - **reporting:** Unknown: not disclosed in the inspected public material; do not assume permission.
    - **IP:** Company-name/logo publicity licence.
    - **clawbacks:** Provider may terminate/change programme; nontransferable/noncash credits. Taxes applicant responsibility.
    - **caps:** R2 $10k; Workers AI Tier3 $2.5k, Tier2 $10k, Tier1 $50k.
    - **overages:** Valid payment method required; overages/expiry billed. Enterprise domains may revert to Free if not transitioned; do not assume all consumption stops.

    ## Dates
    - **application:** Rolling, advertised review up to 48h.
    - **expiry:** One year from confirmation.

    ## Public application fields
    No reliably inspectable field inventory recovered. This is an access gap, not evidence that the application has no questions.

    ## Unresolved and access limits
    - Current official Apply Here target 404. No field list recovered. Public marketing says 100% SLA while terms say no SLA; treat SLA as unresolved contractual conflict.

    ## Official evidence
    - [Cloudflare for Startups official programme page](https://www.cloudflare.com/startups/) — Current as inspected 2026-10-12; publication/update date not supplied
    - [Current page Apply Here target returns 404](https://www.cloudflare.com/lp/startups) — Observed 2026-10-12; publication/update date not supplied

    ## Planning caution
    No application, account creation or outreach was performed. Undisclosed conditions are not permission to spend. Headline credits, pools and investor access are not unrestricted cash. No future edition is assumed from a past edition.
