Note markdown# Guizhou Provincial International-Student Scholarship

**Verified foreign route:** 非中国籍公民，身体健康 — https://sie.gznu.edu.cn/info/1747/89255.htm

**Cycle:** 2026 guide published November 10, 2025; provincial application date deferred to admission notices; no exact next cutoff verified.

**Eligibility:** Bachelor: high-school diploma, excellent results, age ≤25. Master: bachelor, age ≤35; good conduct/no criminal record. Chinese table assigns provincial support to bachelor/master, whereas English prose implies doctorate/language: contradiction unresolved; do not assert those additional levels.

**Application:** International-student portal application and supporting materials; guide omits full provincial checklist. National CSC deadline and fields not transferred.

**Deliverable:** Degree study/research and admission requirements; no startup MVP or pitch-video requirement verified.

**Attendance:** Degree study in Guizhou; original application and admission documents at physical registration.

**Award:** 2026 guide: tuition support, free single/twin dormitory, monthly stipend and medical insurance; monetary rates not specified.

**Costs and conditions:** Normally no university/major/study-length changes. Unapproved registration delay over two weeks cancels scholarship. Funding standard, application fee, travel, tax and annual review detail unknown.

**People:** 1 minimum / 1 maximum. Individual student applicant, not startup-team eligibility.

**Unresolved:** Actual private portal field labels/upload caps, fee (unless explicitly stated), final award contract and current next-call dates not fully verified.

**Distinct family:** Distinct regional authority, fund and application contract; host institutions and scholarship levels count once. Not a national CSC/CAS/NSFC host variant.
