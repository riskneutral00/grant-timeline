Note markdown# Eureka Globalstars Taiwan 2026

Checked 12 October 2026. Public-source diligence; no application or account was submitted.

## Edition and scope

2026 call verified and closed. Expected project start April 2027; no 2027 application call verified. Taiwan download page currently links an August 2025 general manual; historical/manual evidence is separated below.

Team minimum: At least 2 independent for-profit companies across Taiwan and one participating economy; this is an ORGANISATION minimum, not a three-person founder minimum.

Scope: include

## Eligibility

- applicant: Taiwan company plus independent for-profit company in Austria, Finland, Sweden or Netherlands; Taiwan grant does not directly fund universities/research bodies (subcontract or self-fund allowed).

- individual_soleprop: Individual applicant cannot meet company consortium rule. General 2025 A+ manual permits sole proprietorships/partnerships, but the specific 2026 call says companies: sole-proprietor eligibility remains unresolved.

- nationality: Company establishment is specified; founder nationality/residency not separately stated.

- project: Innovative commercialisable R&D, technological risk and mutual benefit; civilian purpose; max 36 months; no single organisation or economy over 70% of eligible costs.

## Product and application requirements

- application: One English consortium application and partner forms, Gantt, signed co-signature; additional Taiwan proposal and national review. Eureka label alone does not guarantee funding.

- prototype_video: No universal MVP/traction threshold or video specified in call; feasibility, market, innovation and commercialisation assessed.

## Attendance and location

- submission: Eureka portal online, account required. National application separately required.

- review: Taiwan may require review meetings and supplementary information; remote permission and venue not specified.

- programme: No accelerator residence requirement established; international R&D collaboration is substantive.

## Award and commercial terms

- type: Restricted R&D grant through national agency, not Eureka prize.

- Taiwan: Up to 50% eligible project costs; amount dependent on industrial impact, qualification and budget; no Taiwan per-project fixed amount on call page.

- match: At least remaining eligible costs must be financed; foreign partners use own national rules.

- IP: Signed consortium agreement before start must settle ownership/use of know-how and IPR.

## Permissible spending and obligations

- founder_salary: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- rent: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- travel: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- computers: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- API_subscriptions: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- historical_manual: August 2025 A+ manual is still linked by official site, but category-by-category 2026 applicability must be confirmed. Do not import early-stage A+ rules into this separate programme.

- reporting: National contract and financial/technical review apply; full current Taiwan award contract not confirmed.

## Dates and deadlines

- Eureka: 20 April–15 September 2026, deadline 17:00 CEST (15:00 UTC).

- Taiwan: 2026 MOEA notice gives April 20–September 15. Eureka page still says national deadline TBA at 17:00 CST. Resolve national cutoff conflict rather than assume identical time.

- future: National procedures December 2026; expected start April 2027.
