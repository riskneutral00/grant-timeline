What you would do

  • Expense rules: Applicant finances the remainder and may need cash flow before reimbursement. Dedicated bank account, original vouchers and return of interest/unspent funds. R&D payroll generally <=60% of total budget; future hires <=30% of research person-months. R&D materials, equipment/software use and maintenance, technology/contract research and patent costs can qualify; generic equipment purchase is not automatically eligible. Reporting lateness incurs daily 0.1% of subsidy penalty.
  • Requirements: Single-company route. Joint route requires two companies, not two people. Small firms below five employees have an insurance-evidence alternative; no numeric R&D personnel minimum verified. New innovative R&D, with accountable milestones. No mandatory application video or pre-existing MVP rule identified.
