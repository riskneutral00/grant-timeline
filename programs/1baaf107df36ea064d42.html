Note markdown# Taichung City Local SBIR

2026 intake closed; next cycle unannounced. This is a matched R&D grant with a TWD 1 million individual ceiling, rather than unrestricted prize money.

Civilian AI, ICT/security software and innovative service systems are eligible themes. Worth considering for new R&D if the company and execution location qualify; existing completed software alone does not establish a fundable project.

**Eligibility and people.** Domestic SME registered in Taichung; funded work must take place in Taichung. SME paid capital up to TWD100m OR fewer than 200 employees. Branches and PRC-invested applicants excluded. Duplicate government funding, arrears and serious violations disqualify. Consecutive awards in 2024 and 2025 exclude a new 2026 award. Existing city projects need adequate additional research and finance capacity. Single-company route. Joint route requires two companies, not two people. Small firms below five employees have an insurance-evidence alternative; no numeric R&D personnel minimum verified. New innovative R&D, with accountable milestones. No mandatory application video or pre-existing MVP rule identified.

**Application.** Company name, founding date, ID, paid capital, responsible-person identity/date of birth, registered and contact addresses, employee count and factory status. Project title, PI/contact/accountant telephone and email, dates and budget; industry, youth/new-company and policy-priority checkboxes. Innovation/public summary, keywords, R&D method, checkpoints, staff CVs and budget; registration, tax, insurance, consent, undertakings, funding disclosures and conditional joint agreements. Paper originals and USB/PDF package required.

**Award and expenses.** Individual cap TWD1,000,000 and at most 50% of eligible project costs; optional joint route TWD2,000,000 total, TWD1,000,000 per company. Tariff-impact bonus remains inside the individual cap. Nine months, 2026-09-01 to 2027-05-31. 50% after contract, balance against approved closing costs. No equity purchase identified. Applicant finances the remainder and may need cash flow before reimbursement. Dedicated bank account, original vouchers and return of interest/unspent funds. R&D payroll generally <=60% of total budget; future hires <=30% of research person-months. R&D materials, equipment/software use and maintenance, technology/contract research and patent costs can qualify; generic equipment purchase is not automatically eligible. Reporting lateness incurs daily 0.1% of subsidy penalty.

**Attendance.** PI or formal project R&D staff must present at review; closure presentation and inspection obligations apply. Progress and financial reports, on-site checks and three years of follow-up add work beyond filing.

**Historical deadline.** 2026-06-26 at 12:00:00; timezone not verified.

**Remaining questions.** Execution rules restrict foreign use but the printed clause omits its duration; clarify before funding globally distributed software. Application fee not explicitly established; no equity provision found. Detailed cost treatment remains subject to approved contract. Clock is published but timezone is not stated; no inferred conversion. No 2027 call verified.

**Evidence coverage.** Read 2026 guidelines, proposal fields and execution provisions. The national map was a discovery aid; current city sources determine the historical deadline.
