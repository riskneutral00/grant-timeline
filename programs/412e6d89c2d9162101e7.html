Note markdown# CITD digital transformation

Checked 12 October 2026.

## Edition and scope
2026 Product Development CITD guide, approved December2025; recorded “digital transformation” name is too broad.

Decision: retain_for_diligence

- **personnel minimum:** None
- **company count:** 1
- **separate consortium:** R&D alliance requires at least three enterprises; exclude that route from small-team recommendation without inferring personnel count.

## Eligibility
- **company:** Domestic manufacturing with factory registration/exemption, or qualifying technical-service business including information services. Positive equity; no rejected banking status, branches/mainland investment excluded.
- **individual:** Registered enterprise required.
- **sole proprietor:** Registered business permitted in guide.
- **project:** New product beyond domestic peer capability; not completed or already mass-produced.

## Application and product requirements
- **submission:** Online工商憑證 authentication; prescribed plan narrative generally <=20pages, eligibility documents.
- **video:** No mandatory video found.
- **traction:** Technical feasibility and product-market evidence, not arbitrary revenue threshold.

## Attendance and location
- **application:** Online.
- **review:** Company representative/PI technical review; one to five attendees per company; identity and employment proof. Alliance members must each attend. Venue/remote mode notified; no blanket remote guarantee.
- **execution:** Site inspection, checkpoints and final review.

## Award and conditions
- **type:** Matched product-development R&D grant
- **currency:** TWD
- **single company maximum:** 2000000
- **alliance maximum:** 10000000
- **equity:** No equity award.
- **security and payment:** Option1: guarantee/security20% of grant to receive at least50% after signing, balance after final approval. Option2: full-grant guarantee for100% advance. Bank guarantee valid until three months after project end; disbursement ratios subject to annual authority adjustments.

## Spend, matching, reporting and recovery
- **founder salary:** Actual approved R&D personnel compensation; founder status alone not enough.
- **office rent:** Ordinary office rent not established.
- **travel flights:** Necessary domestic R&D/technology-transfer travel; documented domestic flights possible. No general overseas entitlement.
- **computers equipment:** Research equipment/software usage/depreciation and maintenance, not general capital purchase.
- **subscriptions API:** Software recognized but SaaS/API treatment not explicitly resolved.
- **matching:** <=50% grant; self-funding ordinarily no more than capital absent financial plan.
- **reporting IP:** Progress/financial reviews and recovery for duplicate, false or ineligible claims. Government R&D IP framework applies; organiser publications of outputs have separate rights.

## Deadlines
- **application close:** 2026-02-09 17:30:59 in announcement; guide says17:30 and contains an inconsistent18:00 reference. Use stricter official announced cutoff.
- **project period:** 2026-02-10 through2026-11-30
- **timezone:** Not explicitly printed in reviewed deadline.
