Note markdown# Vulcanus in Japan

EU/associated-country engineering and STEM student traineeship in Japan; 2026–27 call closed.

## Eligibility and participation

Eligible EU/Single Market Programme SME-pillar citizenship and university enrolment there; fourth official study year through final PhD year. Enrolled until at least late June 2026, fluent English and able to devote eight months. Computer science explicitly included.

August 2026–March 2027: two-month intensive Japanese language course and six-month Japanese-company traineeship; full participation required.

## Support and costs

2026–27 base EUR 8,200 for compulsory programme, including insurance; optional Eyes on Japan group assignment adds EUR 1,000. Host housing and Japanese training provided. Insurance arranged by centre deducted from grant, not extra cash.

Published grant covers programme/insurance; amount is not extra salary. Exact airfare/payment schedule, personal costs, IP and withdrawal conditions need current agreement; no startup equity promise.

## Application and dates

Word form plus webform, motivation and tutor recommendation/academic record considered; complete supporting-document instructions and actual fields not inspected.

Deadline January 20, 2026, 23:00 CET; next call unknown. 2026–27 organization was expressly conditional on EU grant award/signature and could adjust or cancel.
