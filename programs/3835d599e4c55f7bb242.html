What you would do

  • Expense rules: Entry fee not separately established. Tuition-only support leaves own travel/living. Self-booked lodging not reimbursed; home-country transport, extra baggage and personal costs excluded. Insurance exclusions apply. Violations or insufficient attendance can revoke funding.
  • Requirements: Full-time training, at least90% attendance, country report before the course and presentation. Lectures, field visits and workshops. Twenty2026 courses, including AI/cybersecurity/digital MSME training, grouped once.
