Taipei t.Hub Government-Sponsored Coworking Residency

Taipei Department of Economic Development / t.Hub

What to know

Taipei sponsored coworking accepts individuals and teams up to four. Rent is waived, with a refundable TWD5,000 deposit and extra-service charges. Rolling through December 31, 2026, subject to vacancies.
