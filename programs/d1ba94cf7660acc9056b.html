Note markdown# Anhui Provincial Government Scholarship for International Students

Anhui province-funded international study awards, with a 2026 AHPU master's route in systems/data/AI-related research.

## 1. Foreign recruitment and value

Official provincial explanatory PDF: “Applicants must be non-Chinese Citizens with foreign passports”. Foreign technical study support; costs and delayed competitive award weaken immediate founder usefulness.

## 2. Eligibility and people

2026 AHPU admission age at least 18, bachelor's, GPA at least 3.0 or average 70; English/HSK proof and no accompanying family. Standing older provincial PDF has under-28 undergraduate, under-40 master's, under-45 doctorate limits; current host applicability needs confirmation. No gender-based restriction found in the inspected public rules. Exact nationality, admission/host and age requirements are edition-specific as described above; no owner eligibility inferred. Individual applicant/recipient route published.

## 3. Application fields and files

AHPU application, diploma/transcript, 800-word study/research plan, two senior references, physical exam, passport, language proof, photo, publications, criminal-record evidence and RMB 100,000-equivalent bank proof. Host also requests family-registration evidence; no personal material accessed.

## 4. Deliverable

Three-year master's study; 2026 systems science includes big data/intelligent systems and control includes pattern recognition. Merit-based provincial award in years two/three, not guaranteed at admission.

## 5. China attendance

AHPU physical reporting October 12–16, 2026; qualifications and academic conduct maintained.

## 6. Benefits and fees

AHPU 2026 description: provincial RMB 30,000/year for about 10% of later-year students. First-year RMB 20,000 award is university funding, not added to provincial award. Fee status: required.

## 7. Expenses and IP

Host admission fee RMB 400 nonrefundable; tuition RMB 20,000/year, dorm RMB 3,500, insurance RMB 800; RMB 3,500 nonrefundable deposit applied to first-year housing. Other study/living/medical/residence costs remain.

## 8. Edition and dates

2026 AHPU admission closed; provincial award after first study year. Older provincial PDF states RMB 10,000–20,000 and different age caps; do not present those as 2026 universal terms. Exact provincial later-year intake and current province-wide contract unresolved. AHPU admission says before July 1, 2026; history retains that boundary and does not assert inclusive July 1 availability.

## 9. Remaining gaps

Older provincial PDF states RMB 10,000–20,000 and different age caps; do not present those as 2026 universal terms. Exact provincial later-year intake and current province-wide contract unresolved.
