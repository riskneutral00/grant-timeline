## 1. Problem and deliverable

Restricted learning and partner validation opportunity; not direct unrestricted startup funding. Known minimum three must stay visible.

## 2. Eligibility and people

Student limit: 3–5 students; advisers are additional (maximum not established). Advisers optional; organizer allows more than one and no numerical adviser maximum was verified. Record team_max is null because the student ceiling is not a verified whole-team ceiling.

Domestic/foreign university undergraduates or graduates, nationality unrestricted; enrolled at April 11 opening or admitted to graduate school. No separate age/gender/company restriction found. 3–5 students; advisor optional.

## 3. Application fields and files

Names, school/department/year/contact, photos, achievements, team skills/division/name/motivation and partner preferences. Bonder registration requires account; not entered.
