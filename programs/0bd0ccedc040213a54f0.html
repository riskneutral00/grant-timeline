Note markdown# Tokyo Overseas Financial Corporation Establishment and Support Subsidy

Restricted reimbursement for foreign FinTech or asset-management companies establishing Tokyo operations.

## Eligibility and participation

Foreign-law corporation in asset management or FinTech; prior BDCT/Tokyo consultation and approved establishment required. Continue operations for two fiscal years after establishment year.

Actual Tokyo business base and approved registrations/filings required.

## Support and costs

FY2026 first year: half eligible expenses, cap JPY 12.5 million for licensed asset managers or JPY 7.5 million for other asset managers/FinTech. Second/third-year category caps JPY 20 million or JPY 15 million; no automatic sum or universal award.

Half eligible expenses only: office, specialist consulting, fixtures and recruitment; licensed firms may claim additional approved compliance/system categories. Names and subsidy information may be disclosed. Tax/refund details require full guideline.

## Application and dates

Exact live form fields and mandatory uploads have not been verified.

In principle consultation/application by December 25, 2026; business establishment and expense report March 31, 2027. No exact clock verified.
