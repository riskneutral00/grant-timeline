What this event is for

A free one-day collaborative lab developing practical ways for cities to support healthier lives.

Theme and focus

Urban health, prevention and healthy longevity; potential challenges include air/water quality, nature/biodiversity, public spaces, mobility, accessibility and healthy ageing.

What you would do

Selected participants join multidisciplinary teams and develop practical ideas with real-world potential.
