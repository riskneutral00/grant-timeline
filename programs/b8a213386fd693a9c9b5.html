Note markdown# U-start Innovation and Entrepreneurship Program

Checked 12 October 2026. Public-source diligence; no application or account was submitted.

## Edition and scope

Official ROC115/2026 programme package inspected. Publication December 2025 and cover November 2025 are preparation dates for 2026, not a historical 2025 call. Stage 2 runs into 2027.

Team minimum: At least 3 people; newly discovered exclusion under requested minimum-three-plus rule.

Scope: excluded_minimum_3_plus

## Eligibility

- team: At least 3; at least two-thirds, rounded up, Taiwan-national college students (including qualifying fourth-year junior-college students) or graduates in academic years 110–114 first semester. Other members generally ages 18–35, including qualifying foreign residents/visa holders.

- leader: Taiwan student/recent graduate, full time without outside job, at least 18 by funding; becomes prepared-business/company head.

- exclusions: Any member previously receiving U-start support excluded; members cannot be heads of other companies during application/award as defined in guide.

- application_route: Through participating university incubator, up to 15 projects/school; not an unaffiliated solo direct application.

## Product and application requirements

- application: School/team operating and mentoring plans, member eligibility proofs and consents, detailed budget. Incubation contract and business preparatory office/entity steps before funding.

- stage2: Only stage-1 winners may compete; continuation not automatic.

## Attendance and location

- programme: Stage 1 six months with at least 2 mentoring meetings monthly, school supervision and monthly original receipts. Stage-2 reviews/operations follow separate schedule.

- remote: No blanket fully remote entitlement; school incubation participation and review obligations.

## Award and commercial terms

- stage1: TWD500,000 total: TWD350,000 team business expenses and TWD150,000 school incubation support. Not TWD500,000 unrestricted founder cash.

- stage2: TWD350,000–1m for selected continuing teams, not automatic.

- match: Team self-funding required; universal numeric matching ratio not established from reviewed passages.

## Permissible spending and obligations

- founder_salary: Eligible team personnel costs capped at 70% of team subsidy, subject to documented duties and school handling; not arbitrary owner draw.

- rent: Approved business rent may qualify.

- travel: Approved domestic travel eligible; foreign travel excluded.

- computers: Durable equipment and hardware repairs excluded.

- API_subscriptions: Information-service costs appear in business expense categories; exact API subscription eligibility still requires approved budget.

- other: Registration/bookkeeping/materials/advertising may qualify; bonuses, entertainment/public-relations hospitality excluded. School grant covers incubation, no extra administration fee during six-month period; other charges by agreement.

- reporting_clawback: Monthly original receipts, school oversight, audits and return of unused funds; violations may recover funding. Full IP clauses not exhaustively transcribed because programme is excluded.

## Dates and deadlines

- stage1: 2026-02-25 17:00 deadline; May 1–October 31 2026 execution. Taiwan local-time interpretation.

- stage2: Separate August application for existing stage-1 teams; November 1 2026–October 31 2027 execution. Not an open 2027 solo call.
