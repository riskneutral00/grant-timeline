Note markdown# Jinan International Sister-City Student Scholarship

Foreign sister-city students recommended by local government international-affairs office.

**Verified foreign route:** 申请者须非中国籍公民，身心健康 — https://isao.ujn.edu.cn/jxjxm1/jnsgjycjxj.htm

**Cycle:** Current undated programme guidance; no current intake deadline verified.

**Eligibility:** Degree age18–40/non-degree18–60, high-school or higher education, sister-city route and government recommendation. Chinese HSK4≥180; English native/official language, prior English medium or IELTS6/TOEFL70/Duolingo100/EF60.

**Application:** Government recommendation; online admission form/passport/certified diploma/medical/no-criminal/work-history proofs; bachelor transcript/language/CSCA; China transfer/residence documents where applicable.

**Deliverable:** Degree study/research and admission requirements; no startup MVP or pitch-video requirement verified.

**Attendance:** Jinan physical language/degree study and original-document review, arrival residence formalities.

**Award:** Bachelor and one-semester/one-year Chinese-language study: tuition, housing and comprehensive insurance coverage; no living stipend stated.

**Costs and conditions:** Tuition/accommodation/insurance service coverage; not cash award, startup investment or a general visa route. Uncovered living/travel/procedure costs self-funded unless further contract says otherwise; fee/tax unknown.

**People:** 1 minimum / 1 maximum. Individual student applicant, not startup-team eligibility.

**Unresolved:** Actual private portal field labels/upload caps, fee (unless explicitly stated), final award contract and current next-call dates not fully verified.

**Distinct family:** Independent municipal sister-city fund and government nomination gate, distinct from Shandong provincial fund. Degree/language lengths are not separate programmes.
