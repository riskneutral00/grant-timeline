What this event is for

A global pitching competition for AI-native startups seeking investment and exposure to investors. The potential investment is discretionary and subject to due diligence, rather than a guaranteed grant.

Theme and focus

AI-native pre-seed and seed startups.

What you would do

Pitch through a planned Bangkok city round, Southeast Asia regional round, semifinals and grand finale. The city is still recruiting a host.
