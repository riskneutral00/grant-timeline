### Requirements

### Attendance

Most regional rounds virtual, some local in-person; semifinals streamed. Homepage proposes Silicon Valley finale; exact venue/date unconfirmed. Intake lets applicant prefer virtual or in-person, but does not guarantee remote participation through finale. [Source](https://aibuilderscup.com/)

- payment: No fixed equity percentage, payment date, matching contribution, milestone schedule, reporting, clawback or startup-IP clause located. Private investment agreement required before treating any of these as known. [Source](https://aibuilderscup.com/)
