Note markdown# MYHackathon

Checked: 2026-10-12T05:52:00Z

Edition: Current site marked MYH 2026 maintenance, but accessible detailed pages/portal remain 2024. No verified open 2026/2027 intake.

Scope: include; minimum team: 2.

## Eligibility

- Published programme/current FAQ: 2–5 members, all Malaysian citizens, all at least 18. Team leader registers first; members’ details at solution submission.

- No verified current entity/sole-proprietor requirement at award stage. Do not assume individuals can receive conditional grant without later business formation.

## Application field inventory

- Team-leader registration: Required initial stage; exact fields unavailable in closed portal. [Evidence](https://www.myhackathon.gov.my/faq/)

- All team-member names/details: Required with solution; form schema not exposed. [Evidence](https://www.myhackathon.gov.my/faq/)

- Solution submission: Required through leader’s portal Submission section; exact narrative/upload fields inaccessible. [Evidence](https://www.myhackathon.gov.my/faq/)

## Deliverables and requirements

- Practical prototype responding to government-agency problem statement; current programme page mentions seven problems. Detailed problem documents/2026 form unverified.

- Historical participant-kit link is public but Drive folder did not render through retrieval. Website terms concern access/service disclaimers, not detailed grant spending or recipient obligations.

## Attendance

- 2024 historical: Launch hybrid; mentoring days 2–7 online, one leader portal login; final pitch physically required of leader in Klang Valley (venue then TBC).

- Current: FAQ says hybrid. Do not represent 2024 detailed attendance requirements as confirmed new-edition policy.

- Travel: No verified airfare/hotel reimbursement.

## Award and payment

- Advertised: MYR 250,000 conditional grant per winning team plus 12 months pilot implementation coaching, from current programme/FAQ.

- Type: Conditional project grant, not unrestricted contest cash or a promised investment.

- Edition warning: Amount also appears on past-winner material; current intake/contract not verified.

- Equity payment: Equity, tranches, matching and grant agreement not recovered.

## Expenditure and obligations
