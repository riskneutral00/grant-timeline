## Edition and scope
2026 call closed; expenses and claim period still relevant. No 2027 edition verified.

Decision: retain_for_diligence

- **personnel minimum:** None
- **company count:** 1

## Eligibility
- **company:** Kaohsiung company/business or qualifying tax-registered small business; established 2023-02-23 through 2026-02-22; capital <=NT$10m and prior-year revenue <=NT$5m (proration rules apply).
- **individual:** Responsible person must be Taiwan citizen, Kaohsiung household registered, age18–45, with ten hours entrepreneurship training; not an unregistered individual grant.
- **sole proprietor:** Business/tax-registered small enterprise allowed under rules.
- **restrictions:** No special-entertainment business; no prior Youth Bureau startup grant; choose general or competitive grant; same responsible person treated as same applicant; representative change after announcement not allowed.

## Application and product requirements
- **submission:** Digital Kaohsiung service form and scanned stamped attachments; application form now unavailable/closed.
- **training:** Ten hours qualifying entrepreneurship training, online classes permitted.
- **traction:** Existing legal operation and revenue/tax evidence; no MVP/video/deck requirement found beyond prescribed plan.

## Attendance and location
- **application:** Online.
- **inspection:** On-site business verification before approval; subsequent checks, results presentations and promotion cooperation.
- **location:** Kaohsiung business and household-registration conditions must be maintained during award period.

## Award and conditions
- **type:** Expense reimbursement
- **currency:** TWD
- **maximum:** 80000
- **rate:** Up to70% eligible actual costs, within approved amount; not an unconditional cash prize.
- **equity:** No equity term found.

## Spend, matching, reporting and recovery
- **founder salary:** Not in the eligible categories.
- **office rent:** Business-premises rent eligible up toNT$20,000/month; related-family landlord exclusions apply.
- **travel flights:** Not in listed eligible categories.
- **computers equipment:** New directly business-related equipment with unit price >=NT$10,000; administration/decor/consumables excluded. A computer is not automatically eligible.
- **subscriptions API:** Not expressly included; listed marketing website/SEO/advertisement expenses are narrower than general software subscription.
- **other:** Specified advertising, SEO, blogger promotion, sign, website and video marketing costs.
- **proof:** Invoices, payment proof, equipment photographs, rent/lease evidence, ad screenshots and analytics, account details.
- **clawbacks:** False or duplicate claims, ineligible spend or refusal of checks may lead to recovery and exclusion. Changes of business, representative or household location/cancellation during award period must be reported within seven days; two-year bar possible.

## Deadlines
- **application open:** 2026-03-18
- **application close:** 2026-04-15; clock/timezone not expressly confirmed
- **eligible cost period:** 2026-06-01 through 2026-09-30
- **claim deadline:** 2026-11-15
