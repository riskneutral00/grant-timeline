What this event is for

A small operating-cost subsidy to help newly established youth businesses in Kaohsiung survive and improve marketing.

Theme and focus

Local entrepreneurship across industries, excluding special entertainment businesses.

What you would do

Propose eligible rent, productive equipment or marketing costs, then document spending for reimbursement.
