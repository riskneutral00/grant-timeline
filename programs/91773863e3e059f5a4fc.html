Note markdown# Hangzhou Maker World Overseas Talent Entrepreneurship Competition

Foreign-targeted Hangzhou startup competition with AI software routes, cash prizes and conditional local business grants. Concept products are allowed.

## 1. Foreign recruitment and value

Official eligibility explicitly includes “非华裔外国人” (non-ethnic Chinese foreign nationals). Strong conditional civilian AI/software founder value; substantial qualification and landing obligations.

## 2. Eligibility and people

Overseas master's or higher, overseas innovation/entrepreneurship experience, born after January 1, 1976; return/arrival after January 1, 2022 if already in China. Earlier Hangzhou shareholding, legal-representative roles or social insurance and two previous entries exclude the applicant. No gender-based restriction found in the inspected public rules. Exact nationality, admission/host and age requirements are edition-specific as described above; no owner eligibility inferred. Individual applicant/recipient route published.

## 3. Application fields and files

Online registration and business-plan PPT/supporting files: technology, team, business model, strategy and funding. Public signed-in field layout not inspected.

## 4. Deliverable

Principal project owner personally presents. Legal IP or authorized use; one project/track. Up to three nominated core members; whole-team maximum ambiguous.

## 5. China attendance

Online semifinal, required matchmaking event, Hangzhou in-person finals. Absence at any stage withdraws entry.

## 6. Benefits and fees

Main-track 2026 cash RMB 200,000/100,000/50,000; main-final RMB 20,000; semifinal/new-sprout RMB 10,000, noncumulative. These amounts are not verified foreign special-track cash awards. Conditional landing grants RMB 200,000–5 million require Hangzhou entity registration and disbursement approval; foreign special-track benefit schedule remains unverified. Fee status: none.

## 7. Expenses and IP

No participation fee. False information/IP violations can recover prizes. No general participation-cost compensation promised; materials shared with partners and roadshows may be public. Grant matching and restricted-use details not verified.

## 8. Edition and dates

2026 rules published; intake deadline not verified. Deadline, grant disbursement contract, tax and travel coverage unverified. Foreign special-track award schedule separate; do not reuse main-track prizes as its direct promise.

## 9. Remaining gaps

Deadline, grant disbursement contract, tax and travel coverage unverified. Foreign special-track award schedule separate; do not reuse main-track prizes as its direct promise.
