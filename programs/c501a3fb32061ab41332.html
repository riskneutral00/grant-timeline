## Eligibility
- **company:** Pingtung domestic SME company, limited partnership or business; capital<=NT$100m OR fewer than200 employees; relevant factory registration/exemption. No branches/mainland capital or duplicate grants.
- **sole proprietor:** Sole proprietorship and partnership explicitly accepted.
- **individual:** Unregistered person ineligible.
- **consortium:** Two eligible enterprises, different responsible persons who are not spouses or first-degree direct relatives; each cofunds; withdrawal of either terminates joint project.
- **bonuses:** Young founder18–45, company<=8years, first application, civilian digital/SDGs/local culture and rapid commercialization can score extra, not universal entry requirements.

## Application and product requirements
- **submission:** Three paper copies plus electronic Word/PDF and attachments, postal/hand-delivery AND Google Form upload followed by telephone receipt confirmation.
- **duration:** Six to twelve months; approved extension at most two months. Form preset2026-08-01 start is template, contract approval governs.
- **video:** No initial mandatory video found.

## Attendance and location
- **application:** Paper to National Pingtung University Pingshang campus research shared office, administration building7F,51Minsheng East Road,Pingtung; digital upload also.
- **review:** In-person presentation, full-time employee preferably PI. Maximum three attendees/company, at most one outsourced representative; arrive30min early.
- **execution:** On-site audits and presentation, outcomes exhibitions and follow-up. No blanket remote option.

## Award and conditions
- **type:** Matched county R&D subsidy
- **currency:** TWD
- **single company maximum:** 1000000
- **two company total maximum:** 2000000
- **rate:** <=50%, each company<=NT$1m
- **payment:** 50% at commencement, balance after final report and expenditure approval.
- **equity:** No equity purchase.

## Spend, matching, reporting and recovery
- **founder salary:** Formal eligible R&D payroll and effort; personnel normally<=60% total. Founder eligibility depends on actual R&D/payroll, not ownership.
- **office rent:** Not in the listed eligible categories.
- **travel flights:** No general travel category in this programme accounting schedule.
- **computers equipment:** Approved equipment usage/depreciation and maintenance; caps and property-list evidence; operational rental can fall under outsourcing. Not blanket computer purchase.
- **subscriptions API:** Technology/IP acquisition includes software/database; recurring SaaS/API eligibility not explicitly resolved.
- **categories:** R&D personnel, materials, equipment use/maintenance, technology transfer and contract research, qualified patent application expenses.
- **matching:** <=50% grant; self funds ordinarily below capital or financial plan.
- **reporting:** Dedicated account/ledger, paid original vouchers in project dates; all participating-person data consents; project results and follow-up. Midterm/final work and accounting reports, mandatory accountant certification for final claim; audit fee self-funded, cannot be project cost. Three-year outcome tracking. Weekly work records may be required to remedy delayed progress.
- **clawbacks:** Unused funds/interest returned; false or duplicate grant/noncompliance may terminate and recover full grant plus interest.
- **IP:** Research/IP belongs to applicant, but results generally may not be produced/used outside Taiwan jurisdiction for two years from generation without the prescribed approval exception.
