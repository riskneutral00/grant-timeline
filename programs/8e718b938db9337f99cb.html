Note markdown# INNOPAD TAIPEI × Mighty Net Startup Accelerator Grant Program

Checked 12 October 2026. Public-source diligence; no application or account was submitted.

## Edition and scope

Official July 20 2026 recruitment announcement verified; no dated deadline or 2027 edition established.

Team minimum: Not specified; do not interpret as solo approval.

Scope: include

## Eligibility

- entity: Domestic/international startup established less than 8 years at joining. Selected teams must be Taipei-registered or commit to Taipei company registration within 6 months.

- stage: Preference for pre/post-angel growth teams that completed PoC and are preparing PoB.

- focus: Smart living, agriculture, logistics or healthcare.

- individual_soleprop: Company-oriented recruitment; unincorporated/sole-proprietor route and nationality/residency not expressly determined.

## Product and application requirements

- application: Public announcement provides organiser contact but no readable application template/form link.

- product: Industrial application potential and PoC-to-PoB focus; no public required video/deck lengths or revenue threshold found.

## Attendance and location

- location: INNOPAD Taipei, Nangang Depot Social Housing floors 3–4; factory and city PoC opportunities.

- residency: May apply for base seats; registration address usable after 3 months residence from contract signature. Attendance minimum and remote permission unresolved.

## Award and commercial terms

- type: Verified support is in-kind space, PoC access, mentoring, matchmaking and exchanges. Despite English “Grant Program” title, no cash grant amount established.

- space: Limited first-year free seats allocated by need; additional paid seats possible.

- investment: Investor matchmaking/Demo Days do not promise investment.

## Permissible spending and obligations

- founder_salary: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- rent: Limited base seating support only; no housing/office reimbursement promise.

- travel: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- computers: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- API_subscriptions: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- IP_reporting_clawback: Contract and PoC conditions not exposed; not unrestricted funding.

## Dates and deadlines

- application: No deadline in reviewed announcement; availability not guaranteed by old recruitment title.

- registration: Taipei registration within 6 months of joining; address application after 3 months residence.

## Public application checklist

- Application form fields — No accessible public application form in reviewed announcement; cannot enumerate required/optional fields. ([source](https://www.might.com.tw/en/news/mightynetxinnopadtaipei-program-1th-en/))

## Unresolved / access limitations

- Public announcement is not a complete application kit.

- Cash amount, fees, equity, team size, attendance, application cutoff and PoC/IP agreement remain unresolved.
