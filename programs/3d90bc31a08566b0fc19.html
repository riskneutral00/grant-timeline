What this event is for

A pitching and market-entry initiative helping technology startups meet investors, corporates and customers while expanding into Asia through Hong Kong.

Theme and focus

Fintech/insurtech; financial-services AI; green technology; blockchain/digital assets; health/life sciences; AI/robotics; property/transport; consumer/hospitality/creative industries.

What you would do

Pitch through qualifier rounds and, if selected, demonstrate products and compete in live semifinals and the grand finale during Hong Kong FinTech Week x StartmeupHK.
