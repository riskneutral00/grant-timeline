## Edition and scope
2026 competition application closed; shortlist/final/ceremony forthcoming as of2026-10-12.

Decision: retain_for_diligence

- **personnel minimum:** 1
- **personnel maximum:** 6
- **evidence:** Rules explicitly allow one to six participants; at least one Taiwan national.

## Eligibility
- **individual:** Social track permits individuals, startups and companies.
- **company:** No universal incorporation prerequisite for social track.
- **sole proprietor:** May enter as person/team; separate company proof depends on entered identity.
- **nationality:** At least one team member must be Taiwan citizen.
- **student:** Separate campus route needs student identification; up to two advisors; minors need guardian consent.
- **restrictions:** Prior2022–25 winning work cannot be reused; no pre-existing CHT commercial collaboration on entered work by final2026-10-31; own IP/licences required. Multiple entries allowed but highest final award rule applies.

## Application and product requirements
- **proposal:** Word/PDF A4<=20MB, recommended15pages excluding cover/contents/references, font>=12pt; seven required sections.
- **video:** Optional initial video.
- **final:** Final consent and pitch deck mandatory;12-minute pitch/live demo plus award exhibition.

## Attendance and location
- **application:** Online member account; email/password login hides submission controls.
- **final:** Physical final2026-10-31; Banqiao training academy tentative, another schedule labels venueTBA; verify organiser notice.
- **ceremony:** 2026-11-26 tentative, exhibition/award participation required per rules.
- **remote:** No remote alternative found.

## Award and conditions
- **type:** Gross cash prizes, not credits or equity investment
- **currency:** TWD
- **social main prizes:** [250000, 200000, 150000, 100000]
- **social finalists:** Ten finalistsNT$10,000 each; award stacking governed by rules.
- **special:** TwoNT$50,000 special prizes.
- **campus separate:** NT$200k/150k/100k plus threeNT$50k and tenNT$10k finalist prizes.
- **tax:** 10% withholding at qualifyingNT$20,000+ threshold stated; actual residency tax obligations require checking.
- **payment:** Typically one to two months after ceremony.
- **equity:** No equity condition in competition rules.

## Spend, matching, reporting and recovery
- **founder salary:** Not established in the public material reviewed; do not assume permitted or prohibited.
- **office rent:** Not established in the public material reviewed; do not assume permitted or prohibited.
- **travel flights:** Attendance costs/reimbursement not specified.
- **computers equipment:** Not established in the public material reviewed; do not assume permitted or prohibited.
- **subscriptions API:** Not established in the public material reviewed; do not assume permitted or prohibited.
- **spend:** Rules reviewed do not state permitted uses for salary/rent/flights/computers/API. Absence of a budget schedule is not certification of unrestricted use.
- **matching:** No financial match stated.
- **IP:** Creator retains IP; publicity/event records licence granted; CHT priority for commercial negotiation, not automatic ownership transfer.
- **clawbacks:** Fraud, plagiarism, ineligibility and rule breaches can revoke award and require return.
