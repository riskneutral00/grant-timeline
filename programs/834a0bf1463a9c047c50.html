    Note markdown# VentureSpark Cohort 2 2026

    Checked: 2026-10-12T05:53:00Z

    Edition: Cohort 2 2026 completed August 28. No 2027 call established.

    Scope: Include for two-person teams; a sole founder still needs a second founding-team member.

    Team minimum: 2, including founder, confirmed by rendered current FAQ.

    ## Eligibility and legal form
    - **applicant:** Startup with working MVP. Thailand angle required; Thai and expat founders welcome. Overseas startups building for or expanding into Thailand accepted per AMA.
    - **incorporation:** Not required to join; Thai incorporation separately needed for depa grants.
    - **commitment:** At least one founder fully committed full-time.

    ## Application and product requirements
    - **MVP:** Working MVP required; revenue unnecessary; traction helpful.
    - **selection:** Interviews; strong Thailand case and programme fit. Actual application fields, deck/video requirements behind login.

    ## Attendance and location
    - **programme:** June 1–August 28, 2026; about 8–12 hours/week, mostly online.
    - **orientation:** May 28
    - **demo day:** August 27; onsite obligations/venue not conclusively checked.

    ## Award and cost
    - **type:** Mentoring, ecosystem/distribution support and investor access; no guaranteed 2026 funding.
    - **equity:** None to join; separate investment offers may have their own terms.
    - **cost:** Application free; THB15,000 if selected, possible case-specific fee support.
    - **historical only:** 2025 launch promised US$50,000 to top 5; explicitly NOT a 2026 award.
    - **LINE:** Potential ad/messaging credits and distribution, no guaranteed amounts.
