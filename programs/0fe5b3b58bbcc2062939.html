Note markdown# 2026 11th Mighty Net Innovation Program

Checked 12 October 2026. Public-source diligence; no application or account was submitted.

## Edition and scope

11th programme official February 6 2026 announcement verified; current intake availability and 2027 dates unknown.

Team minimum: Not specified.

Scope: include

## Eligibility

- target: Company with technology/product related to AI, smart manufacturing, smart healthcare or related commercial applications.

- prior_accelerator: Must not currently be in an uncompleted training-based accelerator: if previously participated, must have completed it.

- individual_nationality: Incorporation jurisdiction, sole-proprietor acceptance, founder nationality/residency and company-age limit not specified in reviewed page.

## Product and application requirements

- product: Clear commercialisation and industrial-adoption potential. Support tailored by product maturity; no published video/deck/MVP minimum or form schema.

## Attendance and location

- programme: Teams expected to follow schedule/activities and supply implementation updates/outcomes. Live factory testing and possible overseas exhibitions/PoCs.

- remote: Physical industrial sites are central; mandatory frequency, location of interviews and remote alternatives not specified.

## Award and commercial terms

- type: Technology validation, enterprise environments, hardware manufacturing guidance and market connections. No fixed cash award or equity investment established.

- conditional: International exhibitions/PoC opportunities depend on progress/performance and fit; not a funded-trip guarantee.

## Permissible spending and obligations

- founder_salary: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- rent: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- travel: Potential overseas participation only; who pays not stated.

- computers: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- API_subscriptions: Not established in the reviewed public sources; do not treat as approved or unrestricted.

- reporting: Implementation updates and outcome information required.

- IP_clawback: Not publicly established.
