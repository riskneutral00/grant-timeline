Note markdown# Hangzhou Municipal Government Scholarship for International Students

Separate Hangzhou city-funded foreign-student scholarship; province and university scholarships are alternative families, not duplicate host rounds.

## 1. Foreign recruitment and value

Official host page: “Applicants must be of foreign nationality”. Foreign academic/language-study support, limited direct founder value.

## 2. Eligibility and people

HZNU master's bachelor's degree and two professor references, Chinese says under 35; undergraduate high-school qualification and under 30. Foreign passport, health, Chinese competence, strong grades/conduct and no other scholarship. No gender-based restriction found in the inspected public rules. Exact nationality, admission/host and age requirements are edition-specific as described above; no owner eligibility inferred. Individual applicant/recipient route published.

## 3. Application fields and files

Public rules list language proof and graduate references; complete form/checklist not publicly read.

## 4. Deliverable

Full academic study or Chinese-language nondegree study; no startup deliverable. English age wording differs from Chinese boundary wording.

## 5. China attendance

Physical host study; nondegree English description requires over six months.

## 6. Benefits and fees

Standing host standards: master's RMB 30,000/year, undergraduate RMB 20,000/year, nondegree RMB 3,000/semester. Fee status: unknown.

## 7. Expenses and IP

Partial study support, not full guaranteed cost coverage. No equity term identified; research IP/refund/fees unverified.

## 8. Edition and dates

2026 standing host rules; exact intake unverified. Chinese page says May or October annually subject to city notice; English page still says 2022. No exact current cutoff inferred.
