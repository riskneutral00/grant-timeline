Note markdown# Changzhou Longcheng Foreign Talent Special Project

Changzhou employer-hosted foreign expert/entrepreneur project grant. Historical 2025 call requires at least 3:1 employer funding.

## 1. Foreign recruitment and value

City grant circulation explicitly says “引进人才须为外籍人才或团队”. Useful for a foreign software specialist with a Changzhou host and matching budget; weak for unhosted founders.

## 2. Eligibility and people

Foreign talent/team with own IP/core technology or specified senior professional/academic skills; at least seven Changzhou workdays/year. Local host application; individual direct applications not accepted in this university route. Certain national/provincial/city prior awards exclude. No gender-based restriction found in the inspected public rules. Exact nationality, admission/host and age requirements are edition-specific as described above; no owner eligibility inferred. Numerical whole-team minimum not established; employer count is not people count.

## 3. Application fields and files

Project information online, project task/design document, bilingual work/cooperation agreement, passport, CV, degree and exception justification where relevant. Linked Word template failed to read.

## 4. Deliverable

Innovation or civilian research/industrial/social-development project, ordinarily at most two years. Exact numerical participant cap and age/gender limits not found.

## 5. China attendance

At least seven workdays in Changzhou each year; host project duties.

## 6. Benefits and fees

2025 grant at most RMB 100,000/project; host self-funding to requested grant at least 3:1. Fee status: unknown.

## 7. Expenses and IP

2025 university instructions cap indirect costs at 30% of direct costs excluding equipment; management 5% of direct costs. These are host accounting instructions, not unrestricted personal income. Equity, recovery and full eligible-cost contract unresolved.

## 8. Edition and dates

2025 call closed; 2026 special-project terms unverified. October 10 at 14:00 is Changzhou University nomination deadline, not established city deadline. 2026 broad Longcheng upgrade does not prove this special grant's current terms.
