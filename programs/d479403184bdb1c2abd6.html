eligibility: Explicit foreign-talent support package. Entrepreneurship premises in the Ecological CBD; exact company ownership, classification and licensing rules in the full attachment remain unread. Housing/living benefits require Class A/B status. No verified personnel, age, gender or student minimum.

requirements: Local operation, employment and classification govern different benefits. Premises-related one-off support follows one year of operation. No universal MVP, video or pitch rule identified.

attendance: Local Jiangdong work/premises. Starter apartment 7–14 days and free workstation 3–12 months. Advice, investment introductions and market visits included; entry/work permission separate.

award: First two years: up to 50% rent/property subsidy. After one year: CNY 30,000–100,000 depending on leased area. Employment support CNY 2,000 per employee/year. expense rules: Purpose-limited rent, employment and living support; not a single unrestricted grant. Housing benefits follow highest-support/no-double-claim rule with provincial assistance. Exact caps, accounting, fees, equity and recovery terms unknown.
