Note markdown# PIDI Digdaya x Hackathon 2026

Checked: 2026-10-12T05:52:00Z

Edition: 2026 competition concluded per published September timeline; 2027 call not found.

Scope: include; minimum team: 1.

## Eligibility

- OJK programme FAQ expressly allows Indonesian citizens, 1–4 people, personal/company/university/institution/community entry; cross-institution teams allowed, one team per person. BI current FAQ says maximum 5, so max conflicts; minimum 1 supported.

- Student category: every member active diploma/S1/S2 student, domestic or overseas university, evidenced by student card or active-enrolment letter. Professional category includes founders/industry/lecturers/researchers/civil servants/freelancers/professionals.

- No company incorporation requirement published. No registration fee. Specific minimum age and resident requirement not published. Do not import foreign-member exceptions from separate 2025 OJK-Ekraf competition.

## Application field inventory

- Email and email OTP verification: Required at account-registration stage [Evidence](https://hackathon.pidi.id/register)

- Phone number: Required at account-registration stage [Evidence](https://hackathon.pidi.id/register)

- Personal and team data: Required after account registration; detailed profile fields not publicly accessible. [Evidence](https://infinityhackathon.id/program/BI-OJK-2026)

- Team name: Required; one or two words describing solution, compliant with norms/no third-party brand or political/SARA references. [Evidence](https://infinityhackathon.id/program/BI-OJK-2026)

- Idea proposal: Required using provided template; template questions not publicly accessible. [Evidence](https://infinityhackathon.id/program/BI-OJK-2026)

- Student card / active-enrolment proof: Conditional student category. [Evidence](https://infinityhackathon.id/program/BI-OJK-2026)

- Industry and PIDI certification: Semifinal/final condition: at least one provided industry certification and PIDI exam >=70%. [Evidence](https://infinityhackathon.id/program/BI-OJK-2026)

- Working prototype and pitch: Required at Capstone/final quarantine/judging; prototype not necessarily required for first-round idea proposal. [Evidence](https://infinityhackathon.id/program/BI-OJK-2026)

- Second-stage proposal: Required by June 4, 2026. [Evidence](https://www.pidi.id/)

- Video: Required during practitioner stage by July 26, 2026; exact length/format unknown. [Evidence](https://www.pidi.id/)

## Deliverables and requirements

- Digital solutions in finance resilience/innovation; productivity/food/jobs; public service/creative/digital export. AI/ML or blockchain-connected technologies permitted. Proposal can span problem statements; assessed for relevance.

- IP remains with participants subject to detailed intellectual-property clause VI (not recovered). Technical judging includes relevance, novelty, quality and effectiveness/impact.

- Booklet plans 22 weeks/180 learning hours, progressive essential/practitioner/capstone certification; produces functional prototype and pitch deck.

## Attendance

- Application: Online registration/proposal.

- Programme: Hybrid; booklet names LPPI Kemang for offline activity. Essential self-paced online; practitioner includes face-to-face plus online.

- Final: Capstone prototype, quarantine and judging/pitch. Revised BI schedule gives Sept 21–25 quarantine/final judging and Sept 26 FEKDI showcase/awards. No verified fully remote alternative or travel reimbursement.

## Award and payment

- Type: Cash prize pool plus training/certification, job fair and business matching; potential venture-capital collaboration is not guaranteed investment.

- Pool: IDR 1,400,000,000 total advertised; individual place amounts/payment schedule not exposed in reviewed primary sources.

- Equity: No equity transfer condition published; IP retained.

- Fees: OJK FAQ says no registration or other participant fees.
