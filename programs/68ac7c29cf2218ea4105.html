Note markdown# Henan Provincial Government International-Student Scholarship

Non-Chinese students and scholars in degree/non-degree study in Henan.

**Verified foreign route:** 非中国籍公民，身体健康，无犯罪记录 — https://www5.zzu.edu.cn/sie/info/1149/1225.htm

**Cycle:** October 2024 guidance: late January–late April host-specific applications; no exact 2026/27 cutoff verified.

**Eligibility:** Good health/conduct/academic performance; age, diploma and language gates depend on selected degree and host; generally Chinese with some English programmes.

**Application:** Chinese/English provincial scholarship application and preadmission notice in duplicate paper copies; ZZU online admission application and host admission supporting documents; current private fields unread.

**Deliverable:** Degree study/research and admission requirements; no startup MVP or pitch-video requirement verified.

**Attendance:** Study at a participating Henan host; provincial expert award review after host preadmission.

**Award:** 2024 guidance: doctoral CNY40k/year up to four years; master30k up to three; bachelor15k up to four (medicine six); associate10k up to three; language5k/semester or10k/year up to one year.

**Costs and conditions:** Funds for study expenses; host may optionally supplement shortfall, not guaranteed. Host fees must be checked; no startup spending, travel, tax or unrestricted cash claim. Documents not returned.

**People:** 1 minimum / 1 maximum. Individual student applicant, not startup-team eligibility.

**Unresolved:** Actual private portal field labels/upload caps, fee (unless explicitly stated), final award contract and current next-call dates not fully verified.

**Distinct family:** Distinct regional authority, fund and application contract; host institutions and scholarship levels count once. Not a national CSC/CAS/NSFC host variant.
