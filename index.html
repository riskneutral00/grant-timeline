How to read this directory

Upcoming lists everything due in the next 12 months, soonest first, plus rolling programmes. Where a programme has not announced its next round, its date is estimated from the last one (a stored forecast, or one year after the last deadline) and marked ★; estimates are for planning only. Past lists closed rounds, most recent first. No date yet lists programmes with no recorded or estimable date.

Support shows the headline amount only where a structured amount and currency are recorded; otherwise it names the kind of support. Conditions, tiers, currency notes and historical editions are in the full record. Team “Solo OK” means a verified minimum of one; “Not stated” does not establish solo eligibility.

Defence and military topics are excluded. Other requirements are conditions to assess, not proof of eligibility. Check registration, ownership, residency and exact cutoff times before applying.
