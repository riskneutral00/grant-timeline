How to read this directory

Tracked programmes is the default catalogue, with no time cutoff. Open / upcoming / rolling means recorded as open, upcoming or rolling. Confirm eligibility on the official page. Support and fee filters use explicit source-backed classifications; unclassified means unknown, including programmes whose recorded funding text mentions fees. Unknown does not mean free. Startup support credits are noncash benefits; free entry does not mean free service usage, and credits are not a spending cap. The default team filter hides only a recorded minimum of three or more; unknown sizes stay visible with uncertainty. Choose “Any size (show 3+)” to include them again. The database is unchanged. “Solo allowed” includes only a verified minimum of one.

Expired rounds remain visible with next-intake dates unannounced and prior dates preserved as history. Programme, intake and academic-year labels are retained. Historical planning estimates never determine chronology; dated calls use their recorded application opening, then deadline. Announced future programs remain visible regardless of how far ahead they are.

Defense and military topics are excluded. Other requirements are conditions to assess, not proof of applicant eligibility. Check registration, ownership, residency and exact cutoff times before applying. Record review dates can include historical research; the program description explains any uncertainty.
